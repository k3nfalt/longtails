\section*{Cube Fixed Points with Gaussian Noise}
We seek an approximate fixed point of a map $T:C\to C$ on the cube $C=[-R,R]^d\subset\mathbb{R}^d$ of radius $R>0$ and dimension $d\geq 1$. The map is nonexpansive in the supremum norm $\|\cdot\|_\infty$. At each query $x\in C$, the oracle returns $T(x)$ plus fresh centered Gaussian noise with independent coordinates of equal variance and bounded expected squared supremum norm. For a tolerance $\varepsilon>0$, the goal is a measurable output $\bar{x}\in C$ satisfying $\mathbb{E}\|T(\bar{x})-\bar{x}\|_\infty\leq\varepsilon$, with expectation over the algorithm and oracle randomness.

The fixed-point problem is to find
\[
x\in C\quad\text{such that}\quad T(x)=x.
\]
The algorithm receives $d$, $R$, the noise bound $\sigma\geq 0$, and $\varepsilon$. We consider the parameter range
\[
128\varepsilon\leq R,\qquad
\left\lceil R/\varepsilon\right\rceil\leq d.
\]

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Nonexpansiveness]
For all $x,y\in C$,
\[
\|T(x)-T(y)\|_\infty\leq\|x-y\|_\infty.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Additive Gaussian noise]
At call $k=0,1,\ldots$, a query $x_k\in C$ yields the reply $T(x_k)+Z_k\in\mathbb{R}^d$. For some fixed variance $v\geq 0$, the coordinates $(Z_{k,i})_{k\geq 0,\,1\leq i\leq d}$ are mutually independent with common law $\mathcal{N}(0,v)$. The noise sequence is independent of the algorithm's randomness. Each $Z_k$ is Bochner integrable, its squared supremum norm is integrable, and
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb{E}[Z_k]&=0,\\
\mathbb{E}\bigl[\|Z_k\|_\infty^2\bigr]&\leq\sigma^2.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

The algorithm uses measurable initialization, query, and output rules, and its update is jointly measurable in the current state and reply. After the allotted calls, its cube-valued output must have an integrable fixed-point residual satisfying
\[
\mathbb{E}\bigl[\|T(\bar{x})-\bar{x}\|_\infty\bigr]\leq\varepsilon.
\]
