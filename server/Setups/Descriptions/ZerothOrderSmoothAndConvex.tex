\section*{Stochastic Zeroth-Order Convex Minimization}
We consider minimizing a differentiable convex function $f:E\to\mathbb{R}$ on a $d$-dimensional real Hilbert space $E$, where $d\geq1$. Its gradient is $L$-Lipschitz, with $L>0$, and a given point $x^0\in E$ satisfies $\|x^0-x^\star\|\leq R$ for a global minimizer $x^\star$ and $R\geq0$. At each query $x$, the oracle returns an unbiased scalar estimate of $f(x)$ with variance at most $\sigma^2$, where $\sigma\geq0$, using an independent sample. For $\varepsilon>0$, the goal is a measurable output $\bar{x}\in E$ with integrable objective gap satisfying $\mathbb{E}[f(\bar{x})-f(x^\star)]\leq\varepsilon$, averaging over algorithm and oracle randomness.

The optimization problem is
\[
\min_{x\in E} f(x).
\]
The space $E$ is linearly isometric to $\mathbb{R}^d$ with its Euclidean norm; $\|\cdot\|$ denotes the induced norm on $E$. Let $\Omega$ be the measurable sample space. The algorithm receives $E$, $\Omega$, $d$, $L$, $R$, $\sigma$, $\varepsilon$, and $x^0$; the objective and the oracle's sampling distribution are accessed only through oracle replies.

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Convexity and smoothness]
The function $f$ is Fr\'echet differentiable everywhere on $E$. For every $x,y\in E$ and $t\in[0,1]$,
\[
\begin{aligned}
f((1-t)x+ty)&\leq (1-t)f(x)+t f(y),\\
\|\nabla f(x)-\nabla f(y)\|&\leq L\|x-y\|.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Initial distance]
There exists $x^\star\in E$ such that
\[
\begin{aligned}
f(x^\star)&\leq f(x) &&\text{for every }x\in E,\\
\|x^0-x^\star\|&\leq R.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Stochastic function values]
There are a probability measure $\nu$ on $\Omega$ and a jointly measurable map $g:E\times\Omega\to\mathbb{R}$. For every $x\in E$, both $g(x,\cdot)$ and $(g(x,\cdot)-f(x))^2$ are $\nu$-integrable, and
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb{E}_{\xi\sim\nu}[g(x,\xi)]&=f(x),\\
\mathbb{E}_{\xi\sim\nu}[(g(x,\xi)-f(x))^2]&\leq\sigma^2.
\end{aligned}
\]
At call $k\geq0$, a query $x\in E$ receives the scalar reply $g(x,\xi_k)$. Under their joint law $Q$ on $\Omega^{\mathbb{N}}$, the samples $(\xi_k)_{k\geq0}$ are independent and each has distribution $\nu$. Algorithm randomness is independent of this entire sample sequence.
\end{assumption}

Queries may be made at any point of $E$. After the allotted oracle calls, the output $\bar{x}$ must be measurable and satisfy
\[
\mathbb{E}_{P}\bigl[f(\bar{x})-f(x^\star)\bigr]\leq\varepsilon,
\]
with $f(\bar{x})-f(x^\star)$ integrable under $P$, where $P$ is the joint law of the algorithm's randomness and the oracle sample sequence.
