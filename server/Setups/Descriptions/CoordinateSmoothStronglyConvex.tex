\section*{Coordinate-Smooth Strongly Convex Minimization}
We consider minimizing a differentiable, $\mu$-strongly convex function $f:\mathbb{R}^d\to\mathbb{R}$, where $d\geq1$, with coordinate smoothness constants $L_i\geq\mu>0$ for $i=1,\ldots,d$. A given starting point $x^0$ satisfies $f(x^0)-f(x^\star)\leq\Delta$, where $\Delta\geq0$ and $x^\star$ is a global minimizer. At a query $(i,x)$, the oracle returns the scalar $\nabla_i f(x)$, the $i$th gradient coordinate. For $\varepsilon>0$, the goal is a measurable output $\bar{x}\in\mathbb{R}^d$ with integrable objective gap satisfying $\mathbb{E}_{P}[f(\bar{x})-f(x^\star)]\leq\varepsilon$, where $P$ is the law of the algorithm's randomness.

The optimization problem is
\[
\min_{x\in\mathbb{R}^d} f(x).
\]
We use the Euclidean inner product and norm, and write $e_i$ for the $i$th standard basis vector. The algorithm receives $d$, $(L_i)_{i=1}^d$, $\mu$, $\Delta$, $\varepsilon$, and $x^0$.

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Coordinate smoothness]
The function $f$ is differentiable everywhere. For every $x\in\mathbb{R}^d$, $i\in\{1,\ldots,d\}$, and $t\in\mathbb{R}$,
\[
f(x+t e_i)\leq f(x)+t\nabla_i f(x)+\frac{L_i}{2}t^2.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Strong convexity]
For every $x,y\in\mathbb{R}^d$,
\[
f(y)\geq f(x)+\langle\nabla f(x),y-x\rangle
+\frac{\mu}{2}\|y-x\|^2.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Initial objective gap]
There exists $x^\star\in\mathbb{R}^d$ such that
\[
\begin{aligned}
f(x^\star)&\leq f(x) &&\text{for every }x\in\mathbb{R}^d,\\
f(x^0)-f(x^\star)&\leq\Delta.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

Coordinate queries may be made at any point of $\mathbb{R}^d$. After the allotted oracle calls, the output $\bar{x}$ must be measurable, its objective gap must be integrable under $P$, and it must satisfy
\[
\mathbb{E}_{P}\bigl[f(\bar{x})-f(x^\star)\bigr]\leq\varepsilon.
\]
