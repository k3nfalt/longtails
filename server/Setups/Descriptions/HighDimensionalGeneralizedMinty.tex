\section*{High-Dimensional Minty Root Finding}
We seek a zero of a continuous operator $F:\mathbb{R}^d\to\mathbb{R}^d$ in high dimension $d$. On each segment, the Lipschitz constant of $F$ is at most $L_0+L_1 M$ whenever $M$ bounds its Euclidean norm there, with $L_0>0$ and $L_1\geq0$. A given starting point $x^0$ lies within distance $R\geq0$ of a zero $z$ satisfying $\langle F(x),x-z\rangle\geq0$ for all $x\in\mathbb{R}^d$. The oracle returns $F(x)$ at $x$. For tolerance $\varepsilon>0$, the goal is an output $\bar{x}\in\mathbb{R}^d$ with integrable residual norm and $\mathbb{E}\|F(\bar{x})\|\leq\varepsilon$, where the expectation is over the algorithm's randomness.

The residual minimization problem is
\[
\min_{x\in\mathbb{R}^d}\|F(x)\|.
\]
The norm and inner product are Euclidean. The algorithm receives $d\in\mathbb{N}$, $L_0$, $L_1$, $R$, $\varepsilon$, and $x^0\in\mathbb{R}^d$, and may query any point of $\mathbb{R}^d$.

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[High-dimensional regime]
With $T=R(L_0/\varepsilon+L_1)$, the parameters satisfy
\[
\begin{aligned}
T &\geq 1024,\\
L_1 R &\geq 32\log(1+L_1\varepsilon/L_0),\\
d &\geq 8192\left(1+\left\lfloor (T/1024)^2\right\rfloor\right)^3.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Generalized Lipschitz continuity]
The operator $F$ is continuous. For every $x,y\in\mathbb{R}^d$, let
\[
M_{x,y}=\max_{0\leq t\leq 1}\|F(x+t(y-x))\|.
\]
Then
\[
\|F(x)-F(y)\|\leq (L_0+L_1 M_{x,y})\|x-y\|.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Minty zero]
There exists $z\in\mathbb{R}^d$ such that $F(z)=0$, $\|x^0-z\|\leq R$, and
\[
\langle F(x),x-z\rangle\geq 0\qquad\text{for every }x\in\mathbb{R}^d.
\]
\end{assumption}

After the allotted oracle calls, the residual norm $\|F(\bar{x})\|$ must be integrable and satisfy
\[
\mathbb{E}\bigl[\|F(\bar{x})\|\bigr]\leq\varepsilon.
\]
