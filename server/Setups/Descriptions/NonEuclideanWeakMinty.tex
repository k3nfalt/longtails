\section*{Non-Euclidean Weak Minty Problems}
We seek a zero of an operator $F:E\to E^*$ on a finite-dimensional real normed space $E$, where $E^*$ is its continuous dual with induced operator norm $\|\cdot\|_{\mathrm{op}}$. The operator is $L$-Lipschitz, with $L>0$, and satisfies a weak Minty condition with parameter $0\leq\rho\leq 1/(20L)$ at a zero $x^\star$. A differentiable, $\mu$-strongly convex function $h:E\to\mathbb{R}$, with $\mu>0$, bounds the Bregman distance from a given $x^0\in E$ to $x^\star$ by $D\geq0$. The oracle returns $F(x)$ at $x$. For $\varepsilon>0$, the goal is an output $\bar{x}\in E$ satisfying $\|F(\bar{x})\|_{\mathrm{op}}\leq\varepsilon$ almost surely under $P$, the law of the algorithm's randomness.

The problem is to find $x\in E$ such that
\[
F(x)=0.
\]
Write $\langle v,u\rangle=v(u)$ for $v\in E^*$ and $u\in E$. The norm on $E$ is denoted by $\|\cdot\|$, and define the Bregman divergence by
\[
B_h(u,x)=h(u)-h(x)-\langle h'(x),u-x\rangle,
\]
where $h'(x)\in E^*$ is the Fr\'echet derivative. The algorithm receives $E$, $L$, $\rho$, $\mu$, $h$, and $x^0$; the bound $D$ and tolerance $\varepsilon$ may enter only the oracle-call budget.

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Bregman geometry]
The function $h$ is Fr\'echet differentiable everywhere on $E$, and
\[
B_h(u,x)\geq\frac{\mu}{2}\|u-x\|^2,
\qquad x,u\in E.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Lipschitz continuity]
The operator satisfies
\[
\|F(x)-F(y)\|_{\mathrm{op}}\leq L\|x-y\|,
\qquad x,y\in E.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Weak Minty condition and initial bound]
There exists $x^\star\in E$ such that
\[
\begin{aligned}
F(x^\star)&=0,\\
B_h(x^\star,x^0)&\leq D,\\
\langle F(x),x-x^\star\rangle
&\geq-\rho\|F(x)\|_{\mathrm{op}}^2,
\qquad x\in E.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

Queries may be made at any point of $E$. After the allotted oracle calls, the output must satisfy the residual criterion
\[
\|F(\bar{x})\|_{\mathrm{op}}\leq\varepsilon
\quad P\text{-a.s.}
\]
