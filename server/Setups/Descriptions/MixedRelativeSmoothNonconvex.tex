\section*{Nonconvex Optimization with Mixed Relative Noise}
We consider minimizing a differentiable, possibly nonconvex function $f:E\to\mathbb{R}$ on a separable real Hilbert space $E$. Its gradient is $L$-Lipschitz, where $L>0$, and a given point $x^0\in E$ satisfies $f(x^0)-\inf_{x\in E}f(x)\leq\Delta$ for $\Delta\geq0$. The oracle returns unbiased stochastic gradients, with second moments controlled by gradient magnitudes and noise increments controlled jointly by gradient magnitudes and query distance. Consecutive pairs of calls share a sample; samples across pairs are independent. For $\varepsilon>0$, the goal is a measurable output $\bar{x}\in E$ with $\mathbb{E}\|\nabla f(\bar{x})\|\leq\varepsilon$, averaging over sampling and algorithm randomness.

The optimization problem is
\[
\inf_{x\in E} f(x).
\]
The norm $\|\cdot\|$ is induced by the inner product on $E$. Let $\Omega$ be a measurable sample space, and let $M,r,\sigma\geq0$. The algorithm receives $E$, $\Omega$, $L$, $M$, $r$, $\sigma$, $\Delta$, $\varepsilon$, and $x^0$.

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Smoothness]
The function $f$ is Fr\'echet differentiable everywhere on $E$, and
\[
\|\nabla f(x)-\nabla f(y)\|\leq L\|x-y\|,
\qquad x,y\in E.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Initial objective gap]
The given point $x^0$ satisfies
\[
f(x^0)-f(x)\leq\Delta,
\qquad x\in E.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Mixed relative noise]
There exist a probability measure $\nu$ on $\Omega$ and a jointly measurable map $g:E\times\Omega\to E$. Define the centered error by
\[
e(x,\omega)=g(x,\omega)-\nabla f(x).
\]
For every $x\in E$, the random variable $\|e(x,\xi)\|^2$ is integrable for $\xi\sim\nu$, and
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb{E}_{\xi\sim\nu}[g(x,\xi)]&=\nabla f(x),\\
\mathbb{E}_{\xi\sim\nu}\|e(x,\xi)\|^2
&\leq r\|\nabla f(x)\|^2+\sigma^2.
\end{aligned}
\]
Moreover, for every $x,y\in E$, the same sample is used in the noise-increment bound
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb{E}_{\xi\sim\nu}\|e(x,\xi)-e(y,\xi)\|^2
&\leq r\bigl(\|\nabla f(x)\|^2+\|\nabla f(y)\|^2\bigr)\\
&\quad+M^2\|x-y\|^2.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

Queries may be made at any point of $E$. Let $(\xi_j)_{j\geq0}$ be measurable, independent random variables with common law $\nu$, independent of the algorithm's randomness. At call $k\geq0$, a query $x_k\in E$ receives
\[
g\bigl(x_k,\xi_{\lfloor k/2\rfloor}\bigr).
\]
Thus calls $2j$ and $2j+1$ use the same sample $\xi_j$, including when the second query depends on the first reply. Each evaluation counts as one oracle call.

After the allotted oracle calls, the output $\bar{x}$ must be measurable, its gradient norm must be integrable, and
\[
\mathbb{E}_{P}\bigl[\|\nabla f(\bar{x})\|\bigr]\leq\varepsilon,
\]
where $P$ is the joint law of the sample sequence and the independent algorithm randomness.
