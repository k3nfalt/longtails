\section*{Gradient-Only Third-Order Nonconvex Optimization}
We consider minimizing a possibly nonconvex function $f:E\to\mathbb{R}$ on a finite-dimensional real inner product space $E$. The function is three times continuously differentiable, with $L_1$-Lipschitz gradient and $L_3$-Lipschitz third derivative in the induced operator norm, where $L_1,L_3>0$. A given point $x^0\in E$ satisfies $f(x^0)-\inf_{x\in E}f(x)\leq\Delta$, where $\Delta>0$. The oracle returns $\nabla f(x)$ at $x$. For $\varepsilon>0$, the goal is an output $\bar{x}\in E$ satisfying $\|\nabla f(\bar{x})\|\leq\varepsilon$ almost surely under $P$, the law of the algorithm's randomness.

The optimization problem is
\[
\inf_{x\in E} f(x).
\]
The norm $\|\cdot\|$ is induced by the inner product on $E$, and $\|\cdot\|_{\mathrm{op}}$ denotes the induced operator norm, including for multilinear forms. In particular, $\nabla^3 f(x)$ is a trilinear form on $E$. The algorithm receives $E$, $x^0$, $L_1$, $L_3$, $\Delta$, and $\varepsilon$.

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Smoothness]
The function $f$ belongs to $C^3(E;\mathbb{R})$, and its gradient satisfies
\[
\|\nabla f(x)-\nabla f(y)\|\leq L_1\|x-y\|,
\qquad x,y\in E.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Third-derivative regularity]
The third derivative satisfies
\[
\|\nabla^3 f(x)-\nabla^3 f(y)\|_{\mathrm{op}}
\leq L_3\|x-y\|,
\qquad x,y\in E.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Initial objective gap]
The given starting point satisfies
\[
f(x^0)-f(x)\leq\Delta,
\qquad x\in E.
\]
\end{assumption}

Queries may be made at any point of $E$. After the allotted gradient calls, the output must satisfy the first-order stationarity criterion
\[
\|\nabla f(\bar{x})\|\leq\varepsilon
\quad P\text{-a.s.}
\]
