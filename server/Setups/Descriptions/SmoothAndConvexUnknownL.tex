\section*{Convex Minimization with Unknown Smoothness}
We consider minimizing a differentiable convex function $f:E\to\mathbb{R}$ on a real Hilbert space $E$. Its gradient is $L$-Lipschitz for an unknown constant $L>0$, and a given starting point $x^0\in E$ lies within distance $R\geq 0$ of a global minimizer $x^\star$. The oracle returns $\nabla f(x)$ at $x$. For a tolerance $\varepsilon>0$, the goal is an output $\bar{x}\in E$ satisfying $f(\bar{x})-f(x^\star)\leq\varepsilon$ almost surely under $P$, the law of the algorithm's randomness. Only $E$ and $x^0$ are algorithm inputs.

The optimization problem is
\[
\min_{x\in E} f(x).
\]
The norm $\|\cdot\|$ is induced by the inner product on $E$. The parameters $L$, $R$, and $\varepsilon$ may enter the oracle-call budget but are not supplied to the algorithm.

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Convexity]
For every $x,y\in E$ and $t\in[0,1]$,
\[
f((1-t)x+ty)\leq (1-t)f(x)+t f(y).
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Smoothness]
The function $f$ is Fr\'echet differentiable everywhere on $E$, and its gradient satisfies
\[
\|\nabla f(x)-\nabla f(y)\|\leq L\|x-y\|,
\qquad x,y\in E.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Initial distance]
There exists $x^\star\in E$ such that
\[
\begin{aligned}
f(x^\star)&\leq f(x) &&\text{for every }x\in E,\\
\|x^0-x^\star\|&\leq R.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

Queries may be made at any point of $E$. After the allotted oracle calls, the output must satisfy
\[
f(\bar{x})-f(x^\star)\leq\varepsilon
\quad P\text{-a.s.}
\]
