\section*{Finite-Sum Minimization on an $\ell_1$ Ball}
We consider minimizing $F(x)=n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^n f_i(x)$ over $C=\{x\in\mathbb{R}^d:\|x\|_1\leq R\}$, where $d,n$ are positive integers and $R>0$. Each component $f_i:\mathbb{R}^d\to\mathbb{R}$ is convex and differentiable, with an $L$-Lipschitz gradient in the Euclidean norm, where $L>0$. The oracle returns $\nabla f_i(x)$ at a queried component $i$ and feasible point $x\in C$. For $\varepsilon>0$, the goal is a measurable output $\bar{x}\in C$ with integrable objective gap satisfying $\mathbb{E}_{P}[F(\bar{x})-F(x^\star)]\leq\varepsilon$, where $x^\star$ minimizes $F$ on $C$ and $P$ is the law of the algorithm's randomness.

The constraint uses $\|x\|_1=\sum_{j=1}^d|x_j|$, while $\|\cdot\|_2$ denotes the Euclidean norm. The optimization problem is
\[
\min_{x\in C} F(x).
\]
The algorithm receives $d,n,L,R$, and $\varepsilon$.

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Component convexity]
For every $i\in\{1,\ldots,n\}$, every $x,y\in\mathbb{R}^d$, and every $t\in[0,1]$,
\[
f_i((1-t)x+ty)\leq (1-t)f_i(x)+t f_i(y).
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Component smoothness]
Each $f_i$ is differentiable everywhere on $\mathbb{R}^d$, and
\[
\|\nabla f_i(x)-\nabla f_i(y)\|_2
\leq L\|x-y\|_2,
\qquad x,y\in\mathbb{R}^d.
\]
\end{assumption}

Oracle queries consist of a chosen index $i\in\{1,\ldots,n\}$ and a point $x\in C$. The smoothness and convexity conditions hold on the entire ambient space, although queries are restricted to $C$.

Continuity of $F$ and compactness of $C$ ensure that a constrained minimizer exists. Fix $x^\star\in C$ satisfying
\[
F(x^\star)\leq F(x)\qquad\text{for every }x\in C.
\]
After the allotted oracle calls, the output must be a measurable $C$-valued random variable. Its objective gap must satisfy
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb{E}_{P}\bigl[|F(\bar{x})-F(x^\star)|\bigr]&<\infty,\\
\mathbb{E}_{P}\bigl[F(\bar{x})-F(x^\star)\bigr]&\leq\varepsilon.
\end{aligned}
\]
