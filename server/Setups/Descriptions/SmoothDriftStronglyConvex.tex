\section*{Tracking Smooth Strongly Convex Objectives}
We consider tracking the minimizers $x_k^\star$ of differentiable functions $f_k:E\to\mathbb{R}$, indexed by $k\geq0$, on a separable real Hilbert space $E$. Each objective is $\mu$-strongly convex with $L$-Lipschitz gradient, where $0<\mu\leq L$. The minimizer path has bounded initial distance from a given point $x^0\in E$, bounded initial velocity, and uniformly bounded second differences. At call $k$, the oracle returns an unbiased estimate of $\nabla f_k(x)$ with variance at most $\sigma^2$, where $\sigma\geq0$, using a fresh independent sample. For $\varepsilon>0$, the goal is an output $(\bar{x},N)$ satisfying $\mathbb{E}\|\bar{x}-x_{\max\{N-1,0\}}^\star\|^2\leq\varepsilon$, where $N$ counts completed calls and expectation covers oracle and algorithm randomness.

The time-indexed optimization problems are
\[
\min_{x\in E} f_k(x),\qquad k\in\mathbb{N}_0.
\]
The norm $\|\cdot\|$ is induced by the inner product on $E$. The algorithm receives $E$, the measurable sample space $\Omega$, $\mu$, $L$, $\sigma$, $\varepsilon$, and $x^0$; the parameters $\Gamma,R,V\geq0$ and $H\in\mathbb{N}_0$ may enter only the oracle-call budget. The parameter $H$ imposes no additional condition on the objectives or the minimizer path.

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Strong convexity and smoothness]
Each $f_k$ is Fr\'echet differentiable everywhere. For every $k\in\mathbb{N}_0$ and $x,y\in E$,
\[
\begin{aligned}
f_k(y)&\geq f_k(x)+\langle\nabla f_k(x),y-x\rangle
             +\frac{\mu}{2}\|y-x\|^2,\\
\|\nabla f_k(x)-\nabla f_k(y)\|&\leq L\|x-y\|.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Minimizer path and drift]
There exists a sequence $(x_k^\star)_{k\geq0}$ of global minimizers satisfying
\[
\begin{aligned}
f_k(x_k^\star)&\leq f_k(x) &&(k\geq0,\ x\in E),\\
\|x^0-x_0^\star\|&\leq R,\\
\|x_1^\star-x_0^\star\|&\leq V,\\
\|(x_{k+2}^\star-x_{k+1}^\star)
 -(x_{k+1}^\star-x_k^\star)\|&\leq\Gamma &&(k\geq0).
\end{aligned}
\]
The drift bound and tolerance satisfy
\[
9792\,\Gamma^2
\left(\frac{2L}{\mu}
      +\frac{99\sigma^2}{\mu^2\varepsilon}\right)^4
\leq\varepsilon.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Stochastic gradient oracle]
There is a probability measure $\nu$ on $\Omega$ and, for every $k\in\mathbb{N}_0$, a jointly measurable map $g_k:E\times\Omega\to E$. For every fixed $x\in E$, $g_k(x,\cdot)$ is Bochner integrable and $\|g_k(x,\cdot)-\nabla f_k(x)\|^2$ is integrable under $\nu$, with
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb{E}_{\xi\sim\nu}[g_k(x,\xi)]&=\nabla f_k(x),\\
\mathbb{E}_{\xi\sim\nu}
 \bigl[\|g_k(x,\xi)-\nabla f_k(x)\|^2\bigr]&\leq\sigma^2.
\end{aligned}
\]
At call $k$, a query $x\in E$ receives $g_k(x,\xi_k)$, where the samples $(\xi_k)_{k\geq0}$ are independent with common law $\nu$ and are independent of the algorithm's randomness.
\end{assumption}

The output includes the number of completed oracle calls. Specifically, its counter starts at $c_0=0$, increases according to $c_{k+1}=c_k+1$ after each reply, and equals the reported value $N$ at termination. Thus the objective advances with each oracle call, and the reported time cannot be chosen independently of the number of calls.

Let $P$ be the joint law of the algorithm's randomness and the independent oracle samples. After the allotted calls, the output $(\bar{x},N)\in E\times\mathbb{N}_0$ must have integrable squared tracking error under $P$ and satisfy
\[
\mathbb{E}_{P}\bigl[
\|\bar{x}-x_{\max\{N-1,0\}}^\star\|^2
\bigr]\leq\varepsilon.
\]
For $N\geq1$, the reference minimizer belongs to the objective queried on the final call; for $N=0$, it is $x_0^\star$.
