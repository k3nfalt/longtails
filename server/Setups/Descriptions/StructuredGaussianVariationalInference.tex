\section*{Structured Gaussian Variational Inference}
We optimize Gaussian location $m\in\mathbb{R}^d$ and lower-triangular scale $C\in\mathbb{R}^{d\times d}$ with positive diagonal and prescribed row supports of size at most $w$. For a differentiable potential $V:\mathbb{R}^d\to\mathbb{R}$, define $F(m,C)=\mathbb{E}[V(m+C\xi)]-\sum_{i=1}^d\log C_{ii}$, where $\xi\sim\mathcal{N}(0,I_d)$ is standard Gaussian. The gradient of $V$ is $L$-Lipschitz and $\mu$-strongly monotone, with $0<\mu\leq L$. A given feasible pair $(m^0,C^0)$ lies within Euclidean--Frobenius distance $R\geq0$ of a minimizer. The oracle returns $\nabla V(x)$ at $x$. For $\varepsilon>0$, the goal is a feasible output $(\bar m,\bar C)$ with $\mathbb{E}_{P}[F(\bar m,\bar C)-\min F]\leq\varepsilon$, where the minimum is over feasible pairs and $P$ is the law of algorithm randomness.

Let $d,w\in\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$, and equip $\mathbb{R}^d$ with its Euclidean inner product and norm. For each $i\in\{1,\ldots,d\}$, the prescribed support $S_i$ satisfies
\[
i\in S_i\subseteq\{1,\ldots,i\},
\qquad |S_i|\leq w.
\]
Define the structured scale family by
\[
\mathcal{C}_S=\left\{C\in\mathbb{R}^{d\times d}:
C_{ii}>0\ \text{for every }i,\quad
C_{ij}=0\ \text{whenever }j\notin S_i\right\}.
\]
The optimization problem is
\[
\min_{m\in\mathbb{R}^d,\ C\in\mathcal{C}_S} F(m,C).
\]
The expectation defining $F$ uses independent standard normal coordinates of $\xi$. The algorithm receives $d,w,(S_i)_{i=1}^d,L,\mu,R,\varepsilon,m^0,C^0$, where $m^0\in\mathbb{R}^d$ and $C^0\in\mathcal{C}_S$.

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Smoothness and strong monotonicity]
The potential $V$ is differentiable everywhere. For every $x,y\in\mathbb{R}^d$,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\|\nabla V(x)-\nabla V(y)\|&\leq L\|x-y\|,\\
\langle\nabla V(x)-\nabla V(y),x-y\rangle
&\geq\mu\|x-y\|^2.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Attainment and initial distance]
There exists $(m^\star,C^\star)\in\mathbb{R}^d\times\mathcal{C}_S$ such that
\[
\begin{aligned}
F(m^\star,C^\star)&\leq F(m,C)
&&\text{for every }(m,C)\in\mathbb{R}^d\times\mathcal{C}_S,\\
\|m^0-m^\star\|^2+\|C^0-C^\star\|_{\mathrm F}^2&\leq R^2,
\end{aligned}
\]
where $\|C\|_{\mathrm F}^2=\sum_{i=1}^d\sum_{j=1}^d C_{ij}^2$ is the squared Frobenius norm.
\end{assumption}

Queries may be made at any $x\in\mathbb{R}^d$. After the allotted oracle calls, the output $(\bar m,\bar C)$ must be measurable and satisfy $\bar C\in\mathcal{C}_S$ for every realization. For every minimizing pair $(m^\star,C^\star)\in\mathbb{R}^d\times\mathcal{C}_S$, its objective gap must be integrable under $P$ and satisfy
\[
\mathbb{E}_{P}\!\left[F(\bar m,\bar C)-F(m^\star,C^\star)\right]
\leq\varepsilon.
\]
