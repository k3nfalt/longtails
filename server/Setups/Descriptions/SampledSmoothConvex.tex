\section*{Linearly Decaying SGD for Sampled Losses}
We minimize $f(x)=\mathbb{E}_{\xi\sim\nu}[\ell(\xi,x)]$ on a separable real Hilbert space $E$, where $\nu$ is a probability law on a measurable sample space $\Xi$ and each sample loss $\ell(\xi,\cdot)$ is convex with $L$-Lipschitz gradient, $L>0$. A given starting point $x^0$ lies within distance $R\geq0$ of a minimizer $x^\star$, where $\mathbb{E}_{\xi\sim\nu}\|\nabla_x\ell(\xi,x^\star)\|^2\leq\sigma^2$ for $\sigma\geq0$. The oracle returns sample gradients using independent samples from $\nu$. We analyze linearly decaying stochastic gradient descent (SGD), seeking a measurable last iterate $\bar{x}$ with integrable objective gap satisfying $\mathbb{E}[f(\bar{x})-f(x^\star)]\leq\varepsilon$, where $\varepsilon>0$ and expectation is over the samples.

The optimization problem is
\[
\min_{x\in E} f(x).
\]
The norm $\|\cdot\|$ is induced by the inner product on $E$. The algorithm receives $E$, $\Xi$, $L$, $R$, $\sigma$, $\varepsilon$, and $x^0$; the distribution $\nu$ and sample losses are accessed through the oracle.

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Sample convexity and smoothness]
For every $\xi\in\Xi$, the function $\ell(\xi,\cdot):E\to\mathbb{R}$ is Fr\'echet differentiable. Write $g(\xi,x)=\nabla_x\ell(\xi,x)$. For every $x,y\in E$ and $t\in[0,1]$,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\ell(\xi,(1-t)x+ty)&\leq(1-t)\ell(\xi,x)+t\ell(\xi,y),\\
\|g(\xi,x)-g(\xi,y)\|&\leq L\|x-y\|.
\end{aligned}
\]
The map $g:\Xi\times E\to E$ is jointly measurable, and $\ell(\cdot,x)$ is integrable under $\nu$ for every $x\in E$.
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Initial distance and noise at a minimizer]
There exists $x^\star\in E$ such that
\[
\begin{aligned}
f(x^\star)&\leq f(x) &&\text{for every }x\in E,\\
\|x^0-x^\star\|&\leq R.
\end{aligned}
\]
The random variable $\|g(\xi,x^\star)\|^2$ is integrable under $\nu$ and satisfies
\[
\mathbb{E}_{\xi\sim\nu}\bigl[\|g(\xi,x^\star)\|^2\bigr]\leq\sigma^2.
\]
\end{assumption}

Queries may be made at any point of $E$. At call $k$, the oracle returns $g(\xi_k,x)$ for the queried point $x$, where $(\xi_k)_{k\geq0}$ are measurable, mutually independent random variables with common law $\nu$. Let $P$ denote their joint law.

Our result is algorithm-specific: we analyze linearly decaying SGD. The method chooses a deterministic scale $\eta\geq0$ and an integer $T\geq0$ from the available inputs. Starting from the given $x^0$, it performs
\[
\begin{aligned}
a_k&=\begin{cases}
\eta(T-k)/T,&T>0,\\
0,&T=0,
\end{cases}\\
x^{k+1}&=x^k-a_k g(\xi_k,x^k),\qquad k=0,\ldots,N-1,
\end{aligned}
\]
where $N\geq0$ is the allotted number of oracle calls. The tuning parameter $T$ need not equal $N$. Each update uses one oracle reply, and the output is the last iterate $\bar{x}=x^N$, with $\bar{x}=x^0$ when $N=0$.

The output must be measurable under $P$, its objective gap must be integrable, and it must satisfy
\[
\mathbb{E}_{P}\bigl[f(\bar{x})-f(x^\star)\bigr]\leq\varepsilon.
\]
