\section*{Monotone Equations with Inexact Jacobians}
We consider finding a zero of a differentiable monotone map $F:E\to E$ on a finite-dimensional real inner product space $E$. Its Jacobian is $M$-Lipschitz in operator norm, with $M>0$, and a given point $x^0\in E$ lies within distance $R>0$ of a zero. At a query $x\in E$, the oracle returns $F(x)$ and a linear approximation to its Jacobian with operator-norm error at most $\delta\geq0$. For $\varepsilon>0$, the goal is an output $\bar{x}\in E$ satisfying $\|F(\bar{x})\|\leq\varepsilon$ almost surely under $P$, the joint law of algorithm and oracle randomness.

The equation to solve is
\[
F(x)=0,\qquad x\in E.
\]
Write $J_F(x):E\to E$ for the Fr\'echet derivative of $F$ at $x$. The norm $\|\cdot\|$ is induced by the inner product on $E$, and $\|\cdot\|_{\mathrm{op}}$ denotes the induced operator norm. The algorithm receives $E$, $x^0$, $M$, $\delta$, $R$, and $\varepsilon$.

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Monotonicity and Jacobian regularity]
The map $F$ is Fr\'echet differentiable everywhere, and for every $x,y\in E$,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\langle F(x)-F(y),x-y\rangle&\geq0,\\
\|J_F(x)-J_F(y)\|_{\mathrm{op}}&\leq M\|x-y\|.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Nearby zero]
There exists $x^\star\in E$ such that
\[
F(x^\star)=0,\qquad \|x^0-x^\star\|\leq R.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Inexact Jacobian oracle]
Queries may be made at any $x\in E$. Each oracle call returns a pair $(v,A)$, where $v\in E$ and $A:E\to E$ is linear, satisfying
\[
v=F(x),\qquad \|A-J_F(x)\|_{\mathrm{op}}\leq\delta.
\]
These conditions hold for every query history and every realization of the oracle randomness. The approximate Jacobian may depend on both. Algorithm randomness is independent of oracle randomness.
\end{assumption}

After the allotted oracle calls, the output must satisfy the residual criterion
\[
\|F(\bar{x})\|\leq\varepsilon
\quad P\text{-a.s.}
\]
