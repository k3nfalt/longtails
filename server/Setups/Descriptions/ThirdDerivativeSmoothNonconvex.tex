\section*{Nonconvex Optimization with Lipschitz Third Derivatives}
We consider minimizing a possibly nonconvex function $f:E\to\mathbb{R}$ on a finite-dimensional real inner product space $E$. The function is three times continuously differentiable with $L_3$-Lipschitz third derivative, where $L_3>0$. A given point $x^0\in E$ satisfies $f(x^0)-\inf_{x\in E}f(x)\leq\Delta$ and $\|\nabla^2 f(x^0)\|_{\mathrm{op}}\leq H_0$, where $\Delta>0$ and $H_0\geq0$; derivative norms are induced operator norms. Two exact oracles return $(f(x),\nabla f(x))$ and $\nabla^2 f(x)$, respectively. For $\varepsilon>0$, the goal is an output $\bar{x}\in E$ satisfying $\|\nabla f(\bar{x})\|\leq\varepsilon$ almost surely under $P$, the law of the algorithm's randomness.

The optimization problem is
\[
\inf_{x\in E} f(x).
\]
The norm $\|\cdot\|$ is induced by the inner product on $E$. The Hessian $\nabla^2 f(x)$ is a linear operator from $E$ to $E$, and $\nabla^3 f(x)$ is the third derivative viewed as a trilinear form on $E$. The algorithm receives $E$, $x^0$, $L_3$, $\Delta$, $H_0$, and $\varepsilon$.

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Third-derivative regularity]
The function $f$ belongs to $C^3(E;\mathbb{R})$, and
\[
\|\nabla^3 f(x)-\nabla^3 f(y)\|_{\mathrm{op}}
\leq L_3\|x-y\|,
\qquad x,y\in E.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Initial gap and curvature]
The given point $x^0$ satisfies
\[
\begin{aligned}
f(x^0)-f(x)&\leq\Delta &&\text{for every }x\in E,\\
\|\nabla^2 f(x^0)\|_{\mathrm{op}}&\leq H_0.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

Both oracles accept queries at any point of $E$, and their calls are counted separately. After the allotted calls, the output must satisfy
\[
\|\nabla f(\bar{x})\|\leq\varepsilon
\quad P\text{-a.s.}
\]
