\section*{Quasar-Convex Minimization with Lipschitz Hessian}
We consider minimizing a twice continuously differentiable function $f:E\to\mathbb{R}$ on a finite-dimensional real inner product space $E$. For $\gamma\in(0,1]$, the function is $\gamma$-quasar-convex relative to a global minimizer $x^\star$, with $\|x^0-x^\star\|\leq R$ for a given starting point $x^0\in E$ and radius $R>0$. Its Hessian is $H$-Lipschitz, where $H>0$, and has operator norm at most $L_0\geq0$ at $x^0$. The oracle returns $f(x)$, $\nabla f(x)$, and $\nabla^2 f(x)$ at $x$. For $\varepsilon>0$, the goal is an output $\bar{x}\in E$ satisfying $f(\bar{x})-f(x^\star)\leq\varepsilon$ almost surely under $P$, the law of the algorithm's randomness.

The optimization problem is
\[
\min_{x\in E} f(x).
\]
The norm $\|\cdot\|$ is induced by the inner product on $E$, and $\|\cdot\|_{\mathrm{op}}$ denotes the induced operator norm. The Hessian $\nabla^2 f(x):E\to E$ is the derivative of the gradient at $x$. The algorithm receives $E$, $x^0$, $H$, $\gamma$, $R$, $L_0$, and $\varepsilon$.

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Hessian regularity]
The function $f$ is twice continuously Fr\'echet differentiable on $E$, and
\[
\begin{aligned}
\|\nabla^2 f(x)-\nabla^2 f(y)\|_{\mathrm{op}}
&\leq H\|x-y\| &&\text{for all }x,y\in E,\\
\|\nabla^2 f(x^0)\|_{\mathrm{op}}&\leq L_0.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Quasar convexity and initial distance]
There exists $x^\star\in E$ such that, for every $x\in E$,
\[
\begin{aligned}
f(x^\star)&\leq f(x),\\
\gamma\bigl(f(x)-f(x^\star)\bigr)
&\leq \langle\nabla f(x),x-x^\star\rangle,
\end{aligned}
\]
and $\|x^0-x^\star\|\leq R$.
\end{assumption}

Queries may be made at any point of $E$, and each oracle reply is the exact triple
\[
\bigl(f(x),\nabla f(x),\nabla^2 f(x)\bigr).
\]
After the allotted oracle calls, the output must satisfy
\[
f(\bar{x})-f(x^\star)\leq\varepsilon
\quad P\text{-a.s.}
\]
Here $x^\star$ is a fixed global minimizer; all global minimizers have the same objective value.
