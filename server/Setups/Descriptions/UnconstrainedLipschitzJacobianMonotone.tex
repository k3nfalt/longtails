\section*{Monotone Equations with Lipschitz Jacobians}
We consider finding a zero of a differentiable monotone map $F:E\to E$ on a finite-dimensional real inner product space $E$. Its Jacobian $F'$ is $L$-Lipschitz in operator norm, where $L\geq 0$, and a given point $x^0\in E$ lies within distance $D>0$ of a zero $x^\star$. Two exact oracles return $F(x)$ and $F'(x)$, respectively, at $x\in E$. For a tolerance $\varepsilon>0$, the goal is an output $\bar{x}\in E$ satisfying $\|F(\bar{x})\|\leq\varepsilon$ almost surely under $P$, the law of the algorithm's randomness.

The equation to be solved is
\[
F(x)=0,\qquad x\in E.
\]
Write $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle$ for the inner product and $\|\cdot\|$ for its induced norm. The Jacobian $F'(x):E\to E$ is the Fr\'echet derivative, and $\|\cdot\|_{\mathrm{op}}$ denotes the induced operator norm. The algorithm receives $E$, $x^0$, $L$, $D$, $\varepsilon$, and an integer parameter $m\geq 1$.

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Monotonicity]
For every $x,y\in E$,
\[
\langle F(x)-F(y),x-y\rangle\geq 0.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Lipschitz Jacobian]
The map $F$ is Fr\'echet differentiable everywhere on $E$, and
\[
\|F'(x)-F'(y)\|_{\mathrm{op}}\leq L\|x-y\|,
\qquad x,y\in E.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Initial distance to a zero]
There exists $x^\star\in E$ such that
\[
\begin{aligned}
F(x^\star)&=0,\\
\|x^0-x^\star\|&\leq D.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

Both oracles accept queries at any point of $E$. Calls to the map oracle and the Jacobian oracle are counted separately. After the allotted calls, the output must satisfy the residual criterion
\[
\|F(\bar{x})\|\leq\varepsilon
\quad P\text{-a.s.}
\]
