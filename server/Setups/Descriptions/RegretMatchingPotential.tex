\section*{Regret Matching+ in Potential Games}
We consider a two-player game with payoff matrices $A,C\in\mathbb{R}^{m\times n}$, where $m,n\geq 1$ are the players' numbers of actions. Mixed strategies are probability vectors in $\mathbb{R}^m$ and $\mathbb{R}^n$. Unilateral payoff differences agree with those of an exact potential taking values in $[0,B]$, where $B>0$. The oracle returns $Ay$ and $C^\top x$ at a strategy pair $(x,y)$. For $\varepsilon>0$, Regret Matching+ must return probability vectors $(\bar{x},\bar{y})$ for which neither player can increase its expected payoff by more than $\varepsilon$ through any unilateral pure-action deviation.

Write
\[
\Delta_d=\left\{z\in\mathbb{R}^d:z_i\geq 0\text{ for all }i,\ \sum_{i=1}^d z_i=1\right\}.
\]
The players maximize the respective payoffs $x^\top Ay$ and $x^\top Cy$ over $x\in\Delta_m$ and $y\in\Delta_n$. The algorithm receives only $m$, $n$, and $\varepsilon$; the bound $B$ may enter the oracle-call budget but is not an algorithm input.

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Bounded exact potential]
There exists a matrix $H\in\mathbb{R}^{m\times n}$ such that, for all $i,i'\in\{1,\ldots,m\}$ and $j,j'\in\{1,\ldots,n\}$,
\[
\begin{aligned}
0\leq H_{ij}&\leq B,\\
A_{ij}-A_{i'j}&=H_{ij}-H_{i'j},\\
C_{ij}-C_{ij'}&=H_{ij}-H_{ij'}.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

Queries may be made at any $(x,y)\in\mathbb{R}^m\times\mathbb{R}^n$. One oracle call returns both exact payoff vectors
\[
\begin{aligned}
u_i&=\sum_{j=1}^n A_{ij}y_j, &&i=1,\ldots,m,\\
v_j&=\sum_{i=1}^m C_{ij}x_i, &&j=1,\ldots,n.
\end{aligned}
\]

The setup is algorithm-specific: we analyze Regret Matching+ with simultaneous updates and retention of the most recent pair passing the deviation test. For $d\geq 1$ and $q\in\mathbb{R}^d$, define
\[
S_d(q)_i=
\begin{cases}
q_i\big/\sum_{\ell=1}^d q_\ell,&\displaystyle\sum_{\ell=1}^d q_\ell>0,\\
1/d,&\displaystyle\sum_{\ell=1}^d q_\ell\leq 0.
\end{cases}
\]
Initialize the two regret vectors as $q^0=0\in\mathbb{R}^m$ and $r^0=0\in\mathbb{R}^n$, and initialize the retained pair as
\[
z^0=(S_m(0),S_n(0)).
\]
At iteration $t\geq 0$, query
\[
\begin{aligned}
x^t&=S_m(q^t), & y^t&=S_n(r^t),\\
u^t&=Ay^t, & v^t&=C^\top x^t.
\end{aligned}
\]
Define the instantaneous deviation gains and update the regret vectors by
\[
\begin{aligned}
a_i^t&=u_i^t-(x^t)^\top u^t,
& q_i^{t+1}&=\max\{q_i^t+a_i^t,0\},\\
b_j^t&=v_j^t-(y^t)^\top v^t,
& r_j^{t+1}&=\max\{r_j^t+b_j^t,0\}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The retained pair is updated using the strategies queried at that iteration:
\[
z^{t+1}=
\begin{cases}
(x^t,y^t),& a_i^t\leq\varepsilon\text{ for every }i
\text{ and }b_j^t\leq\varepsilon\text{ for every }j,\\
z^t,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\]
After the allotted $T$ oracle calls, the output is $(\bar{x},\bar{y})=z^T$. Thus, if no queried pair passes the test, the output remains the initial uniform pair.

The required accuracy is an $\varepsilon$-Nash equilibrium:
\[
\begin{aligned}
\bar{x}&\in\Delta_m,\qquad \bar{y}\in\Delta_n,\\
(A\bar{y})_i&\leq\bar{x}^\top A\bar{y}+\varepsilon,
&&i=1,\ldots,m,\\
(C^\top\bar{x})_j&\leq\bar{x}^\top C\bar{y}+\varepsilon,
&&j=1,\ldots,n.
\end{aligned}
\]
These requirements hold for every realization, rather than only in expectation or almost surely.
