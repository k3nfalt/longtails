\section*{Expected Smooth Convex Minimization}
We consider minimizing a differentiable convex function $f:\mathbb{R}^d\to\mathbb{R}$ with $L$-Lipschitz gradient, where $d$ is a nonnegative integer and $L>0$. A given point $x^0\in\mathbb{R}^d$ lies within Euclidean distance $R>0$ of a global minimizer $x^\star$. The oracle returns $\nabla f(x)$ at $x$. For a tolerance $\varepsilon>0$, the goal is a random output $\bar{x}\in\mathbb{R}^d$ with integrable objective gap and $\mathbb{E}_{P}[f(\bar{x})-f(x^\star)]\leq\varepsilon$, where $P$ is the law of the algorithm's randomness. The parameters satisfy the accuracy and dimension restrictions specified below.

The optimization problem is
\[
\min_{x\in\mathbb{R}^d} f(x).
\]
Throughout, $\|\cdot\|$ denotes the Euclidean norm. The algorithm receives $d$, $L$, $x^0$, $R$, and $\varepsilon$.

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Parameter regime]
The parameters satisfy $d\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}$ and $L,R,\varepsilon>0$, with
\[
\begin{aligned}
262144\,\varepsilon &\leq LR^2,\\
65536\left(\left\lfloor
\frac{\sqrt{LR^2/\varepsilon}}{512}
\right\rfloor+1\right)^5 &\leq d.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Convexity and smoothness]
The function $f$ is differentiable everywhere on $\mathbb{R}^d$. For every $x,y\in\mathbb{R}^d$ and $t\in[0,1]$,
\[
\begin{aligned}
f((1-t)x+ty)&\leq (1-t)f(x)+tf(y),\\
\|\nabla f(x)-\nabla f(y)\|&\leq L\|x-y\|.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Initial distance]
There exists $x^\star\in\mathbb{R}^d$ such that
\[
\begin{aligned}
f(x^\star)&\leq f(x)
&&\text{for every }x\in\mathbb{R}^d,\\
\|x^0-x^\star\|&\leq R.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

Queries may be made at any point of $\mathbb{R}^d$. After the allotted oracle calls, the output $\bar{x}$ must have an integrable objective gap under $P$ and satisfy
\[
\mathbb{E}_{P}\bigl[f(\bar{x})-f(x^\star)\bigr]
\leq\varepsilon.
\]
