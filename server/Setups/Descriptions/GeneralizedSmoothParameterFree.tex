\section*{Convex Minimization with Hidden Smoothness}
We consider minimizing a convex function $f:E\to\mathbb{R}$ on a real Hilbert space $E$. The function $f$ is $(L_0,L_1)$-smooth with hidden constants $L_0>0$ and $L_1\geq0$, and has a differentiable gradient. A given point $x^0\in E$ satisfies $\|x^0-x^\star\|\leq R$ and $f(x^0)-f(x^\star)\leq\Delta$ for a global minimizer $x^\star$ and hidden bounds $R,\Delta>0$. The oracle returns $\nabla f(x)$ at $x$. For $\varepsilon>0$, the goal is an output $\bar{x}\in E$ satisfying $f(\bar{x})-f(x^\star)\leq\varepsilon$ almost surely under $P$, the law of the algorithm's randomness.

The optimization problem is
\[
\min_{x\in E} f(x).
\]
The norm $\|\cdot\|$ is induced by the inner product on $E$, and $\|\cdot\|_{\mathrm{op}}$ denotes the induced operator norm. The algorithm receives only $E$, $x^0$, and a scalar $h_0>0$; the parameters $L_0,L_1,R,\Delta$, and $\varepsilon$ may enter the oracle-call budget but are not algorithm inputs.

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Convexity]
For every $x,y\in E$ and $t\in[0,1]$,
\[
f((1-t)x+ty)\leq (1-t)f(x)+t f(y).
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Generalized smoothness]
The function $f$ is Fr\'echet differentiable everywhere on $E$, and its gradient $\nabla f:E\to E$ is Fr\'echet differentiable everywhere. Its Hessian $\nabla^2 f(x):E\to E$ satisfies
\[
\|\nabla^2 f(x)\|_{\mathrm{op}}
\leq L_0+L_1\|\nabla f(x)\|,
\qquad x\in E.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Initial distance and objective gap]
There exists $x^\star\in E$ such that
\[
\begin{aligned}
f(x^\star)&\leq f(x) &&\text{for every }x\in E,\\
\|x^0-x^\star\|&\leq R,\\
f(x^0)-f(x^\star)&\leq\Delta.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

Queries may be made at any point of $E$. After the allotted oracle calls, the output must satisfy
\[
f(\bar{x})-f(x^\star)\leq\varepsilon
\quad P\text{-a.s.}
\]
The minimizer $x^\star$ is fixed independently of the algorithm's randomness.
