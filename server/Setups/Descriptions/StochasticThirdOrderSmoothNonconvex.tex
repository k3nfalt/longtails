\section*{Third-Order Smooth Stochastic Nonconvex Optimization}
We seek an approximate stationary point of a possibly nonconvex function $f:E\to\mathbb{R}$ on a finite-dimensional real inner product space $E$. Its first three derivatives are globally Lipschitz, and the gap between the starting value and the infimum is bounded. At each query $x\in E$, the oracle uses a fresh independent sample to return an unbiased estimate of $\nabla f(x)$ with uniformly bounded variance. For a tolerance $\varepsilon>0$, the goal is a measurable output $\bar{x}\in E$ satisfying $\mathbb{E}[\|\nabla f(\bar{x})\|]\leq\varepsilon$, where the expectation includes both oracle and algorithm randomness.

The underlying minimization problem is
\[
\inf_{x\in E} f(x).
\]
The algorithm receives $E$ with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra, the measurable sample space $\Omega$, the starting point $x^0\in E$, the smoothness constants $L_1,L_2,L_3>0$, the noise bound $\sigma\geq 0$, the initial gap bound $\Delta\geq 0$, and $\varepsilon$. We write $\|\cdot\|$ for the inner product norm and $\|\cdot\|_{\mathrm{op}}$ for the induced operator and multilinear norms.

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Smoothness through third order]
The function $f$ is three times continuously differentiable. For all $x,y\in E$,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\|\nabla f(x)-\nabla f(y)\| &\leq L_1\|x-y\|,\\
\|\nabla^2 f(x)-\nabla^2 f(y)\|_{\mathrm{op}} &\leq L_2\|x-y\|,\\
\|\nabla^3 f(x)-\nabla^3 f(y)\|_{\mathrm{op}} &\leq L_3\|x-y\|.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Initial objective gap]
The starting point satisfies
\[
f(x^0)-\inf_{x\in E}f(x)\leq\Delta.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Stochastic gradient oracle]
On call $k$ at any query $x\in E$, the oracle returns $g(x,\xi_k)$, where $g:E\times\Omega\to E$ is jointly measurable. The samples $(\xi_k)_{k\geq 0}$ are independent random variables with common law $\mu$ on $\Omega$, and the sample sequence is independent of the algorithm's randomness. For every fixed $x\in E$ and $\xi\sim\mu$, the vector $g(x,\xi)$ is Bochner integrable, the squared error $\|g(x,\xi)-\nabla f(x)\|^2$ is integrable, and
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb{E}_{\xi\sim\mu}[g(x,\xi)] &= \nabla f(x),\\
\mathbb{E}_{\xi\sim\mu}\bigl[\|g(x,\xi)-\nabla f(x)\|^2\bigr] &\leq \sigma^2.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

After the allotted oracle calls, the output $\bar{x}$ must be measurable under the joint randomness of the algorithm and the sample sequence. Its gradient norm must be integrable under this law and satisfy
\[
\mathbb{E}\bigl[\|\nabla f(\bar{x})\|\bigr]\leq\varepsilon.
\]
