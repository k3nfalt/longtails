\section*{Generalized Lipschitz Monotone Root Finding}
We seek a zero of a continuous monotone operator $F:E\to E$ on a real inner product space $E$ with induced norm $\|\cdot\|$. For constants $L_0>0$ and $L_1\geq0$, the Lipschitz bound along each segment is $L_0+L_1M$ whenever $M$ bounds the norm of $F$ on that segment. A given point $x^0\in E$ satisfies $\|F(x^0)\|\leq G$ and lies within distance $R$ of a zero, where $G,R\geq0$. The oracle returns $F(x)$ at $x$. For $\varepsilon>0$, the goal is an output $\bar{x}\in E$ with $\|F(\bar{x})\|\leq\varepsilon$ almost surely under $P$, the law of the algorithm's randomness.

The root-finding problem is to find $x\in E$ such that
\[
F(x)=0.
\]
Write $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle$ for the inner product on $E$. The algorithm receives only $E$, $L_0$, $L_1$, and $x^0$; the residual bound $G$, radius $R$, and tolerance $\varepsilon$ may enter the oracle-call budget but are not algorithm inputs.

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Continuity and monotonicity]
The operator $F$ is continuous, and for every $x,y\in E$,
\[
\langle F(x)-F(y),x-y\rangle\geq0.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Generalized Lipschitz condition]
For every $x,y\in E$ and $M\in\mathbb{R}$ satisfying
\[
\|F(x+\theta(y-x))\|\leq M
\qquad\text{for every }\theta\in[0,1],
\]
the operator satisfies
\[
\|F(x)-F(y)\|\leq (L_0+L_1M)\|x-y\|.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Initial residual and distance]
There exists $x^\star\in E$ such that
\[
\begin{aligned}
F(x^\star)&=0,\\
\|x^0-x^\star\|&\leq R,\\
\|F(x^0)\|&\leq G.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

Queries may be made at any point of $E$. After the allotted oracle calls, the output must satisfy the residual criterion
\[
\|F(\bar{x})\|\leq\varepsilon
\quad P\text{-a.s.}
\]
