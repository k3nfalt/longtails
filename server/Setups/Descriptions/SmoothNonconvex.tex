\section*{Smooth Nonconvex Stationarity}
We consider a differentiable, possibly nonconvex function $f:E\to\mathbb{R}$ on a real Hilbert space $E$. Its gradient is $L$-Lipschitz, where $L>0$, and a given starting point $x^0\in E$ satisfies $f(x^0)-\ell\leq\Delta$ for a global lower bound $\ell\in\mathbb{R}$ and $\Delta\geq0$. The oracle returns $\nabla f(x)$ at $x$. For a tolerance $\varepsilon>0$, the goal is a measurable output $\bar{x}\in E$ with integrable squared gradient norm satisfying $\mathbb{E}_{P}[\|\nabla f(\bar{x})\|^2]\leq\varepsilon^2$, where $P$ is the law of the algorithm's randomness.

We seek approximate stationarity for the optimization problem
\[
\inf_{x\in E} f(x).
\]
The norm $\|\cdot\|$ is induced by the inner product on $E$, which is equipped with a measurable structure. The algorithm receives $E$, $L$, $\Delta$, $\varepsilon$, and $x^0$; the objective is accessed through the oracle.

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Smoothness]
The function $f$ is Fr\'echet differentiable everywhere on $E$, and its gradient satisfies
\[
\|\nabla f(x)-\nabla f(y)\|\leq L\|x-y\|,
\qquad x,y\in E.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Lower bound and initial gap]
There exists $\ell\in\mathbb{R}$ such that
\[
\begin{aligned}
\ell&\leq f(x) &&\text{for every }x\in E,\\
f(x^0)-\ell&\leq\Delta.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

Queries may be made at any point of $E$. After the allotted oracle calls, the output $\bar{x}$ must be measurable with respect to the given measurable structure on $E$, and must satisfy
\[
\mathbb{E}_{P}\!\left[\|\nabla f(\bar{x})\|^2\right]<\infty,
\qquad
\mathbb{E}_{P}\!\left[\|\nabla f(\bar{x})\|^2\right]\leq\varepsilon^2.
\]
