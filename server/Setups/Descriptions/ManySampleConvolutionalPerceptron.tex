\section*{Many-Sample Convolutional Separation}
We consider strict separation of one positive reference vector and $n\geq1$ negative sample vectors in $\mathbb{R}^d$, where $d\geq1$, using parameters $\theta=((a,b),w)\in\mathbb{R}^2\times\mathbb{R}^d$. The score is $h_\theta(x)=a\langle x,w\rangle+b\langle Sx,w\rangle$, where $S$ is the cyclic coordinate shift and $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle$ is the Euclidean inner product. The reference is constant across coordinates; samples have bounded positive constant backgrounds and small centered defects whose cyclic differences share a separating direction. The oracle returns a selected example's signed score and parameter direction. The goal is an output with strictly positive reference score and strictly negative scores on all samples, for every realization of algorithm randomness.

Write the data as a reference $x_0\in\mathbb{R}^d$ and samples $x_1,\ldots,x_n\in\mathbb{R}^d$. Coordinates are indexed by $j=0,\ldots,d-1$, with
\[
(Sx)_j=x_{j-1\bmod d},
\qquad
u=\frac{1}{\sqrt d}(1,\ldots,1).
\]
All inner products and norms on $\mathbb{R}^d$ are Euclidean. The algorithm receives $d,n,g,R,\mu,\kappa$, where $g,R$ bound the background amplitudes, $\mu$ scales the defects, and $\kappa$ is a directional margin. The data and their geometric witnesses are accessed only through the oracle.

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Parameter regime]
The dimensions satisfy $d,n\in\mathbb{N}_{\geq1}$, and
\[
0<g\leq R,\qquad \mu>0,\qquad 0<\kappa\leq1.
\]
Define
\[
\rho=\frac{g}{4R},\qquad
\delta=\left(\frac{\mu}{4R}\right)^2,\qquad
B=\left\lceil\frac{8}{\rho^2}\log\frac{1}{\delta}\right\rceil_++2,
\]
where $\lceil t\rceil_+$ is the smallest nonnegative integer at least $t$. These quantities satisfy
\[
\begin{aligned}
\delta&\leq e^{-1},\\
\delta(B+1)^3&\leq\frac{\kappa^3}{16777216},\\
\delta(B+1)^2&\leq\frac{\kappa^2\rho^4}{16777216}.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Background and defect geometry]
There exist amplitudes $c_0,c_1,\ldots,c_n\in\mathbb{R}$, defects $z_1,\ldots,z_n\in\mathbb{R}^d$, and a vector $v\in\mathbb{R}^d$ such that
\[
\begin{aligned}
x_0&=\frac{c_0}{\sqrt2}\,u,
&g\leq c_0&\leq R,\\
x_i&=\frac{c_i}{\sqrt2}\,u+\mu z_i,
&g\leq c_i&\leq R
\qquad (1\leq i\leq n).
\end{aligned}
\]
The defects and common direction satisfy
\[
\begin{aligned}
\langle u,z_i\rangle&=0,
&\|z_i\|&\leq\frac{1}{\sqrt2}
&& (1\leq i\leq n),\\
\|v\|&=1,\\
-\left\langle\frac{z_i-Sz_i}{\sqrt2},v\right\rangle
&\geq\kappa
&&&& (1\leq i\leq n).
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

For oracle access, define signed examples $q_0=x_0$ and $q_i=-x_i$ for $1\leq i\leq n$. A query consists of any index $i\in\{0,\ldots,n\}$ and any parameter $\theta=((a,b),w)\in\mathbb{R}^2\times\mathbb{R}^d$. The oracle returns the exact pair
\[
\bigl(m_i(\theta),G_i(\theta)\bigr),
\]
where
\[
\begin{aligned}
m_i(\theta)&=a\langle q_i,w\rangle+b\langle Sq_i,w\rangle,\\
G_i(\theta)&=\bigl((\langle q_i,w\rangle,\langle Sq_i,w\rangle),
                    a q_i+b Sq_i\bigr).
\end{aligned}
\]
Initialization is chosen by the algorithm. After the allotted oracle calls, its output $\bar\theta\in\mathbb{R}^2\times\mathbb{R}^d$ must satisfy
\[
\begin{aligned}
h_{\bar\theta}(x_0)&>0,\\
h_{\bar\theta}(x_i)&<0\qquad (1\leq i\leq n),
\end{aligned}
\]
for every realization of the algorithm's randomness.
