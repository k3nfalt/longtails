\section*{Monotone Variational Inequalities with Lipschitz Jacobians}
We consider a monotone map $F:E\to E$ on a compact convex set $X$ in a finite-dimensional real inner product space $E$. The map is differentiable on $X$ with an $L$-Lipschitz Jacobian, where $L>0$. Two exact oracles return $F(x)$ and its Jacobian, respectively. For $\varepsilon>0$, the goal is a feasible output $\bar{x}\in X$ admitting an outward normal $n\in E$, meaning $\langle n,z-\bar{x}\rangle\leq0$ for every $z\in X$, such that $\|F(\bar{x})+n\|\leq\varepsilon$ almost surely under $P$, the law of the algorithm's randomness.

The underlying variational inequality is to find $x\in X$ such that
\[
\langle F(x),z-x\rangle\geq0
\qquad\text{for every }z\in X.
\]
Write $J_F(x):E\to E$ for the Fr\'echet derivative of $F$ at $x$. The norm $\|\cdot\|$ is induced by the inner product on $E$, and $\|\cdot\|_{\mathrm{op}}$ denotes the induced operator norm. The algorithm receives $E$, $X$, a given starting point $x^0$, constants $L,D,\varepsilon>0$, and an integer $m\geq1$.

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Feasible set and starting point]
The set $X\subseteq E$ is compact and convex, and
\[
x^0\in X,
\qquad
\|x-y\|\leq D\quad\text{for every }x,y\in X.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Monotonicity]
For every $x,y\in X$,
\[
\langle F(x)-F(y),x-y\rangle\geq0.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Lipschitz Jacobian]
The map $F:E\to E$ is Fr\'echet differentiable at every point of $X$, and
\[
\|J_F(x)-J_F(y)\|_{\mathrm{op}}
\leq L\|x-y\|
\qquad\text{for every }x,y\in X.
\]
\end{assumption}

Queries to either oracle may be made at any point of $E$. The value oracle returns $F(x)$ at $x$, and the Jacobian oracle returns $J_F(x)$ at $x$; outside $X$, the latter is interpreted as the zero operator wherever $F$ is not differentiable. Calls to the two oracles are counted separately.

Define the normal cone at $x\in X$ by
\[
N_X(x)=\{n\in E:\langle n,z-x\rangle\leq0
\text{ for every }z\in X\}.
\]
After the allotted oracle calls, the required accuracy is
\[
\bar{x}\in X,
\qquad
\exists n\in N_X(\bar{x}):\
\|F(\bar{x})+n\|\leq\varepsilon
\quad P\text{-a.s.}
\]
