\section*{Zeros of H\"older Monotone Operators}
We seek a zero of a monotone operator $F:E\to E$ on a finite-dimensional real inner product space $E$. The operator satisfies a mixed Lipschitz--H\"older bound with constants $L,H\geq 0$ and exponent $\alpha\in(0,1]$. A given point $x^0\in E$ lies within distance $R>0$ of a zero $x^\star$ of $F$. These regularity parameters and $R$ are hidden from the algorithm. The oracle returns $F(x)$ at $x$. For $\varepsilon>0$, the goal is an output $\bar{x}\in E$ satisfying $\|F(\bar{x})\|\leq\varepsilon$ almost surely under $P$, the law of the algorithm's randomness.

The problem is to find
\[
x^\star\in E\quad\text{such that}\quad F(x^\star)=0.
\]
Write $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle$ for the inner product and $\|\cdot\|$ for its induced norm. The algorithm receives only $E$, $x^0$, a radius parameter $r_0$, and $\varepsilon$, where $0<r_0\leq R$; the parameters $L,H,\alpha,R$ may enter only the oracle-call budget.

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Monotonicity]
For every $x,y\in E$,
\[
\langle F(x)-F(y),x-y\rangle\geq 0.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Mixed Lipschitz--H\"older continuity]
For every $x,y\in E$,
\[
\|F(x)-F(y)\|
\leq L\|x-y\|+H\|x-y\|^{\alpha}.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Initial distance to a zero]
There exists a zero $x^\star$ of $F$ satisfying
\[
\|x^0-x^\star\|\leq R.
\]
\end{assumption}

Queries may be made at any point of $E$. After the allotted oracle calls, the output must satisfy the residual criterion
\[
\|F(\bar{x})\|\leq\varepsilon
\quad P\text{-a.s.}
\]
