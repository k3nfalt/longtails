\section*{Convex Minimization with Hessian Access}
We consider minimizing a three-times continuously differentiable convex function $f:E\to\mathbb{R}$ on a finite-dimensional real inner product space $E$. We assume a gradient-dependent third-derivative bound, a hidden bound on the Hessian at a given starting point $x^0\in E$, and bounds on its distance and objective gap to a global minimizer $x^\star\in E$. Two exact oracles return $\nabla f(x)$ and $\nabla^2 f(x)$ at $x\in E$, respectively. For a tolerance $\varepsilon>0$, the goal is an output $\bar{x}\in E$ satisfying $f(\bar{x})-f(x^\star)\leq\varepsilon$ almost surely under $P$, the law of the algorithm's randomness.

The optimization problem is
\[
\min_{x\in E} f(x).
\]
The norm $\|\cdot\|$ is induced by the inner product on $E$, and $\|\cdot\|_{\mathrm{op}}$ denotes the induced operator and multilinear norms. We regard $\nabla^2 f(x)$ as a linear operator from $E$ to $E$ and $\nabla^3 f(x)$ as a trilinear form on $E$. The parameters satisfy
\[
M_0,M_1,H_0\geq 0,
\qquad R,\Delta,\varepsilon>0.
\]
The algorithm receives $E$, $x^0$, $M_0$, $M_1$, $R$, $\Delta$, and $\varepsilon$; the hidden parameter $H_0$ may enter only the oracle-call budget.

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Regularity and convexity]
The function $f$ belongs to $C^3(E;\mathbb{R})$ and, for every $x,y\in E$ and $t\in[0,1]$,
\[
f((1-t)x+ty)\leq (1-t)f(x)+t f(y).
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Third-derivative bound]
For every $x\in E$,
\[
\|\nabla^3 f(x)\|_{\mathrm{op}}
\leq M_0+M_1\|\nabla f(x)\|.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Initial Hessian and minimizer bounds]
At the given starting point,
\[
\|\nabla^2 f(x^0)\|_{\mathrm{op}}\leq H_0.
\]
There exists $x^\star\in E$ such that
\[
\begin{aligned}
f(x^\star)&\leq f(x) &&\text{for every }x\in E,\\
\|x^0-x^\star\|&\leq R,\\
f(x^0)-f(x^\star)&\leq\Delta.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

Queries to either oracle may be made at any point of $E$. Gradient calls and Hessian calls are counted separately. After the allotted calls, the output must satisfy
\[
f(\bar{x})-f(x^\star)\leq\varepsilon
\quad P\text{-a.s.}
\]
