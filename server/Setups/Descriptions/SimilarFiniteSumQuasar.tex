\section*{Similar Finite-Sum Quasar Minimization}
We consider minimizing $F(x)=n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^n f_i(x)$ on a real Hilbert space $E$, where $n\geq 1$ and the functions $f_i:E\to\mathbb{R}$ are differentiable. The potentially nonconvex objective has a Lipschitz gradient, satisfies a quadratic lower model, and obeys strong quasar-convexity and quadratic growth relative to a global minimizer $x^\star$. Component-gradient deviations from the mean are Lipschitz with a restricted similarity constant. A given point $x^0\in E$ has bounded objective gap. The oracle returns $\nabla f_i(x)$ at a queried pair $(i,x)$. For $\varepsilon>0$, the goal is an output $\bar{x}\in E$ with $F(\bar{x})-F(x^\star)\leq\varepsilon$ almost surely under the algorithm's randomness.

The optimization problem is
\[
\min_{x\in E} F(x).
\]
The norm $\|\cdot\|$ is induced by the inner product $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle$ on $E$. The parameters satisfy
\[
\begin{aligned}
&0<\mu\leq L, \qquad 0<\gamma\leq 1,\\
&\delta\geq 0, \qquad 384\delta^2\leq\gamma^3\mu^2,\\
&\Delta\geq 0, \qquad \varepsilon>0.
\end{aligned}
\]
The algorithm receives $E$, $n$, $L$, $\mu$, $\gamma$, $\delta$, $\Delta$, $\varepsilon$, and $x^0$.

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Smoothness and component similarity]
Each $f_i$ is Fr\'echet differentiable everywhere on $E$. For every $x,y\in E$ and $i\in\{1,\ldots,n\}$,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\|\nabla F(x)-\nabla F(y)\|&\leq L\|x-y\|,\\
\| (\nabla f_i(x)-\nabla F(x))
-(\nabla f_i(y)-\nabla F(y))\|
&\leq\delta\|x-y\|.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Quadratic lower model]
For every $x,y\in E$,
\[
F(x)+\langle\nabla F(x),y-x\rangle
-\frac{\gamma\mu}{2\sqrt{\mu/L}}\|y-x\|^2
\leq F(y).
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Strong quasar-convexity and quadratic growth]
There exists a global minimizer $x^\star\in E$ such that
\[
F(x^0)-F(x^\star)\leq\Delta,
\]
and, for every $x\in E$,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\gamma F(x)+\langle\nabla F(x),x^\star-x\rangle
+\frac{\gamma\mu}{2}\|x-x^\star\|^2
&\leq\gamma F(x^\star),\\
\frac{\gamma\mu}{2(2-\gamma)}\|x-x^\star\|^2
&\leq F(x)-F(x^\star).
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

Queries may use any component index $i\in\{1,\ldots,n\}$ and any point $x\in E$. After the allotted component-gradient calls, the output must satisfy
\[
F(\bar{x})-F(x^\star)\leq\varepsilon
\quad P\text{-a.s.},
\]
where $P$ is the law of the algorithm's randomness.
