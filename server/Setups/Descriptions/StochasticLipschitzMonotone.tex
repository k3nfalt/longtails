\section*{Stochastic Lipschitz Monotone Root Finding}
We seek a zero of a monotone, $L$-Lipschitz operator $F:E\to E$ on a finite-dimensional real Hilbert space $E$, where $L>0$. A given point $x^0\in E$ lies within distance $R\geq0$ of a zero $x^\star$. At each query, the oracle returns an unbiased estimate of $F(x)$ with mean squared error at most $\sigma^2$, where $\sigma\geq0$, using independent, identically distributed samples. For $\varepsilon>0$, the goal is a measurable output $\bar{x}\in E$ with integrable squared residual satisfying $\mathbb{E}\|F(\bar{x})\|^2\leq\varepsilon^2$, where expectation includes oracle and independent algorithm randomness.

The root-finding problem is
\[
\text{find }x\in E\text{ such that }F(x)=0.
\]
Write $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle$ for the inner product on $E$ and $\|\cdot\|$ for its induced norm. Let $\Omega$ be the measurable sample space. The algorithm receives $E$, $\Omega$, $x^0$, $L$, $R$, $\sigma$, and $\varepsilon$; it accesses $F$ through the oracle.

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Monotonicity and Lipschitz continuity]
For every $x,y\in E$,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\langle F(x)-F(y),x-y\rangle&\geq0,\\
\|F(x)-F(y)\|&\leq L\|x-y\|.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Nearby zero]
There exists $x^\star\in E$ such that
\[
F(x^\star)=0,
\qquad \|x^0-x^\star\|\leq R.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Stochastic operator oracle]
There exist a probability measure $\nu$ on $\Omega$ and a jointly measurable map $g:E\times\Omega\to E$. For every $x\in E$, the map $g(x,\cdot)$ is Bochner integrable under $\nu$, the squared error $\|g(x,\cdot)-F(x)\|^2$ is integrable, and
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb{E}_{\xi\sim\nu}[g(x,\xi)]&=F(x),\\
\mathbb{E}_{\xi\sim\nu}\bigl[\|g(x,\xi)-F(x)\|^2\bigr]&\leq\sigma^2.
\end{aligned}
\]
At call $k\geq0$, a query $x\in E$ receives the reply $g(x,\xi_k)$. The measurable sample variables $(\xi_k)_{k\geq0}$ are mutually independent with common law $\nu$ under a joint probability law on $\Omega^{\mathbb{N}}$. Algorithm randomness is independent of this sample sequence.
\end{assumption}

Queries may be made at any point of $E$. Let $P$ denote the joint law of the algorithm randomness and oracle samples. After the allotted oracle calls, the output $\bar{x}$ must be measurable, its squared residual must be integrable under $P$, and
\[
\mathbb{E}_{P}\bigl[\|F(\bar{x})\|^2\bigr]\leq\varepsilon^2.
\]
