\section*{Cohypomonotone Root Finding}
We consider finding a zero of a differentiable map $F:E\to E$ on a finite-dimensional real inner product space $E$. The map is $\rho$-cohypomonotone, where $\rho\geq 0$, and its derivative $J(x)=DF(x)$ is $H$-Lipschitz in operator norm, where $H>0$. A given starting point $x^0\in E$ lies within distance $R>0$ of a zero $x^\star$ of $F$. The oracle returns $(F(x),J(x))$ at $x$. For a tolerance $\varepsilon>0$, the goal is an output $\bar{x}\in E$ satisfying $\|F(\bar{x})\|\leq\varepsilon$ almost surely under $P$, the law of the algorithm's randomness.

The root-finding problem is to find $x\in E$ such that
\[
F(x)=0.
\]
Write $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle$ for the inner product and $\|\cdot\|$ for its induced norm; $\|\cdot\|_{\mathrm{op}}$ denotes the induced operator norm for linear maps from $E$ to $E$. The algorithm receives $E$, $H$, $\rho$, $R$, $\varepsilon$, and $x^0$.

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Lipschitz derivative]
The map $F$ is Fr\'echet differentiable everywhere on $E$, with derivative $J(x):E\to E$, and
\[
\|J(x)-J(y)\|_{\mathrm{op}}\leq H\|x-y\|,
\qquad x,y\in E.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Cohypomonotonicity]
For every $x,y\in E$,
\[
\langle F(x)-F(y),x-y\rangle
\geq -\rho\|F(x)-F(y)\|^2.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Initial distance to a zero]
There exists $x^\star\in E$ such that
\[
\begin{aligned}
F(x^\star)&=0,\\
\|x^0-x^\star\|&\leq R.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

Queries may be made at any point of $E$, and both components of the oracle reply are exact. After the allotted oracle calls, the output must satisfy the residual criterion
\[
\|F(\bar{x})\|\leq\varepsilon
\quad P\text{-a.s.}
\]
