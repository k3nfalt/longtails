\section*{Window SGD under the PL Condition}
We consider minimizing a possibly nonconvex function $f:E\to\mathbb{R}$ on a finite-dimensional real inner product space $E$. The objective has a Lipschitz gradient, a Lipschitz Hessian, and a global minimizer $x^\star$, and satisfies the Polyak--\L{}ojasiewicz condition. A given starting point has bounded objective gap. At $x\in E$, the oracle returns $g(x,\xi)$, an unbiased gradient estimate using a fresh independent sample $\xi$, with uniformly bounded second and fourth noise moments. Window SGD must produce a measurable output $\bar{x}\in E$ with integrable objective gap and $\mathbb{E}[f(\bar{x})-f(x^\star)]\leq\varepsilon$, where $\varepsilon>0$ and expectation includes sampling and algorithm randomness.

The optimization problem is
\[
\min_{x\in E} f(x).
\]
Write $\|\cdot\|$ for the inner product norm and $\|\cdot\|_{\mathrm{op}}$ for the induced operator norm. Fix a starting point $x^0\in E$, a measurable sample space $\Omega$, and parameters
\[
0<\mu\leq L,\qquad \rho,\sigma,\Delta\geq0,\qquad K\geq1,\qquad \varepsilon>0.
\]
The algorithm receives $E$, $\Omega$, $L$, $\mu$, $\rho$, $\sigma$, $K$, $\Delta$, $\varepsilon$, and $x^0$.

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Gradient and Hessian regularity]
The function $f$ is Fr\'echet differentiable on $E$, and its gradient is Fr\'echet differentiable on $E$. For every $x,y\in E$,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\|\nabla f(x)-\nabla f(y)\|&\leq L\|x-y\|,\\
\|\nabla^2 f(x)-\nabla^2 f(y)\|_{\mathrm{op}}&\leq\rho\|x-y\|.
\end{aligned}
\]
Here $\nabla^2 f(x):E\to E$ is the derivative of the gradient.
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Polyak--\L{}ojasiewicz condition and initial gap]
There exists $x^\star\in E$ such that, for every $x\in E$,
\[
\begin{aligned}
f(x^\star)&\leq f(x),\\
2\mu\bigl(f(x)-f(x^\star)\bigr)&\leq\|\nabla f(x)\|^2,
\end{aligned}
\]
and the given starting point satisfies
\[
f(x^0)-f(x^\star)\leq\Delta.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Independent stochastic gradients and fourth moments]
There is a probability measure $\nu$ on $\Omega$ and a jointly measurable map $g:E\times\Omega\to E$. The measurable samples $(\xi_k)_{k\geq0}$ are mutually independent, each with law $\nu$. At its $k$th call, the oracle returns $g(x,\xi_k)$ for any query $x\in E$.
For every fixed $x\in E$, $g(x,\xi)$ is Bochner integrable under $\xi\sim\nu$, and
\[
\mathbb{E}_{\xi\sim\nu}[g(x,\xi)]=\nabla f(x).
\]
Writing $e(x,\xi)=g(x,\xi)-\nabla f(x)$, both $\|e(x,\xi)\|^2$ and $\|e(x,\xi)\|^4$ are integrable, with
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb{E}_{\xi\sim\nu}\bigl[\|e(x,\xi)\|^2\bigr]&\leq\sigma^2,\\
\mathbb{E}_{\xi\sim\nu}\bigl[\|e(x,\xi)\|^4\bigr]&\leq K\sigma^4.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

Our formulation is algorithm-specific: we analyze Window SGD. The algorithm chooses a constant step size $\eta>0$ and integers $T_a,T_b\geq0$ from its inputs. Independently of the oracle samples, it draws $\tau$ uniformly from $\{0,\ldots,T_b\}$ and sets $H=T_a+\tau$. Starting from the given $x^0$, its recurrence for a budget of $N\geq0$ oracle calls is
\[
\begin{aligned}
g_k&=g(x^k,\xi_k),\\
x^{k+1}&=
\begin{cases}
x^k-\eta g_k,&k<H,\\
x^k,&k\geq H,
\end{cases}
\qquad k=0,\ldots,N-1.
\end{aligned}
\]
The output is $\bar{x}=x^N$. Calls continue to count after the iterate is frozen. Thus, when $N\geq T_a+T_b$, the output is the SGD iterate at the uniformly selected index $H$; a smaller budget truncates the updates at $N$.

Let $P$ be the joint law of $\tau$ and the oracle samples. The output must be measurable, its objective gap must be integrable under $P$, and the accuracy requirement is
\[
\mathbb{E}_{P}\bigl[f(\bar{x})-f(x^\star)\bigr]\leq\varepsilon.
\]
