\section*{Smooth Convex Finite-Sum Minimization}
We consider minimizing $F(x)=n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^{n}f_i(x)$ on a real Hilbert space $E$, where $n\geq1$ is an integer and each $f_i:E\to\mathbb{R}$ is differentiable and convex with an $L$-Lipschitz gradient, for $L>0$. A given starting point $x^0\in E$ lies within distance $R\geq0$ of a global minimizer $x^\star$ of $F$. The oracle returns $\nabla f_i(x)$ at a queried pair $(i,x)$. For $\varepsilon>0$, the goal is a measurable output $\bar{x}\in E$ with integrable objective gap satisfying $\mathbb{E}_{P}[F(\bar{x})-F(x^\star)]\leq\varepsilon$, where $P$ is the law of the algorithm's randomness.

The optimization problem is
\[
\min_{x\in E} F(x).
\]
The norm $\|\cdot\|$ is induced by the inner product on $E$, which is equipped with a measurable structure. The algorithm receives $E$, $n$, $L$, $R$, $\varepsilon$, and $x^0$.

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Component convexity]
For every $i\in\{1,\ldots,n\}$, $x,y\in E$, and $t\in[0,1]$,
\[
f_i((1-t)x+ty)\leq (1-t)f_i(x)+t f_i(y).
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Component smoothness]
Each $f_i$ is Fr\'echet differentiable everywhere on $E$, and
\[
\|\nabla f_i(x)-\nabla f_i(y)\|\leq L\|x-y\|,
\qquad x,y\in E,\quad i\in\{1,\ldots,n\}.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Initial distance]
There exists $x^\star\in E$ such that
\[
\begin{aligned}
F(x^\star)&\leq F(x) &&\text{for every }x\in E,\\
\|x^0-x^\star\|&\leq R.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

Queries may select any component $i\in\{1,\ldots,n\}$ and any point $x\in E$, and each call returns its exact component gradient. After the allotted oracle calls, the output $\bar{x}$ must be measurable with respect to the given measurable structure on $E$, its objective gap must be integrable under $P$, and it must satisfy
\[
\mathbb{E}_{P}\bigl[F(\bar{x})-F(x^\star)\bigr]\leq\varepsilon.
\]
