\section*{Finite-Dimensional Weak Minty Root Finding}
We seek an approximate zero of a Lipschitz operator $F:\mathbb{R}^d\to\mathbb{R}^d$ in dimension $d\geq 1$. A given starting point $x^0\in\mathbb{R}^d$ lies within a prescribed radius of a zero $x^\star$ at which $F$ satisfies the weak Minty condition. The oracle returns $F(x)$ at any query point $x\in\mathbb{R}^d$. For a tolerance $\varepsilon>0$, the goal is an output $\bar{x}\in\mathbb{R}^d$ with integrable Euclidean residual norm satisfying $\mathbb{E}[\|F(\bar{x})\|]\leq\varepsilon$, where the expectation is over the algorithm's randomness.

The underlying equation is
\[
F(x)=0,\qquad x\in\mathbb{R}^d.
\]
We use the Euclidean inner product and its induced norm. The algorithm receives $d$, $x^0$, the Lipschitz constant $L$, the weak Minty parameter $\rho$, the radius $R$, and the tolerance $\varepsilon$, with
\[
d\in\{1,2,\ldots\},\qquad L>0,\qquad
0\leq\rho\leq\frac{1}{20L},\qquad 0<\varepsilon<LR.
\]

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Lipschitz continuity]
For all $x,y\in\mathbb{R}^d$,
\[
\|F(x)-F(y)\|\leq L\|x-y\|.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Weak Minty condition at a nearby zero]
There exists $x^\star\in\mathbb{R}^d$ with $F(x^\star)=0$ and $\|x^\star-x^0\|\leq R$ such that, for every $x\in\mathbb{R}^d$,
\[
\langle F(x),x-x^\star\rangle\geq-\rho\|F(x)\|^2.
\]
\end{assumption}

After the allotted oracle calls, the output $\bar{x}$ must satisfy
\[
\|F(\bar{x})\|\in L^1(P),\qquad
\mathbb{E}_{P}[\|F(\bar{x})\|]\leq\varepsilon,
\]
where $P$ is the law of the algorithm's randomness.
