\section*{PL Minimization with Relative Discrepancy}
We consider minimizing a differentiable, possibly nonconvex function $f:E\to\mathbb{R}$ on a real Hilbert space $E$. Its gradient is $L$-Lipschitz, with $L>0$, and it satisfies the Polyak--\L{}ojasiewicz condition with parameter $\mu\in(0,L]$. A given point $x^0\in E$ has objective gap at most $\Delta\geq0$ above a global minimizer $x^\star$. At call $k\geq0$, the oracle returns a deterministic vector $g_k(x)\in E$ at $x$; accumulated errors over every consecutive block at a fixed point have norm at most $D\|\nabla f(x)\|$, with $D\geq0$. For $\varepsilon>0$, the goal is an output $\bar{x}\in E$ satisfying $f(\bar{x})-f(x^\star)\leq\varepsilon$ almost surely under $P$, the law of the algorithm's randomness.

The optimization problem is
\[
\min_{x\in E} f(x).
\]
The norm $\|\cdot\|$ is induced by the inner product on $E$. The algorithm receives only $E$, $L$, $D$, and $x^0$; the parameters $\mu$, $\Delta$, and $\varepsilon$ may enter the oracle-call budget but are not algorithm inputs.

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Smoothness]
The function $f$ is Fr\'echet differentiable everywhere on $E$, and its gradient satisfies
\[
\|\nabla f(x)-\nabla f(y)\|\leq L\|x-y\|,
\qquad x,y\in E.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Polyak--\L{}ojasiewicz condition and initial gap]
There exists $x^\star\in E$ such that
\[
\begin{aligned}
f(x^\star)&\leq f(x) &&\text{for every }x\in E,\\
f(x^0)-f(x^\star)&\leq\Delta,\\
2\mu\bigl(f(x)-f(x^\star)\bigr)&\leq\|\nabla f(x)\|^2
&&\text{for every }x\in E.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Relative discrepancy]
The oracle is specified by a fixed sequence of maps $g_k:E\to E$, indexed by $k=0,1,\ldots$. For every $x\in E$ and all nonnegative integers $s,n$,
\[
\left\|\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}
\bigl(g_{s+i}(x)-\nabla f(x)\bigr)\right\|
\leq D\|\nabla f(x)\|.
\]
The sum is zero when $n=0$. Each block in this condition is evaluated at the same point $x$.
\end{assumption}

Queries may be made at any point of $E$. After the allotted oracle calls, the output must satisfy
\[
f(\bar{x})-f(x^\star)\leq\varepsilon
\quad P\text{-a.s.}
\]
