\section*{Second-Order Weak Minty Root Finding}
We seek a zero of a Lipschitz map $F:E\to E$ on a finite-dimensional real inner product space $E$. Its Jacobian $J$ is Lipschitz, and a given starting point $x^0\in E$ lies within distance $R\geq 0$ of a zero $x^\star$ satisfying the weak Minty condition. At any $x\in E$, the oracle returns either $F(x)$ or $J(x)$, as requested. For a tolerance $\varepsilon>0$, the goal is an output $\bar{x}\in E$ with integrable residual norm satisfying $\mathbb{E}[\|F(\bar{x})\|]\leq\varepsilon$, where the expectation is over the algorithm's randomness.

The root-finding problem is
\[
F(x)=0,\qquad x\in E.
\]
The algorithm receives $E$, the Lipschitz constants $L_1,L_2>0$ for $F$ and $J$, respectively, the weak Minty parameter $0\leq\rho\leq 1/L_1$, the tolerance $\varepsilon$, and the starting point $x^0$. The radius $R$ may enter only the oracle-call budget.

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Operator and Jacobian regularity]
The map $F$ is Fr\'echet differentiable at every $x\in E$, with derivative $J(x):E\to E$. For all $x,y\in E$,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\|F(x)-F(y)\| &\leq L_1\|x-y\|,\\
\|J(x)-J(y)\|_{\mathrm{op}} &\leq L_2\|x-y\|,
\end{aligned}
\]
where $\|\cdot\|_{\mathrm{op}}$ is the operator norm induced by the inner product norm on $E$.
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Weak Minty condition at a nearby zero]
There exists $x^\star\in E$ with $F(x^\star)=0$ and $\|x^0-x^\star\|\leq R$ such that, for every $x\in E$,
\[
\langle F(x),x-x^\star\rangle \geq -\frac{\rho}{2}\|F(x)\|^2.
\]
\end{assumption}

Each call requests either an operator value or a full Jacobian, and both query types count toward a single budget. After the allotted calls, the output must satisfy
\[
\|F(\bar{x})\|\in L^1(P),\qquad
\mathbb{E}_{P}[\|F(\bar{x})\|]\leq\varepsilon,
\]
where $P$ is the law of the algorithm's randomness.
