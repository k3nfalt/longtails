\section*{Cohypomonotone Root Finding with Adjoints}
We seek a zero of a differentiable map $F:E\to E$ on a finite-dimensional real inner product space $E$. The map is $L$-Lipschitz and $\rho$-cohypomonotone, and its derivative $J(x):E\to E$ is $L_2$-Lipschitz, where $L>0$, $L_2\geq0$, and $\rho\geq0$. A given point $x^0\in E$ lies within distance $R\geq0$ of a zero. The oracle returns $F(x)$ or the adjoint product $J(x)^*w$ for $x,w\in E$. For $\varepsilon>0$, the goal is an output $\bar{x}\in E$ satisfying $\|F(\bar{x})\|\leq\varepsilon$ almost surely under $P$, the law of the algorithm's randomness.

The root-finding problem is to find $x\in E$ such that
\[
F(x)=0.
\]
The norm $\|\cdot\|$ is induced by the inner product $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle$ on $E$, and $\|\cdot\|_{\mathrm{op}}$ denotes the induced operator norm. The algorithm receives only $E$, $L$, $L_2$, $\rho$, and $x^0$; the radius $R$ and tolerance $\varepsilon$ may enter the oracle-call budget but are not algorithm inputs.

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Differentiability and Lipschitz bounds]
The map $F$ is Fr\'echet differentiable at every $x\in E$, with derivative $J(x)$. For every $x,y\in E$,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\|F(x)-F(y)\|&\leq L\|x-y\|,\\
\|J(x)-J(y)\|_{\mathrm{op}}&\leq L_2\|x-y\|.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Cohypomonotonicity]
For every $x,y\in E$,
\[
\langle F(x)-F(y),x-y\rangle
\geq -\rho\|F(x)-F(y)\|^2.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Initial distance to a zero]
There exists $x^\star\in E$ such that
\[
\begin{aligned}
F(x^\star)&=0,\\
\|x^0-x^\star\|&\leq R.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

Oracle queries may use any $x,w\in E$. Both reply types are exact, with the adjoint characterized by
\[
\langle J(x)^*w,h\rangle=\langle w,J(x)h\rangle,
\qquad h\in E.
\]
Each evaluation of $F(x)$ or $J(x)^*w$ counts as one call toward a shared oracle-call budget. After the allotted calls, the output must satisfy
\[
\|F(\bar{x})\|\leq\varepsilon
\quad P\text{-a.s.}
\]
