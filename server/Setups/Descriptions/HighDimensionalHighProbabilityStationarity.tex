\section*{High-Dimensional Stationarity with High Probability}
We seek an approximately stationary point of a possibly nonconvex function $f:\mathbb{R}^d\to\mathbb{R}$ in high dimension $d$. Its gradient is $L$-Lipschitz for $L>0$, and the objective value at a given starting point $x^0$ is within $\Delta$ of the finite infimum. At each query, the oracle uses a fresh independent sample to return an unbiased gradient estimate with mean squared error at most $\sigma^2$, where $\sigma\geq 0$. Given a tolerance $\varepsilon>0$ and a failure probability $0<\delta\leq 1/4$, the goal is a measurable output $\bar{x}\in\mathbb{R}^d$ satisfying $\|\nabla f(\bar{x})\|\leq\varepsilon$ with probability at least $1-\delta$.

Write $f_\star$ for the value of the optimization problem
\[
\inf_{x\in\mathbb{R}^d} f(x),
\]
and use the Euclidean norm throughout. The algorithm receives $d$, $L$, $\sigma$, $\Delta$, $\varepsilon$, $\delta$, and $x^0$. The gap bound $\Delta$ and the dimension $d\in\mathbb{N}$ lie in the regime
\[
\begin{aligned}
L\Delta &\geq 2^{54}\varepsilon^2,\\
d &\geq 2^{40}\left(1+\frac{L\Delta}{\varepsilon^2}\left(1+\frac{\sigma^2}{\varepsilon^2}\right)\right)^4.
\end{aligned}
\]

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Smoothness and initial gap]
The function $f$ is differentiable on $\mathbb{R}^d$, and for all $x,y\in\mathbb{R}^d$,
\[
\|\nabla f(x)-\nabla f(y)\|\leq L\|x-y\|.
\]
Its infimum $f_\star$ is finite and satisfies $f(x^0)-f_\star\leq\Delta$.
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Stochastic gradient oracle]
On call $k$ at any $x\in\mathbb{R}^d$, the oracle returns $g(x,\xi_k)$, where $g:\mathbb{R}^d\times\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}^d$ is jointly measurable. The real-valued samples $(\xi_k)_{k\geq 0}$ are independent with common law $\nu$, and the sequence is independent of the algorithm's randomness. For each $x\in\mathbb{R}^d$ and $\xi\sim\nu$, the vector $g(x,\xi)$ is Bochner integrable, the squared error $\|g(x,\xi)-\nabla f(x)\|^2$ is integrable, and
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb{E}_{\xi\sim\nu}[g(x,\xi)] &= \nabla f(x),\\
\mathbb{E}_{\xi\sim\nu}\bigl[\|g(x,\xi)-\nabla f(x)\|^2\bigr] &\leq \sigma^2.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

After the allotted oracle calls, the output $\bar{x}$ must be measurable and satisfy
\[
P\bigl(\|\nabla f(\bar{x})\|\leq\varepsilon\bigr)\geq 1-\delta,
\]
where $P$ is the joint law of the algorithm's randomness and the oracle samples.
