\section*{High-Dimensional Strong Quasar Minimization}
We consider minimizing a differentiable, possibly nonconvex function $f:\mathbb{R}^d\to\mathbb{R}$ with an $L$-Lipschitz gradient, where $d$ is the dimension and $L>0$. The function is strongly quasar-convex toward a global minimizer $x^\star$, with parameters $\mu>0$ and $0<\gamma\leq 1/256$. A given starting point $x^0\in\mathbb{R}^d$ satisfies $f(x^0)-f(x^\star)\leq\Delta$, where $\Delta\geq0$. The oracle returns $\nabla f(x)$ at $x$. For $\varepsilon>0$, the goal is an output $\bar{x}\in\mathbb{R}^d$ with integrable objective gap satisfying $\mathbb{E}_{P}[f(\bar{x})-f(x^\star)]\leq\varepsilon$, where $P$ is the law of the algorithm's randomness.

The optimization problem is
\[
\min_{x\in\mathbb{R}^d} f(x).
\]
We use the Euclidean inner product $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle$ and its induced norm $\|\cdot\|$. The algorithm receives $d$, $L$, $x^0$, $\mu$, $\gamma$, $\Delta$, and $\varepsilon$.

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Parameter and dimension regime]
The dimension $d$ is a nonnegative integer. In addition to the parameter ranges above, define $\kappa=L/\mu$ and require
\[
\begin{aligned}
870400\mu&\leq L,\\
4\mu&\leq\gamma^2 L,\\
1000\gamma\sqrt{\kappa}\,\varepsilon&\leq\Delta,\\
\left(\frac{\sqrt{\kappa}}{\gamma}\right)^5
\left(\frac{\Delta}{1000\gamma\sqrt{\kappa}\,\varepsilon}\right)^3
&\leq d.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Smoothness]
The function $f$ is Fr\'echet differentiable everywhere, and
\[
\|\nabla f(x)-\nabla f(y)\|\leq L\|x-y\|,
\qquad x,y\in\mathbb{R}^d.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Strong quasar convexity and initial gap]
There exists a global minimizer $x^\star\in\mathbb{R}^d$ satisfying the stated initial objective-gap bound such that, for every $x\in\mathbb{R}^d$,
\[
 f(x)-f(x^\star)
 \leq \frac{1}{\gamma}\langle\nabla f(x),x-x^\star\rangle
 -\frac{\mu}{2}\|x-x^\star\|^2.
\]
\end{assumption}

Queries may be made at any point of $\mathbb{R}^d$. After the allotted oracle calls, the output must have an integrable objective gap under $P$ and satisfy
\[
\mathbb{E}_{P}\bigl[f(\bar{x})-f(x^\star)\bigr]\leq\varepsilon.
\]
