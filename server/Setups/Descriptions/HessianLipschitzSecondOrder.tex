\section*{Hessian-Lipschitz Second-Order Stationarity}
We consider minimizing a possibly nonconvex, twice continuously differentiable function $f:E\to\mathbb{R}$ on a finite-dimensional real inner product space $E$, with induced norm $\|\cdot\|$. Its Hessian is $H$-Lipschitz for $H>0$, and a given point $x^0\in E$ satisfies $f(x^0)-\inf_{x\in E}f(x)\leq\Delta$ for $\Delta\geq0$. Two exact oracles return $(f(x),\nabla f(x))$ and $\nabla^2 f(x)$, respectively. For $\varepsilon>0$ and $\rho\in(0,1)$, the goal is an output $\bar{x}\in E$ satisfying both $\|\nabla f(\bar{x})\|\leq\varepsilon$ and $\langle v,\nabla^2 f(\bar{x})v\rangle\geq-\sqrt{H\varepsilon}\|v\|^2$ for every $v\in E$, with probability at least $1-\rho$ over the algorithm's randomness.

The optimization problem is
\[
\inf_{x\in E} f(x).
\]
The Hessian $\nabla^2 f(x):E\to E$ is a linear operator, and $\|\cdot\|_{\mathrm{op}}$ denotes the induced operator norm. The algorithm receives $E$, $x^0$, $H$, $\Delta$, $\varepsilon$, $\rho$, and an additional real parameter $m\geq1$; the setup imposes no further condition involving $m$.

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Hessian regularity]
The function $f$ is twice continuously Fr\'echet differentiable on $E$, and
\[
\|\nabla^2 f(x)-\nabla^2 f(y)\|_{\mathrm{op}}
\leq H\|x-y\|,
\qquad x,y\in E.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Initial objective gap]
The given point $x^0$ satisfies
\[
f(x^0)-f(x)\leq\Delta,
\qquad x\in E.
\]
\end{assumption}

Queries to either oracle may be made at any point of $E$. The first oracle returns the exact function value and gradient together; the second returns the exact Hessian. Calls to these two oracles are counted separately.

After the allotted calls, the output must satisfy
\[
P\!\left(
\left\{
\begin{aligned}
&\|\nabla f(\bar{x})\|\leq\varepsilon,\\
&\langle v,\nabla^2 f(\bar{x})v\rangle
\geq-\sqrt{H\varepsilon}\|v\|^2
\quad\text{for every }v\in E
\end{aligned}
\right\}
\right)\geq1-\rho,
\]
where $P$ is the law of the algorithm's randomness.
