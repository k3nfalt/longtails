\section*{Strongly Monotone Hidden-Potential Root Finding}
We seek a zero $x^\star$ of an operator $F:E\to E$ on a real Hilbert space $E$. The operator is $\mu$-strongly monotone, with $\mu>0$, and admits a hidden differentiable potential $\varphi:E\to\mathbb{R}$ whose gradient is $L$-Lipschitz; the residual $F-\nabla\varphi$ is $s$-Lipschitz, where $L,s\geq0$. A given point $x^0\in E$ satisfies $\|x^0-x^\star\|\leq R$, with $R\geq0$. Each oracle call at $x\in E$ returns exactly $F(x)$. Given $\varepsilon>0$, the output $\bar{x}\in E$ must satisfy $\|\bar{x}-x^\star\|\leq\varepsilon$ almost surely. The quantities $(E,x^0,\mu,L,s,R,\varepsilon)$ are public; the potential is hidden.

The root-finding problem is
\[
\text{find }x^\star\in E\quad\text{such that}\quad F(x^\star)=0,
\]
where $E$ may be infinite dimensional, with inner product $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle$ and induced norm $\|\cdot\|$. Admissible operators and oracle access satisfy the following conditions.

\begin{assumption}[Strong monotonicity]
For every $x,y\in E$,
\[
\langle F(x)-F(y),x-y\rangle
\geq \mu\|x-y\|^2.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Hidden potential and residual regularity]
There exists an everywhere Fr\'echet differentiable function $\varphi:E\to\mathbb{R}$ such that, for every $x,y\in E$,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\|\nabla\varphi(x)-\nabla\varphi(y)\|
&\leq L\|x-y\|,\\
\|(F(x)-\nabla\varphi(x))-(F(y)-\nabla\varphi(y))\|
&\leq s\|x-y\|.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Existence and initial distance]
There exists $x^\star\in E$ satisfying
\[
F(x^\star)=0,
\qquad \|x^0-x^\star\|\leq R.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Exact operator access]
Oracle $0$ returns $F(x)\in E$ at every query $x\in E$, at every call index and for every realization of its randomness. Each query costs one oracle call. Any internal algorithm randomness is independent of the oracle randomness.
\end{assumption}

The algorithm accesses $F$ through this oracle and may choose queries adaptively anywhere in $E$. Its selection may depend on the entire public tuple $(E,x^0,\mu,L,s,R,\varepsilon)$. Neither $\varphi$ nor its gradient is supplied separately. A call budget $K\in\mathbb{N}$ may depend on the same public tuple.

After at most $K$ oracle calls, the algorithm returns $\bar{x}\in E$. Writing $\mathbb{P}$ for the joint law of the algorithm and oracle randomness, success requires a zero $x^\star$ of $F$ such that
\[
\|\bar{x}-x^\star\|\leq\varepsilon
\qquad \mathbb{P}\text{-almost surely}.
\]
Strong monotonicity makes the zero unique, so the same $x^\star$ witnesses the initial distance bound and the accuracy requirement.
