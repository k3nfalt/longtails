\section*{Strongly Monotone Hidden-Potential Root Finding}
We seek a zero of a map $F:E\to E$ on a real Hilbert space $E$. The map is $\mu$-strongly monotone, where $\mu>0$, and admits a hidden differentiable potential $\varphi:E\to\mathbb{R}$ whose gradient is $L$-Lipschitz, with $L\geq0$, while $F-\nabla\varphi$ is $s$-Lipschitz, with $s\geq0$. A given point $x^0\in E$ lies within distance $R\geq0$ of a zero $x^\star$. The oracle returns $F(x)$ at $x$. For $\varepsilon>0$, the goal is an output $\bar{x}\in E$ satisfying $\|\bar{x}-x^\star\|\leq\varepsilon$ almost surely under $P$, the law of the algorithm's randomness.

The root-finding problem is
\[
\text{find }x^\star\in E\text{ such that }F(x^\star)=0.
\]
Write $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle$ for the inner product on $E$ and $\|\cdot\|$ for its induced norm. The algorithm receives $E$, $x^0$, $\mu$, $L$, $s$, $R$, and $\varepsilon$; the potential $\varphi$ is not supplied.

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Strong monotonicity]
For every $x,y\in E$,
\[
\langle F(x)-F(y),x-y\rangle
\geq \mu\|x-y\|^2.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Hidden potential]
There exists an everywhere Fr\'echet differentiable function $\varphi:E\to\mathbb{R}$ such that, for every $x,y\in E$,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\|\nabla\varphi(x)-\nabla\varphi(y)\|
&\leq L\|x-y\|,\\
\|(F-\nabla\varphi)(x)-(F-\nabla\varphi)(y)\|
&\leq s\|x-y\|.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Initial distance]
There exists $x^\star\in E$ satisfying
\[
F(x^\star)=0,
\qquad \|x^0-x^\star\|\leq R.
\]
\end{assumption}

Queries may be made at any point of $E$. Strong monotonicity makes the zero $x^\star$ unique. After the allotted oracle calls, the output must satisfy
\[
\|\bar{x}-x^\star\|\leq\varepsilon
\quad P\text{-a.s.}
\]
