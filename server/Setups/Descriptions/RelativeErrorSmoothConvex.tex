\section*{Convex Minimization with Relative Error}
We consider minimizing a differentiable convex function $f:E\to\mathbb{R}$ on a real Hilbert space $E$. The function admits a quadratic upper model with curvature $L>0$, and a given starting point $x^0\in E$ lies within distance $R\geq0$ of a global minimizer $x^\star$. At $x$, the oracle returns a vector $g$ satisfying $\|g-\nabla f(x)\|\leq\alpha\|\nabla f(x)\|$, where $0\leq\alpha<1$. For $\varepsilon>0$, the goal is an output $\bar{x}\in E$ satisfying $f(\bar{x})-f(x^\star)\leq\varepsilon$ almost surely under $P$, the joint law of algorithm and oracle randomness.

The optimization problem is
\[
\min_{x\in E} f(x).
\]
Write $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle$ for the inner product on $E$ and $\|\cdot\|$ for its induced norm. The algorithm receives $E$, $L$, $x^0$, $\alpha$, $R$, and $\varepsilon$.

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Convexity and smoothness]
The function $f$ is Fr\'echet differentiable everywhere on $E$. For every $x,y\in E$,
\[
\begin{aligned}
f(x)+\langle\nabla f(x),y-x\rangle
&\leq f(y),\\
f(y)&\leq f(x)+\langle\nabla f(x),y-x\rangle
+\frac{L}{2}\|y-x\|^2.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Initial distance]
There exists $x^\star\in E$ such that
\[
\begin{aligned}
f(x^\star)&\leq f(x) &&\text{for every }x\in E,\\
\|x^0-x^\star\|&\leq R.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Relative oracle error]
Queries may be made at any point of $E$. For every finite query--reply history $h$, every query $x\in E$, and every realization $s$ of the oracle randomness used for that reply, the returned vector $g(h,x,s)\in E$ satisfies
\[
\|g(h,x,s)-\nabla f(x)\|
\leq\alpha\|\nabla f(x)\|.
\]
Oracle randomness may be dependent across calls, and replies may depend on the history. The algorithm's randomness is independent of the oracle randomness.
\end{assumption}

After the allotted oracle calls, the output must satisfy
\[
f(\bar{x})-f(x^\star)\leq\varepsilon
\quad P\text{-a.s.}
\]
