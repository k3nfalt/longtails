\section*{Monotone Roots with Lipschitz Second Derivatives}
We consider finding a zero of a twice continuously differentiable monotone map $F:E\to E$ on a finite-dimensional real inner product space $E$. Its second derivative is $L$-Lipschitz, where $L>0$. A given point $x^0\in E$ satisfies $\|F'(x^0)\|_{\mathrm{op}}\leq H_0$, where $H_0\geq0$, and lies within distance $D>0$ of a zero $x^\star$. Here primes denote Fr\'echet derivatives and $\|\cdot\|_{\mathrm{op}}$ denotes the induced operator norm. Two oracles return $F(x)$ and $F'(x)$, respectively. For $\varepsilon>0$, the goal is an output $\bar{x}\in E$ with $\|F(\bar{x})\|\leq\varepsilon$ almost surely under $P$, the law of the algorithm's randomness.

The root-finding problem is to find $x\in E$ such that
\[
F(x)=0.
\]
The norm $\|\cdot\|$ is induced by the inner product on $E$. The derivative $F'(x)$ is a linear map from $E$ to $E$, and $F''(x)$ is a bilinear map from $E\times E$ to $E$; their norms are the induced operator norms. The algorithm receives $E$, $x^0$, $L$, $D$, $H_0$, and $\varepsilon$.

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Monotonicity]
For every $x,y\in E$,
\[
\langle F(x)-F(y),x-y\rangle\geq0.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Lipschitz second derivative]
The map $F$ is twice continuously Fr\'echet differentiable on $E$, and
\[
\|F''(x)-F''(y)\|_{\mathrm{op}}
\leq L\|x-y\|,
\qquad x,y\in E.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Initial bounds]
There exists $x^\star\in E$ such that
\[
\begin{aligned}
F(x^\star)&=0,\\
\|x^0-x^\star\|&\leq D,\\
\|F'(x^0)\|_{\mathrm{op}}&\leq H_0.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

Both oracles are exact and may be queried at any point of $E$. Calls to the value oracle and the derivative oracle are counted separately. After the allotted calls, the output must satisfy the residual criterion
\[
\|F(\bar{x})\|\leq\varepsilon
\quad P\text{-a.s.}
\]
