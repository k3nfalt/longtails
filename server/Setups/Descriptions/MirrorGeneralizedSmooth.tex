\section*{Generalized Smooth Convex Mirror Geometry}
We minimize a differentiable convex function $f:E\to\mathbb{R}$ on a finite-dimensional real normed space $E$. The function $f$ is $(L_0,L_1)$-smooth, with $L_0>0$ and $L_1\geq0$. A differentiable mirror function $\psi:E\to\mathbb{R}$ induces a Bregman divergence $B_\psi$ dominating half the squared norm. A given point $x^0\in E$ satisfies $B_\psi(x^\star,x^0)\leq D$ and $f(x^0)-f(x^\star)\leq\Delta$ for a minimizer $x^\star$, where $D,\Delta>0$. The oracle returns exact derivatives and Bregman proximal minimizers. For $\varepsilon>0$, the goal is $f(\bar{x})-f(x^\star)\leq\varepsilon$ almost surely under $P$, the law of the algorithm's randomness.

The optimization problem is
\[
\min_{x\in E} f(x).
\]
Write $E^*$ for the continuous dual of $E$. Derivatives $\nabla f(x)$ and $\nabla\psi(x)$ belong to $E^*$, and their norms are the induced operator norms $\|\cdot\|_{\mathrm{op}}$. Define
\[
B_\psi(u,z)=\psi(u)-\psi(z)-\nabla\psi(z)[u-z].
\]
The algorithm receives $E$, $L_0$, $L_1$, $D$, $\Delta$, and $x^0$; the tolerance $\varepsilon$ may enter only the oracle-call budget.

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Convexity and generalized smoothness]
The function $f$ is everywhere Fr\'echet differentiable, its derivative is continuous, and
\[
f((1-t)x+ty)\leq(1-t)f(x)+tf(y),
\qquad x,y\in E,\quad t\in[0,1].
\]
Its $(L_0,L_1)$-smoothness means that, for every $x,y\in E$ and $M\in\mathbb{R}$,
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\|\nabla f(x+\theta(y-x))\|_{\mathrm{op}}\leq M
\quad\text{for all }\theta\in[0,1]\\
&\qquad\Longrightarrow\quad
\|\nabla f(x)-\nabla f(y)\|_{\mathrm{op}}
\leq (L_0+L_1M)\|x-y\|.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Mirror geometry and proximal access]
The function $\psi$ is everywhere Fr\'echet differentiable and satisfies
\[
B_\psi(y,x)\geq\frac12\|y-x\|^2,
\qquad x,y\in E.
\]
For every $z\in E$ and $v\in E^*$, a selected proximal minimizer exists:
\[
\operatorname{prox}_\psi(z,v)\in
\mathop{\rm arg\,min}_{u\in E}
\bigl\{v[u]+B_\psi(u,z)\bigr\}.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Initial bounds]
There exists $x^\star\in E$ such that
\[
\begin{aligned}
f(x^\star)&\leq f(x) &&\text{for every }x\in E,\\
B_\psi(x^\star,x^0)&\leq D,\\
f(x^0)-f(x^\star)&\leq\Delta.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

The oracle returns $\nabla f(x)$ at $x$. It also returns $\operatorname{prox}_\psi(z,v)$ on a query $(z,v)\in E\times E^*$. Both types of queries are exact, may be made anywhere in their stated domains, and count toward a single total oracle-call budget.

After the allotted calls, the output $\bar{x}\in E$ must satisfy
\[
f(\bar{x})-f(x^\star)\leq\varepsilon
\quad P\text{-a.s.},
\]
where the global minimizer $x^\star$ is fixed independently of the algorithm's randomness.
