\section*{Stationarity with Gradient-Dependent Third Derivatives}
We consider a possibly nonconvex objective $f:E\to\mathbb{R}$ on a finite-dimensional real inner product space $E$. The function is three times continuously differentiable, with its third derivative bounded by $L_0+L_1\|\nabla f(x)\|$ at each $x\in E$, where $L_0>0$ and $L_1\geq0$. A given starting point $x^0\in E$ has objective gap at most $\Delta>0$ above the infimum and gradient norm at most $G_0\geq0$. Two exact oracles return the gradient and Hessian. For $\varepsilon>0$, the goal is an output $\bar{x}\in E$ satisfying $\|\nabla f(\bar{x})\|\leq\varepsilon$ almost surely under $P$, the law of the algorithm's randomness.

The underlying optimization problem is
\[
\inf_{x\in E} f(x).
\]
The norm $\|\cdot\|$ is induced by the inner product on $E$, and derivative norms $\|\cdot\|_{\mathrm{op}}$ are the induced operator norms. The algorithm receives $E$, $x^0$, $L_0$, $L_1$, $\Delta$, $G_0$, $\varepsilon$, and an integer $m\geq1$.

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Third-derivative bound]
The function $f$ belongs to $C^3(E;\mathbb{R})$ and satisfies
\[
\|\nabla^3 f(x)\|_{\mathrm{op}}
\leq L_0+L_1\|\nabla f(x)\|,
\qquad x\in E.
\]
Here $\nabla^3 f(x)$ denotes the trilinear third derivative of $f$, equivalently the second derivative of its gradient under the inner product identification.
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Initial bounds]
The given point $x^0$ satisfies
\[
\begin{aligned}
f(x^0)-f(x)&\leq\Delta &&\text{for every }x\in E,\\
\|\nabla f(x^0)\|&\leq G_0.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

Queries to either oracle may be made at any point of $E$. The gradient oracle returns $\nabla f(x)$ at $x$. The Hessian oracle returns $\nabla^2 f(x)$ at $x$, as a linear operator from $E$ to $E$. Gradient and Hessian calls are counted separately.

After the allotted calls to each oracle, the output must satisfy
\[
\|\nabla f(\bar{x})\|\leq\varepsilon
\quad P\text{-a.s.}
\]
