\section*{Cohypomonotone Operator Inclusions}
We seek a zero of $F+A$ on a real inner product space $E$, where $F:E\to E$ is $L$-Lipschitz and $A:E\rightrightarrows E$ is monotone. The sum is $\rho$-cohypomonotone, with $L>0$, $\rho\geq0$, and $\rho L<1$. A given point $x^0\in E$ is within distance $R\geq0$ of a zero. The oracle returns exact values of $F$ and resolvent evaluations of $A$. For $\varepsilon>0$, the goal is an output $\bar{x}\in E$ admitting $\xi\in A(\bar{x})$ with $\|F(\bar{x})+\xi\|\leq\varepsilon$, almost surely under the law of the algorithm's randomness.

The inclusion problem is to find $x\in E$ such that
\[
0\in F(x)+A(x),
\]
where $F(x)+A(x)=\{F(x)+a:a\in A(x)\}$. Write $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle$ for the inner product and $\|\cdot\|$ for its induced norm. The algorithm receives only $E$, $L$, $\rho$, and $x^0$; the radius $R$ and tolerance $\varepsilon$ may enter the oracle-call budget but are not algorithm inputs.

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Lipschitz continuity and monotonicity]
For every $x,y\in E$,
\[
\|F(x)-F(y)\|\leq L\|x-y\|.
\]
For every $x,y\in E$, $a\in A(x)$, and $b\in A(y)$,
\[
\langle a-b,x-y\rangle\geq0.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Cohypomonotonicity]
For every $x,y\in E$, $a\in A(x)$, and $b\in A(y)$, setting $u=F(x)+a$ and $v=F(y)+b$, we have
\[
\langle u-v,x-y\rangle\geq-\rho\|u-v\|^2.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Resolvent access]
There is a map $J:\mathbb{R}\times E\to E$, written $J_\gamma(z)$, such that, for every $\gamma>0$ and $z\in E$,
\[
\frac{z-J_\gamma(z)}{\gamma}\in A(J_\gamma(z)).
\]
The oracle returns $F(x)$ for a query $x\in E$, or $J_\gamma(z)$ for a query $(\gamma,z)\in\mathbb{R}\times E$. These replies are exact. For $\gamma\leq0$, the map $J_\gamma$ is unrestricted. Both query types count toward the same oracle-call budget.
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Initial distance]
There exists $x^\star\in E$ satisfying
\[
\begin{aligned}
-F(x^\star)&\in A(x^\star),\\
\|x^0-x^\star\|&\leq R.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

After the allotted oracle calls, the output must satisfy
\[
\exists\,\xi\in A(\bar{x})\quad
\|F(\bar{x})+\xi\|\leq\varepsilon
\quad P\text{-a.s.},
\]
where $P$ is the law of the algorithm's randomness. The required output is $\bar{x}$; the residual witness $\xi$ need not be returned.
