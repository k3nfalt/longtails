\section*{Uncalibrated Sharp Minimization}
We consider minimizing a Lipschitz function $f:E\to\mathbb{R}$ on a real inner product space $E$, with a global minimizer $x^\star$ and a given starting point $x^0\in E$. The objective grows at least linearly with distance from $x^\star$. The oracle returns a multiplicative estimate of the objective gap and a perturbed supporting subgradient, both multiplied by the same unknown positive scale. The perturbation bound is strictly smaller than the sharpness constant. For a tolerance $\varepsilon>0$, the goal is an output $\bar{x}\in E$ satisfying $f(\bar{x})-f(x^\star)\leq\varepsilon$ almost surely under $P$, the joint law of algorithm and oracle randomness.

The optimization problem is
\[
\min_{x\in E} f(x).
\]
Write $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle$ for the inner product and $\|\cdot\|$ for its induced norm. The parameters satisfy
\[
0\leq\rho<\mu\leq G,\qquad
0<\alpha\leq\beta,\qquad
R\geq0,\qquad \varepsilon>0.
\]
The algorithm receives only $E$, $\mu$, $\rho$, $\alpha$, $\beta$, and $x^0$; the constants $G$, $R$, and $\varepsilon$ may enter the oracle-call budget but are not algorithm inputs.

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Lipschitz continuity]
For every $x,y\in E$,
\[
|f(x)-f(y)|\leq G\|x-y\|.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Sharpness and initial distance]
There exists a global minimizer $x^\star\in E$ such that
\[
\begin{aligned}
f(x^\star)&\leq f(x) &&\text{for every }x\in E,\\
\|x^0-x^\star\|&\leq R,\\
f(x)-f(x^\star)&\geq\mu\|x-x^\star\|
&&\text{for every }x\in E.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Uncalibrated oracle]
Queries may be made at any $x\in E$. For every query history and every realization of the oracle randomness, the reply $(s,v)\in\mathbb{R}\times E$ admits a representation
\[
\begin{aligned}
s&=\gamma r, & v&=\gamma(g+e), & \gamma&>0,
\end{aligned}
\]
where $r\in\mathbb{R}$ and $g,e\in E$ satisfy
\[
\begin{aligned}
\|g\|&\leq G, & \|e\|&\leq\rho,\\
f(x)+\langle g,y-x\rangle&\leq f(y)
&&\text{for every }y\in E,\\
\alpha\bigl(f(x)-f(x^\star)\bigr)&\leq r
\leq\beta\bigl(f(x)-f(x^\star)\bigr).
\end{aligned}
\]
The scale $\gamma$ and the quantities $r$, $g$, and $e$ may depend on the query, its history, and the oracle randomness. Only $(s,v)$ is returned. The algorithm's randomness is independent of the oracle randomness; oracle replies may be dependent across calls.
\end{assumption}

After the allotted oracle calls, the output must satisfy
\[
f(\bar{x})-f(x^\star)\leq\varepsilon
\quad P\text{-a.s.}
\]
