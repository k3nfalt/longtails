\section*{Convex Optimization with Constant Momentum}
We consider minimizing a convex function $f:E\to\mathbb{R}$ on a separable real Hilbert space $E$. An initial point $x^0\in E$ is within distance $R\geq0$ of a global minimizer $x^\star$. Each oracle call returns $g(\xi,x)\in E$ at query $x$, using a fresh independent sample $\xi$ from a probability law $p$ on a measurable space $\Xi$. The jointly measurable oracle has mean in the subdifferential of $f$ and second moment at most $G^2$, where $G>0$. With fixed momentum $\beta\in[0,1)$, the required final iterate $\bar{x}$ satisfies $\int(f(\bar{x})-f(x^\star))\,d\mathbb{P}\leq\varepsilon$, where $\varepsilon>0$ and $\mathbb{P}$ is the joint randomness law.

The optimization problem is
\[
\min_{x\in E} f(x),
\]
where the norm on $E$ is induced by its inner product. The public inputs are $(E,\Xi,G,R,\beta,\varepsilon,x^0)$, including the measurable structure on $\Xi$; the objective $f$, sampling law $p$, and oracle map $g$ belong to the unknown instance. Their admissibility is specified below.

\begin{assumption}[Convexity]
For every $x,y\in E$ and $t\in[0,1]$,
\[
f((1-t)x+ty)\leq(1-t)f(x)+tf(y).
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Attainment and initial distance]
There exists $x^\star\in E$ such that
\begin{align*}
f(x^\star)&\leq f(x) \qquad\text{for every }x\in E,\\
\|x^0-x^\star\|&\leq R.
\end{align*}
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Stochastic subgradient access]
The law $p$ is a probability measure on $\Xi$, and $g:\Xi\times E\to E$ is jointly measurable, using the Borel measurable structure on $E$. For every $x\in E$, the scalar function $\xi\mapsto\|g(\xi,x)\|^2$ is integrable and
\[
\int_{\Xi}\|g(\xi,x)\|^2\,p(d\xi)\leq G^2.
\]
Consequently, $g(\cdot,x)$ is Bochner integrable. Its mean
\[
h(x)=\int_{\Xi}g(\xi,x)\,p(d\xi)
\]
satisfies, for every $x,y\in E$,
\[
f(x)+\langle h(x),y-x\rangle\leq f(y).
\]
At call $k\geq0$, a query $x\in E$ returns $g(\xi_k,x)$ and costs one oracle call. The samples $(\xi_k)_{k\geq0}$ are independent with common law $p$, and independent of any internal algorithm randomness.
\end{assumption}

The allowed algorithms are restricted to constant-momentum stochastic gradient iterations. Using the public inputs, the algorithm selects $\gamma\in\mathbb{R}$ and $N\in\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$. Starting from $m^0=0$, it queries $x^k$ and updates
\begin{align*}
\eta_k&=\gamma\left(N+1-k+\frac{\beta}{1-\beta}\right),\\
m^{k+1}&=\beta m^k+(1-\beta)g(\xi_k,x^k),\\
x^{k+1}&=x^k-\eta_k m^{k+1}.
\end{align*}
Queries range over all of $E$. An externally specified budget $K\in\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$ may depend on the full public parameter tuple. Each iteration uses exactly one oracle call, and the output after $K$ calls is the final iterate $\bar{x}=x^K$.

Writing $\mathbb{P}$ for the joint law of the algorithm and oracle randomness, success requires that the objective gap be integrable and that
\[
\int\bigl(f(\bar{x}(\omega))-f(x^\star)\bigr)\,\mathbb{P}(d\omega)
\leq\varepsilon.
\]
Here $x^\star$ is a global minimizer; the minimizer witnessing the initial distance bound can be used because all global minimizers have the same objective value.
