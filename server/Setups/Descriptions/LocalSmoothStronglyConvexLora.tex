\section*{Locally Initialized LoRA Gradient Descent}
We consider minimizing a differentiable, strongly convex function $f:\mathbb{R}^{m\times n}\to\mathbb{R}$ with Lipschitz gradient through LoRA factors $x=(A,B)\in\mathbb{R}^{m\times r}\times\mathbb{R}^{r\times n}$, where $m,n,r$ are positive integers and $r<\min\{m,n\}$. A global minimizer $X^\star$ admits full-rank factors with controlled conditioning, and the given starting factors lie in a prescribed neighborhood of those factors. The oracle returns $\nabla f(X)$ at $X$. For $\varepsilon>0$, the goal is final factors $\bar{x}=(\bar{A},\bar{B})$ satisfying $f(\bar{A}\bar{B})-f(X^\star)\leq\varepsilon$ almost surely under $P$, the law of the algorithm's randomness.

Write $E=\mathbb{R}^{m\times n}$ and $\mathcal{F}=\mathbb{R}^{m\times r}\times\mathbb{R}^{r\times n}$. We study
\[
\min_{(A,B)\in\mathcal{F}} f(AB).
\]
Matrix spaces carry the Frobenius inner product and norm. The factor space carries the product norm
\[
\|(A,B)\|^2=\|A\|_F^2+\|B\|_F^2.
\]
Vector norms are Euclidean, and $\|\cdot\|_{\mathrm{op}}$ denotes the induced operator norm. The algorithm receives $m,n,r$, the parameters $\mu,\rho,s,R,\varepsilon$, and the starting factors $x^0=(A^0,B^0)$, with
\[
0<\mu\leq\rho,\qquad 0<s\leq R,\qquad \varepsilon>0.
\]

Our assumptions:
\begin{assumption}[Strong convexity and smoothness]
The function $f$ is differentiable everywhere on $E$. For all $X,Y\in E$,
\[
\begin{aligned}
f(Y)&\geq f(X)+\langle\nabla f(X),Y-X\rangle_F
             +\frac{\mu}{2}\|Y-X\|_F^2,\\
\|\nabla f(X)-\nabla f(Y)\|_F&\leq\rho\|X-Y\|_F.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Minimizing factors and local initialization]
There exist factors $x^\star=(A^\star,B^\star)\in\mathcal{F}$ such that $X^\star=A^\star B^\star$ is a global minimizer of $f$ on $E$:
\[
f(X^\star)\leq f(X)\qquad\text{for every }X\in E.
\]
Define the linear map $\mathcal{J}_\star:\mathcal{F}\to E$ by
\[
\mathcal{J}_\star(H_A,H_B)=H_A B^\star+A^\star H_B.
\]
These same factors satisfy
\[
\begin{aligned}
\|\mathcal{J}_\star\|_{\mathrm{op}}&\leq R,\\
\|A^\star w\|&\geq s\|w\| &&\text{for every }w\in\mathbb{R}^r,\\
\|(B^\star)^\top w\|&\geq s\|w\| &&\text{for every }w\in\mathbb{R}^r,\\
\|x^0-x^\star\|&\leq\frac{\mu s^2}{256\rho R}.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

Our result is algorithm-specific: we analyze LoRA gradient descent with simultaneous updates of both factors. Starting from the given $x^0$, each iteration queries the product $A^kB^k$ and uses the returned matrix $G^k$ in
\[
\begin{aligned}
G^k&=\nabla f(A^kB^k),\\
A^{k+1}&=A^k-\eta_k G^k(B^k)^\top,\\
B^{k+1}&=B^k-\eta_k(A^k)^\top G^k.
\end{aligned}
\]
The scalar step size $\eta_k$ must be strictly positive for every history and every oracle reply; it may depend on the algorithm's randomness, its accumulated information, and the current reply. After $K\geq0$ oracle calls, the output is the final factor pair $\bar{x}=x^K$, and it must satisfy
\[
f(\bar{A}\bar{B})-f(X^\star)\leq\varepsilon
\quad P\text{-a.s.}
\]
