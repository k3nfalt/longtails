\section*{Gap-Dependent Convex Stochastic Optimization}
We consider minimizing a differentiable convex function $f:E\to\mathbb{R}$ on a separable real Hilbert space $E$. Around each point, a local quadratic upper model has coefficient $H_0+H_1(f(x)-f(x^\star))$, where $H_0,H_1\geq0$ and $x^\star$ is a global minimizer. The initial point $x^0\in E$ satisfies $\|x^0-x^\star\|\leq R$ and $f(x^0)-f(x^\star)\leq\Delta$, with $R,\Delta>0$. Each call returns an unbiased stochastic gradient with independent sampling and variance bounded by $(\rho-1)\|\nabla f(x)\|^2$, where $\rho\geq1$ and $\nabla f$ is the gradient of $f$. For $0<\varepsilon\leq\Delta$ and $0<\delta<1$, the required measurable output $\bar{x}\in E$ satisfies $\mathbb{P}(f(\bar{x})-f(x^\star)>\varepsilon)\leq\delta$, where $\mathbb{P}$ is the joint law of algorithm and oracle randomness.

The optimization problem is
\[
\min_{x\in E} f(x),
\]
where the norm on $E$ is induced by its inner product and $E$ carries its Borel sigma-algebra. The public inputs are $E$, a measurable sample space $\Omega$, the initial point $x^0$, and the parameters
\[
\begin{aligned}
&H_0,H_1\geq0,\qquad R,\Delta>0,\qquad \rho\geq1,\\
&0<\varepsilon\leq\Delta,\qquad 0<\delta<1.
\end{aligned}
\]
The admissible objective and oracle satisfy the following conditions.

\begin{assumption}[Convexity and initialization]
The function $f$ is differentiable everywhere on $E$. For every $x,y\in E$ and $t\in[0,1]$,
\[
f((1-t)x+ty)\leq(1-t)f(x)+tf(y).
\]
There exists a global minimizer $x^\star\in E$ such that
\[
\begin{aligned}
f(x^\star)&\leq f(x)\qquad\text{for every }x\in E,\\
\|x^0-x^\star\|&\leq R,\\
f(x^0)-f(x^\star)&\leq\Delta.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Gap-dependent upper model]
For every $x,y\in E$ satisfying
\[
(2\sqrt{3}+\sqrt{6})\sqrt{H_1}\,\|y-x\|\leq1,
\]
the objective obeys
\[
\begin{aligned}
f(y)\leq{}&f(x)+\langle\nabla f(x),y-x\rangle\\
&+\bigl(H_0+H_1(f(x)-f(x^\star))\bigr)\|y-x\|^2.
\end{aligned}
\]
Here $x^\star$ is the minimizer witnessing the initialization bounds.
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Strong-growth gradient oracle]
There exist a probability measure $p$ on $\Omega$ and a jointly measurable map $g:E\times\Omega\to E$. For every $x\in E$, the map $g(x,\cdot)$ is Bochner integrable and the function $\omega\mapsto\|g(x,\omega)-\nabla f(x)\|^2$ is integrable with respect to $p$. They satisfy
\[
\begin{aligned}
\int_\Omega g(x,\omega)\,dp(\omega)&=\nabla f(x),\\
\int_\Omega\|g(x,\omega)-\nabla f(x)\|^2\,dp(\omega)
&\leq(\rho-1)\|\nabla f(x)\|^2.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Independent sampling and access]
The oracle uses a probability law $P$ on $\Omega^{\mathbb{N}}$. The coordinate samples $\xi_k(\xi)=\xi(k)$, for $k\in\mathbb{N}$, are measurable and mutually independent under $P$, and each has law $p$. At call $k$, a query $x\in E$ receives the vector $g(x,\xi_k)\in E$ and costs one oracle call. Queries may be adaptive and may lie anywhere in $E$. Internal algorithm randomness is independent of the entire oracle sample sequence.
\end{assumption}

The objective is accessed through this oracle. The algorithm is selected using only the public inputs; the objective $f$, minimizer $x^\star$, oracle map $g$, and probability laws $p$ and $P$ are not supplied as public inputs. An externally specified call budget $K\in\mathbb{N}$ may depend on the full public parameter tuple.

After $K$ calls, the algorithm returns an $E$-valued measurable random point $\bar{x}$. Under the joint law $\mathbb{P}$ of its independent internal randomness and the oracle samples, success requires
\[
\int \mathbf{1}_{\{f(\bar{x})-f(x^\star)>\varepsilon\}}\,d\mathbb{P}
\leq\delta.
\]
The criterion concerns the objective gap of the returned point relative to a global minimizer. Since all global minimizers have the same objective value, the minimizer witnessing the initialization bounds can be used here.
