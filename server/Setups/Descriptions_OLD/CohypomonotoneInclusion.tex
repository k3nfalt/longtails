\section*{Cohypomonotone Inclusion with Resolvent Access}
We seek $x\in E$ satisfying $0\in F(x)+A(x)$ in a real inner product space $E$, where $F:E\to E$ is $L$-Lipschitz, $A:E\rightrightarrows E$ is monotone, and $F+A$ is $\rho$-cohypomonotone, with $L>0$, $\rho\geq0$, and $\rho L<1$. The initial point $x^0\in E$ is within distance $R\geq0$ of a solution $x^\star$. Each call returns either $F(x)$ or $J_\gamma(z)$ exactly, where $J_\gamma$ is a resolvent of $A$ for $\gamma>0$. For $\varepsilon>0$, the output $\bar{x}\in E$ must almost surely admit $\xi\in A(\bar{x})$ with $\|F(\bar{x})+\xi\|\leq\varepsilon$. Only $(E,L,\rho,x^0)$ are public inputs.

The inclusion problem is
\[
\text{find }x\in E\text{ such that }0\in F(x)+A(x),
\]
where $F(x)+A(x)=\{F(x)+a:a\in A(x)\}$. The space $E$ may be infinite dimensional, and its norm is induced by its inner product; completeness is not required. The admissible operators and oracle satisfy the following conditions.

\begin{assumption}[Lipschitz continuity and monotonicity]
For every $x,y\in E$,
\[
\|F(x)-F(y)\|\leq L\|x-y\|.
\]
For every $a\in A(x)$ and $b\in A(y)$,
\[
\langle a-b,x-y\rangle\geq0.
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Cohypomonotonicity]
For every $x,y\in E$, $a\in A(x)$, and $b\in A(y)$, the sum operator satisfies
\[
\begin{aligned}
\langle (F(x)+a)-(F(y)+b),x-y\rangle
&\geq -\rho\|(F(x)+a)-(F(y)+b)\|^2.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Solution and initial distance]
There exists $x^\star\in E$ such that
\[
\begin{aligned}
-F(x^\star)&\in A(x^\star),\\
\|x^0-x^\star\|&\leq R.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Exact operator and resolvent access]
There exists a map $J:\mathbb{R}\times E\to E$, written $J_\gamma(z)=J(\gamma,z)$, such that, for every $z\in E$ and $\gamma>0$,
\[
\gamma^{-1}\bigl(z-J_\gamma(z)\bigr)\in A\bigl(J_\gamma(z)\bigr).
\]
The oracle accepts either an operator query $x\in E$ or a resolvent query $(\gamma,z)\in\mathbb{R}\times E$, and returns $F(x)$ or $J_\gamma(z)$, respectively. These replies hold at every call index and for every realization of oracle randomness. Each query of either type costs one oracle call. Resolvent queries with $\gamma\leq0$ are permitted, but the displayed inclusion constrains only positive $\gamma$. Any internal algorithm randomness is independent of oracle randomness.
\end{assumption}

Queries may be chosen adaptively anywhere in their respective domains. The algorithm is selected using only $(E,L,\rho,x^0)$; the distance bound $R$ and tolerance $\varepsilon$ are not supplied to it. An externally specified call budget $K\in\mathbb{N}$ may depend on the full parameter tuple $(E,L,\rho,x^0,R,\varepsilon)$ and counts both query types.

After $K$ calls, the algorithm returns $\bar{x}\in E$. Writing $\mathbb{P}$ for the joint law of algorithm and oracle randomness, the required accuracy is
\[
\exists\,\xi\in A(\bar{x})\quad
\|F(\bar{x})+\xi\|\leq\varepsilon
\qquad \mathbb{P}\text{-almost surely}.
\]
The witness $\xi$ may depend on the realized output and is not itself required as an algorithm output.
