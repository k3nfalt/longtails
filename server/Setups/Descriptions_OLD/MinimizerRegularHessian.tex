\section*{Strongly Convex Minimizer-Regular Optimization}
We consider minimizing a differentiable function $f:E\to\mathbb{R}$ on a nonzero finite-dimensional real Hilbert space $E$. Its gradient is $L$-Lipschitz, $f$ is $\mu$-strongly convex with $0<\mu\leq L$, and its Hessian varies at most linearly with rate $M\geq0$ relative to a global minimizer $x^\star$. A given point $x^0$ satisfies $\|x^0-x^\star\|\leq R$ for $R>0$. Each oracle call at $x\in E$ returns the exact gradient $\nabla f(x)$. For $\varepsilon>0$, the required output $\bar{x}\in E$ satisfies $f(\bar{x})-f(x^\star)\leq\varepsilon$ almost surely.

The optimization problem is
\begin{equation*}
\min_{x\in E} f(x),
\end{equation*}
where the norm is induced by the inner product on $E$. The function and oracle obey the following conditions.

\begin{assumption}[Curvature and gradient smoothness]
The function $f$ and its gradient are differentiable everywhere. For every $x,y\in E$,
\begin{align*}
f(y) &\geq f(x)+\langle\nabla f(x),y-x\rangle
       +\frac{\mu}{2}\|y-x\|^2,\\
\|\nabla f(x)-\nabla f(y)\| &\leq L\|x-y\|.
\end{align*}
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Minimizer and Hessian regularity]
There exists $x^\star\in E$ such that, for every $x\in E$,
\begin{align*}
f(x^\star) &\leq f(x),\\
\|x^0-x^\star\| &\leq R,\\
\|\mathrm{D}(\nabla f)(x)-\mathrm{D}(\nabla f)(x^\star)\|_{\mathrm{op}}
&\leq M\|x-x^\star\|.
\end{align*}
Here $\mathrm{D}(\nabla f)(x)$ is the derivative of the gradient, equipped with the operator norm.
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Exact-gradient oracle]
At every call index and query point $x\in E$, the oracle returns $\nabla f(x)\in E$ for every realization of its randomness. Each query costs one oracle call. Internal algorithm randomness is independent of oracle randomness.
\end{assumption}

Queries may be chosen adaptively anywhere in $E$. All setup parameters $(E,L,x^0,\mu,M,R,\varepsilon)$ are available to the algorithm, while $f$ is accessed through the oracle. An externally specified budget $K\in\mathbb{N}$ may depend on these parameters.

After $K$ oracle calls, the algorithm returns $\bar{x}\in E$. Writing $\mathbb{P}$ for the joint law of its internal randomness and the oracle randomness, success requires a global minimizer $x^\star$ for which
\begin{equation*}
f(\bar{x})-f(x^\star)\leq\varepsilon
\qquad \mathbb{P}\text{-almost surely}.
\end{equation*}
