This result removes the exponential dimension requirement from randomized initialization hardness for convex generalized-smooth optimization. The oracle-complexity rate is already known. The new contribution is exact localization preserving gradient-dependent curvature, and the resulting lower bound in polynomial dimension.

The [main theorem](PolynomialDimensionGeneralizedSmoothLowerBound.lean) uses [GeneralizedSmoothConvexPolynomialDimension](../Setups/GeneralizedSmoothConvexPolynomialDimension.lean). Write q = L₁R. For positive parameters with q ≥ 2²⁰, L₀/L₁² ≤ ε ≤ L₀R²/16, and d ≥ ⌈2⁴⁴(1+q)⁵⌉, every randomized adaptive gradient-oracle algorithm fails on some admissible instance at every integer budget

**0 ≤ b ≤ ⌊q / (4096 log(2+q))⌋.**

Success requires an integrable objective gap with expectation at most ε for the actual execution output. The hard instance may depend on the algorithm and budget, but not its realized seed. Its oracle is deterministic. The lower includes zero queries, unrestricted query locations and outputs, and arbitrary algorithm randomization. All parameters, including R and ε, are public. No update-family, span, or output-selection restriction is imposed.

The setup reuses exactly the admissible objective and oracle conditions of [RadiusOnlyGeneralizedSmoothConvex](../Setups/RadiusOnlyGeneralizedSmoothConvex.lean), specialized to Euclidean space: convex f, differentiable gradient, curvature bound ‖D∇f(x)‖ ≤ L₀ + L₁‖∇f(x)‖, and a minimizer within distance R of the start. There is no bound on the initial gap, gradient magnitude, or query radius. The numerical conditions select the initialization-dominated accuracy regime and sufficient dimension; they do not restrict the objective class further. The [supporting lower proof](../Proofs/PolynomialDimensionGeneralizedSmoothLowerBound.lean) proves that this parameter regime is nonempty.

The existing [RadiusFreeSherpa algorithm](../Algorithms/RadiusFreeSherpa.lean), returning its accepted accelerated state output, achieves the same expected criterion within

**⌈64√(L₀R²/ε) + 32768(1+q)log(2+q)⌉ gradient calls.**

[RadiusFreeSherpaExpected.expected_success](../Proofs/RadiusFreeSherpaExpected.lean) connects this guarantee to actual execution, counting every trial query. Uniqueness of exact gradients makes the output seed-independent, so its gap is constant and integrable. This transfers the known [upper theorem](RadiusFreeGeneralizedAccelerated.lean), not a new upper-complexity claim. Since √(L₀R²/ε) ≤ q, the bounds match up to two logarithmic factors throughout the specified interval. No fixed-confidence conversion is used.

The closest prior randomized result is [PR #166](https://github.com/k3nfalt/longtails/pull/166), research commit 0ed1e4a644756cf188d8f32e323bf2b8cb1f8ebd. It has the same lower budget, accuracy interval, gradient oracle, and expected criterion, but requires d ≥ ⌈2²⁰(1+q)⁸ exp(2√q)⌉. Its coupled conjugate chain, compact convolution, coordinate-prefix construction, and finite-family expectation argument are reused supporting work, not new contributions. The present result replaces its localization and dimension accounting, retaining the full nonlinear family and accuracy interval. This is a scope improvement, not a faster q-rate or an optimal dimension bound.

The mechanism is proved in [AdaptivePolynomialHinge](../Proofs/AdaptivePolynomialHinge.lean), [AdaptivePolynomialLocalization](../Proofs/AdaptivePolynomialLocalization.lean), and [AdaptiveGeneralizedSmoothLocalization](../Proofs/AdaptiveGeneralizedSmoothLocalization.lean). For n ≥ 2, define pₙ(t) = tⁿ(n+1−nt) on [0,1], extended by zero and one outside. Its derivative satisfies

**0 ≤ pₙ′ ≤ 2n(n+1)(pₙ + 2⁻ⁿ).**

Its compact primitive ψ_W has derivative pₙ(s/W), is zero for s ≤ 0, and equals s−Wn/(n+2) for s ≥ W. Both derivatives match at the endpoints. Given a normalized convex seed f ≥ 0, minimizer radius r, gradient cap G, and curvature L₀/3 + (L₁/3)‖∇f‖, form w(x) = a(‖x‖²−B²)/2 and F = f + ψ_W(w−f), with W = aB². The proved sufficient conditions are B ≥ 2r, L₁B ≥ 256n(n+1), a = G/B + L₀/1000, and G2⁻ⁿL₁ ≤ L₀. They imply convexity, the original target curvature bound, preservation of the inner objective and minimizer, F ≥ f, and exactly ∇F(x) = ax outside radius 4B. The exterior objective is w−Wn/(n+2), not w. Far-away returned outputs are also proved inaccurate.

The transition lies between B and 4B. Convexity and the minimizer radius prevent cancellation between seed and radial gradients: their weighted norm sum is at most twice the blended gradient norm. The transition curvature splits into a weight-proportional term absorbed into L₁‖∇F‖ and an exponentially small remainder absorbed into L₀. The bound n(n+1)2⁻ⁿ ≤ 3/2 controls that remainder. These are derived facts, not public restrictions or assumed curvature conclusions.

For chain length m = b+1, G = (εL₁/2)exp((m−1)/√m). The [explicit tuning](../Proofs/AdaptiveTransientParameters.lean) chooses n = ⌈16√q⌉ and B = 2¹⁷R, proving the sufficient conditions above. The exterior radius is 2¹⁹R; the prefix threshold stays 1/(4L₁√m). Thus the hiding requirement is at most m + 2⁴³q²m³ ≤ q + 2⁴³q⁵ ≤ 2⁴⁴(1+q)⁵. The parameterized localization estimate also explains logarithmic-degree tuning in the peak gradient; the Lean main result uses the explicit √q degree and does not export a separately optimized logarithmic-degree theorem.

The [localized chain](../Proofs/AdaptiveGeneralizedSmoothLocalizedChain.lean) derives all seed properties from the inherited nonlinear conjugate construction. Its regularizer couples total mass with coordinate proportions, so it is neither quadratic nor separable. Compact convolution preserves exact prefix identities. The [partial oracle](../Proofs/AdaptiveGeneralizedSmoothPartialOracle.lean) keeps the full-chain normalization, degree, wall, and width common to every truncated transcript. The [hard oracle](../Proofs/AdaptiveGeneralizedSmoothHardOracle.lean) and finite-family proof count both queries and the final output, integrate before selecting the witness, and establish the required NonConvergence quantifiers.

The exact prior PR #166 construction is the strongest directly applicable comparison regardless of publication status.

Standard reductions do not yield the new dimension guarantee. The prior fixed-degree smooth maximum adds curvature proportional to a, forcing a = O(L₀) and a radius proportional to the exponentially large peak gradient. Uniform-Hessian gluing has the same obstruction. Bounded-query random embeddings do not handle unrestricted probes on their own. A sigmoid or log-sum-exp blend never exactly removes hidden dependence; approximate equality is insufficient against an exact oracle. Conversely, a compact weight starting at zero cannot satisfy p′ ≤ Cp alone without vanishing identically. The exponentially small additive term resolves this conflict. Rescaling the old theorem preserves q and cannot eliminate its exponential dimension requirement.

Limitations: the lower q-rate is unchanged, the polynomial dimension and constants have slack, and the small-accuracy regime is excluded. No randomized value-and-gradient or expected-stopping-cost lower bound is claimed. Bounded gradients and normalization are proved internally for the construction and are not imposed on admissible objectives. No literal Includes claim equates expected and almost-sure success: its arbitrary output maps need not be measurable, so almost-sure bounded gap alone does not establish integrability. The direct Sherpa execution proof supplies the upper comparison without that invalid inference.
