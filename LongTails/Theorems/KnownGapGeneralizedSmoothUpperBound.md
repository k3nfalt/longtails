This theorem is the upper half of the pair for the known-gap class; it is a transfer of two existing repository results, not a new upper-complexity claim.

The [setup](../Setups/GeneralizedSmoothConvexKnownGapPolynomialDimension.lean) is the Euclidean convex (L₀,L₁)-smooth class with exact gradient oracle, known radius R and known initial gap Δ (Everest's 𝒫_Δ), with the expected-gap success criterion; its valid regime is L₀, L₁, R, ε > 0, 16ε ≤ L₀R², L₁R ≥ 2²⁰, Δ ≥ ε(2 + L₁R)², Δ ≥ ε√(L₀R²/ε), d ≥ ⌈2⁴⁶(1 + L₁R + √(L₀R²/ε))⁶⌉. The regime is chosen for the lower bound; the transferred upper bounds use only positivity of the parameters and Δ > 0, which the gap conditions imply. The guarantee is that the named deterministic output has objective gap at most ε against the minimizer x* of the setup, hence expected gap at most ε under every seed law. Further details of the setup are in the [companion lower-bound note](KnownGapGeneralizedSmoothLowerBound.md). The [main theorem](KnownGapGeneralizedSmoothUpperBound.lean) proves `Convergence` with the budget

**min{ 2⌈4096(1 + √(L₀R²/ε) + ⌈(L₁R)^{2/3}⌉ log(4 + 4Δ/ε))⌉, ⌈64√(L₀R²/ε) + 32768(1 + L₁R) log(2 + L₁R)⌉ }**

gradient calls. The first formula is the budget of [GeneralizedAcceleratedConvex](GeneralizedAcceleratedConvex.lean) (Everest's Algorithm 4 with positive initial weight a₀ = R²/(2Δ), formalized as [DampedGeneralizedAccelerated](../Algorithms/DampedGeneralizedAccelerated.lean)); the second is the budget of [RadiusFreeGeneralizedAccelerated](RadiusFreeGeneralizedAccelerated.lean) ([RadiusFreeSherpa](../Algorithms/RadiusFreeSherpa.lean)), which ignores Δ. The [algorithm](../Algorithms/KnownGapSelection.lean) runs whichever of the two has the smaller public budget; it is built as a sum-typed combination so that its seed type stays trivial, and [KnownGapUpperTransfer](../Proofs/KnownGapUpperTransfer.lean) shows that its execution coincides with the execution of the selected method.

Because both methods are deterministic and admissible oracles return the exact gradient (unique by `HasGradientAt.unique`), the execution output does not depend on the algorithm or oracle seeds; its objective gap is a constant, hence integrable, and the expected-gap criterion follows from the pointwise guarantees `accelerated_gap` and `sherpa_gap`, which replay the two existing proofs through [run_accuracy](../Proofs/GeneralizedConvergence.lean) and the radius-free run analysis.

Compared with Everest's Theorem B.7(b), the first formula has log(4 + 4Δ/ε) where Everest has log^{2/3}(2Δ/ε) (their Lemma B.6 Hölder counting is not formalized in the repository); compared with their Theorem 3.7 the second formula is the same up to constants. The minimum is at most a polylog(2 + L₀R²/ε + L₁R)·log^{1/3}(Δ/ε) factor above the lower bound in the interpolation regime and a polylog factor above it in the saturated regime, as detailed in the lower-bound note. No claim beyond the two transferred guarantees is made.
