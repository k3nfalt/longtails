\caption{Accelerated composite optimization under generalized smoothness: \href{https://github.com/k3nfalt/longtails/blob/main/LongTails/Setups/CompositeGeneralizedSmooth.lean}{CompositeGeneralizedSmooth}.}\label{tab:CompositeGeneralizedSmooth}
\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize
Minimize $F=f+\psi$ on a convex set $Q$ in a real Hilbert space. The function $f:E\to\mathbb R$ is convex with continuous gradient and satisfies
$\|\nabla f(x)-\nabla f(y)\|\le (L_0+L_1\max_{t\in[0,1]}\|\nabla f(x+t(y-x))\|)\|x-y\|$.
The function $\psi:E\to\mathbb R$ is convex on $Q$, and exact proximal minimizers over $Q$ exist for every positive step and every center and linear term.
For $x_0\in Q$, some minimizer $x_\star\in Q$ satisfies $\|x_0-x_\star\|\le R$, $F(x_0)-F(x_\star)\le\Delta$, and $\|\nabla f(x_\star)\|\le G$, with $L_0,R,\Delta,\varepsilon>0$ and $L_1,G\ge0$.
Target: a feasible output with $F(x)-F(x_\star)\le\varepsilon$, deterministically.
