\caption{Smooth quasar-convex optimization with amortized LMO errors: \href{https://github.com/k3nfalt/longtails/blob/main/LongTails/Setups/QuasarAmortizedLMO.lean}{QuasarAmortizedLMO}.}\label{tab:QuasarAmortizedLMO}
\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On a real Hilbert space, $f$ is differentiable with globally $L$-Lipschitz gradient. The convex set $C$ contains $x_0$, has diameter at most $D$, and admits a minimizer $x_\star$ satisfying $\tau(f(x)-f(x_\star))\le\langle\nabla f(x),x-x_\star\rangle$ for every $x\in C$. Here $L,D,\Gamma\ge0$, $0<\tau\le1$, and $\varepsilon>0$. Gradients are exact; the LMO returns $v_k\in C$ with $\langle v,v_k\rangle\le\langle v,u\rangle+\delta_k$ for every direction $v$, $u\in C$, and seed, where $\delta_k\ge0$ and $\sum_{k<n}\delta_k\le\Gamma$ for every $n$. The target is a feasible output with $f(x_K)-f(x_\star)\le\varepsilon$ almost surely under the execution measure $P$. The constructed Frank--Wolfe method uses $\eta_k=2/(\tau k+2)$; the setup does not restrict the algorithm.}\par\smallskip
