\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize Convex differentiable $f:E\to\mathbb R$ on a second-countable real Hilbert space with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra; $\nabla f$ is $L$-Lipschitz and a global minimizer $x_\star$ satisfies $\|x_0-x_\star\|\le R$. Here $L,\varepsilon>0$ and $R,\sigma\ge0$. The jointly measurable oracle $g$ has integrable values and squared errors, with $\int g(x,\omega)\,dp(\omega)=\nabla f(x)$ and $\int\|g(x,\omega)-\nabla f(x)\|^2\,dp(\omega)\le\sigma^2$ for a probability law $p$. Samples are IID, $P=p^{\otimes\mathbb N}$. The target is measurable output $\widehat x$ with integrable gap and $\int[f(\widehat x)-f(x_\star)]\,dP\le\varepsilon$. The setup imposes no algorithm restriction.}\par\smallskip
