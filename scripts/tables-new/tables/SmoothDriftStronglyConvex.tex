\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On a separable real Hilbert space, each deterministic $f_k$ is differentiable, $\mu$-strongly convex and $L$-smooth, with $0<\mu\le L$. Minimizers satisfy $\|x_0-x_0^\star\|\le R$, $\|x_1^\star-x_0^\star\|\le V$ and $\|x_{k+2}^\star-2x_{k+1}^\star+x_k^\star\|\le\Gamma$. Jointly measurable stochastic gradients use independent samples with common law $p$, are unbiased and have variance at most $\sigma^2$ uniformly in $x$; the relevant first and second moments are integrable. For $\varepsilon>0$ and $R,V,\Gamma,\sigma\ge0$, admissibility requires $9792\Gamma^2(2L/\mu+99\sigma^2/(\mu^2\varepsilon))^4\le\varepsilon$. The target is $\int\|x_N(\omega)-x_{N-1}^\star\|^2\,dP(\omega)\le\varepsilon$, where $P=p^{\otimes\mathbb N}$. The setup requires the output time to count oracle calls, not a particular update rule.}\par\smallskip
