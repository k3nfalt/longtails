\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize Compact convex $X\subset E$, $Y\subset F$ in finite-dimensional real inner-product spaces; feasible initial point; $\operatorname{diam}(X\times Y)\le D$. Player costs are $h+g$ and $g-h$: $h$ is $\mu$-strongly convex in $x$ and $\nu$-strongly concave in $y$; $g$ is jointly convex. Gradients within the domain exist, $\nabla(h\pm g)$ are $L$-Lipschitz, and $\nabla g$ is $\delta$-Lipschitz. Here $0<\mu,\nu\le L$, $0\le\delta\le L$, and $D,\varepsilon>0$. An equilibrium exists. Access: own gradients and exact Euclidean projection. Target: feasible $z_{\rm out}$ with $\|z_{\rm out}-z^\star\|^2\le\varepsilon$ for an equilibrium $z^\star$.}\par\smallskip
