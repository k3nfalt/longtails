A minimizer $x_\star\in C$ satisfies $\|x_0-x_\star\|\le R$, where $L>0$, $R\ge0$, and $\varepsilon>0$. Inputs include $L,R,\varepsilon,x_0$ and exact $f_\star=f(x_\star)$.
