\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize Convex $f:E\to\mathbb R$ on a real Hilbert space has a differentiable gradient satisfying $\|\nabla^2 f(x)\|\le L_0+L_1\|\nabla f(x)\|$ everywhere. A minimizer $x_\star$ satisfies $\|x_0-x_\star\|\le R$ and $f(x_0)-f(x_\star)\le\Delta$, with $L_0,R,\Delta,\varepsilon>0$ and $L_1\ge0$. Exact gradients are available; $L_0,L_1,R,\Delta$ are public inputs. The target is $f(x_{\rm out})-f(x_\star)\le\varepsilon$ almost surely; our constructions guarantee it for every seed. The setup imposes no algorithm restriction.}\par\smallskip
