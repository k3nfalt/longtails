Target: $x\in X$ and some $\xi\in N_X(x)$ with $\|F(x)+\xi\|\le\varepsilon$. & \textbf{(1)} extends to constraints and nonzero $F(x^*)$.
& On $X=E$, \textbf{(1)} has an extra $1+s$ factor in the leading and logarithmic terms, and larger leading constants. \\

& \textbf{(1)} extends beyond global Lipschitz continuity; matches the rate at $L_1=0$.
