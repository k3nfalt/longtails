\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On a real Hilbert space, differentiable $f$ satisfies the global quadratic upper and lower bounds with constants $L$ and $\mu$, where $0<\mu\le L$. A minimizer $x_\star$ exists with $\|x_0-x_\star\|\le R$. Every oracle answer obeys $\|g-\nabla f(x)\|\le\alpha\|\nabla f(x)\|+\delta$, with $0\le\alpha<1$ and $\delta,R\ge0$; errors may depend on the call and seed, without independence or unbiasedness. For $\varepsilon>0$ and $136\delta^2\le\mu(1-\alpha)^2\varepsilon$, the constructed method guarantees $f(x_N)-f(x_\star)\le\varepsilon$ for every seed. Its updates use $L,\mu,\alpha,x_0$; the setup does not restrict the algorithm family. Prior-work bounds below are compared on $E=\mathbb R^d$.}\par\smallskip
