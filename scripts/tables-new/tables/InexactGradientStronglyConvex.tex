\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On a real Hilbert space, $f$ is differentiable with global quadratic upper and lower models of curvature $L$ and $\mu$, where $0<\mu\le L$. A minimizer $x^\star$ satisfies $\|x_0-x^\star\|\le R$. Every oracle reply obeys $\|g-\nabla f(x)\|\le\alpha\|\nabla f(x)\|+\delta$, with $0\le\alpha<1$ and $\delta,R\ge0$; errors may depend on the call and seed. The target is $f(x_N)-f(x^\star)\le\varepsilon$ almost surely, with $\varepsilon>0$ and $136\delta^2\le\mu(1-\alpha)^2\varepsilon$. The constructed method uses $L,\mu,\alpha,x_0$; the setup imposes no algorithm restriction.}\par\smallskip
