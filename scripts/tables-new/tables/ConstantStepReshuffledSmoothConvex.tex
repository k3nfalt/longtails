\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On a real Hilbert space with its Borel measurable structure, $F=n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^n f_i$, $n\geq1$, each $f_i$ is convex with $L_i$-Lipschitz gradient, $0\leq L_i\leq L_{\max}$ and $n^{-1}\sum_iL_i\leq L_{\mathrm{avg}}$, where $0<L_{\mathrm{avg}}\leq L_{\max}$. A minimizer $x_\star$ satisfies $\|x_0-x_\star\|\leq R$ and $n^{-1}\sum_i\|\nabla f_i(x_\star)\|^2\leq\sigma^2$, with $R,\sigma\geq0$. The setup requires constant-step RR with independent uniform permutations and exact component gradients. For $\varepsilon>0$, \textbf{(1)} guarantees $\int [F(x_{nK}(\omega))-F(x_\star)]\,dP(\omega)\leq\varepsilon$, where $P$ is the permutation law. Costs count all component-gradient calls; comparisons are on the stated common restrictions.}\par\smallskip
