\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, $f$ has an attained minimum $f_\star=f(x_\star)$, $L$-Lipschitz gradient, $\rho$-Lipschitz Hessian, and $2\mu(f-f_\star)\leq\|\nabla f\|^2$ globally; $f(x_0)-f_\star\leq\Delta$. Here $0<\mu\leq L$, $\rho,\sigma,\Delta\geq0$, $K\geq1$, $\varepsilon>0$. A jointly measurable IID oracle satisfies $\mathbb E g(x)=\nabla f(x)$, $\mathbb E\|g(x)-\nabla f(x)\|^2\leq\sigma^2$, and $\mathbb E\|g(x)-\nabla f(x)\|^4\leq K\sigma^4$. The setup requires constant-step SGD with a uniformly sampled final-window output $\hat x$; the target is $\int(f(\hat x)-f_\star)\,dP\leq\varepsilon$, with measurable output and integrable gap.}\par\smallskip
