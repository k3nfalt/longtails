\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, $f$ is differentiable, $f\ge f_{\rm low}$, and $f(x_0)-f_{\rm low}\le\Delta$. For every $x,u$, a linear map $Q(x)$ satisfies $f(x+u)\le f(x)+\langle\nabla f(x),u\rangle+\frac12\langle Q(x)u,u\rangle$ and $\langle Q(x)\nabla f(x),\nabla f(x)\rangle\le L_g\|\nabla f(x)\|^2$. A jointly measurable oracle uses IID samples with law $p$: $\int g(x,\omega)\,dp=\nabla f(x)$, $\int\|\zeta\|^2\,dp\le\sigma^2$, and $\int\langle Q(x)\zeta,\zeta\rangle\,dp\le L_\xi\int\|\zeta\|^2\,dp$, with these quantities integrable. Here $L_g,\varepsilon>0$ and $L_\xi,\sigma,\Delta\ge0$; $Q$ need not be symmetric, positive semidefinite, or uniformly bounded. Target: measurable output $\hat x$ with $\int\|\nabla f(\hat x)\|\,dP\le\varepsilon$, where $P$ is the execution law.}\par\smallskip
