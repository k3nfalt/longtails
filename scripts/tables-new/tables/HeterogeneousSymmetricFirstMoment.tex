\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize
$f:\mathbb R^{b+h}\to\mathbb R$ is differentiable with $L$-Lipschitz gradient and $f(x_0)-\inf f\le\Delta$; $b+h>0$, $L,\Delta,\varepsilon,\delta>0$ and $M,\sigma_b,\sigma_h\ge0$.
The jointly measurable, integrable oracle uses IID samples of law $p$ and satisfies $\int g(x,\omega)\,dp=\nabla f(x)$.
Writing $\zeta=g-\nabla f$, each body coordinate is symmetric, $\int\|\zeta_b\|_1\,dp\le\sigma_b$, and $\int\|\zeta_h\|_2^2\,dp\le\sigma_h^2$.
Almost surely, $|\zeta_i(x,\omega)-\zeta_i(y,\omega)|\le K(\omega)\|x-y\|$ for all body coordinates and points, with measurable $K\ge0$ and $\int K\,dp\le M$.
The setup restricts algorithms to bias-corrected body Adam with $(\beta_1,\beta_2)=(0.9,0.999)$ and stabilizer $\delta$, coupled to head AdaGrad.
Target: $\int\|\nabla f(x_J)\|\,d\mathbb P\le\varepsilon$, where $\mathbb P$ combines the IID sample law with an independent uniform iterate index $J$.}\par\smallskip
