\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize In a finite-dimensional real inner-product space $E$, minimize $F=f+\psi$ over $C$. The function $f:E\to\mathbb R$ is convex and $C^2$, with $\|\nabla^2 f(x)-\nabla^2 f(y)\|\le H\|x-y\|$ globally; $\psi$ is convex on $C$ and its epigraph over $C$ is closed. A minimizer $x_\star\in C$ exists, $x_0\in C$, and $\|x_0-x_\star\|\le R$. Parameters $H,R,\varepsilon>0$ and integer $m\ge1$ are supplied. Oracles return exact gradients, Hessians, and composite cubic/positive-definite quadratic model solutions. The target is feasible $x$ with $F(x)-F(x_\star)\le\varepsilon$; \textbf{(1)} guarantees this for every oracle seed.}\par\smallskip
