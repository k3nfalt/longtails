\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On a separable real Hilbert space, finitely many self-adjoint operators satisfy $0\preceq H_i\preceq LI$ and $f(x)-f(u)=\frac12\sum_i\pi_i\langle x-u,H_i(x-u)\rangle$, with $\|x_0-u\|\le R$. The oracle returns $H_{s_t}(x-u)$ along a Markov chain with arbitrary initial law, stationary distribution $\pi$, and $\kappa(i,\cdot)\ge\alpha\pi$. Here $L>0$, $R\ge0$, $0<\alpha\le1$, and $\varepsilon>0$. The target is $\int[f(\widehat x(\omega))-f(u)]\,dP(\omega)\le\varepsilon$. The setup permits any output; \textbf{(1)} constructs plain SGD's last iterate.}\par\smallskip
