Jointly measurable value oracle $g$ uses fresh IID seeds of law $p$, with $\int g(x,z)\,dp(z)=f(x)$ and $\int(g(x,z)-f(x))^2\,dp(z)\le\sigma^2$; these integrals are finite. Here $R,\sigma\ge0$, $\varepsilon>0$. Target: measurable output $\hat x$ with integrable gap and $\int[f(\hat x(\omega))-f(x_\star)]\,dP(\omega)\le\varepsilon$, where $P$ is the execution law.
