\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize Convex $f:E\to\mathbb R$ on a separable real Hilbert space with its Borel measurable structure; a minimizer $x_\star$ satisfies $\|x_0-x_\star\|\le R$. The jointly measurable oracle $g(\xi,x)$ has $\int g(\xi,x)\,dp(\xi)\in\partial f(x)$ and integrable squared norm with $\int\|g(\xi,x)\|^2\,dp(\xi)\le G^2$ for every $x$. Samples are IID from the probability law $p$. Here $G>0$, $R\ge0$, $0\le\beta<1$, and $\varepsilon>0$. The setup restricts algorithms to constant-$\beta$ EMA momentum SGD. Result \textbf{(1)} uses zero initial momentum and horizon-dependent affine step decay, tuned with $G,R,\beta,\varepsilon$, and returns $x_N$ with $\int[f(x_N)-f(x_\star)]\,dP\le\varepsilon$, where $P=p^{\otimes\mathbb N}$.}\par\smallskip
