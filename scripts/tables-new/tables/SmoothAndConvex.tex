\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize A convex differentiable $f:E\to\mathbb{R}$ on a real Hilbert space has an exact $L$-Lipschitz gradient oracle, $L>0$, and a minimizer $x_\star$ with $\|x_0-x_\star\|\le R$, $R\ge0$. The target is $f(x_{\mathrm{out}})-f(x_\star)\le\varepsilon$, $\varepsilon>0$, almost surely under the oracle law (deterministically here). The setup imposes no algorithm restriction.}\par\smallskip
