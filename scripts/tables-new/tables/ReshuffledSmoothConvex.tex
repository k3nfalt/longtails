\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize Let $f=n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^n f_i$ on a real Hilbert space with its Borel measurable structure, $n\ge1$. Each $f_i$ is convex with $\ell_i$-Lipschitz gradient, $0\le\ell_i\le L_{\max}$ and $n^{-1}\sum_i\ell_i\le L_{\rm avg}$, where $0<L_{\rm avg}\le L_{\max}$. A minimizer $x_\star$ satisfies $\|x_0-x_\star\|\le R$ and $n^{-1}\sum_i\|\nabla f_i(x_\star)\|^2\le\sigma^2$, with $R,\sigma\ge0$. The oracle returns exact component gradients. The setup restricts algorithms to RR; \textbf{(1)} uses fresh independent uniform permutations and a known-horizon linearly decreasing epoch step. Its measurable last iterate has integrable gap and satisfies $\int [f(x_{nK}(\omega))-f(x_\star)]\,dP(\omega)\le\varepsilon$, $\varepsilon>0$, under the permutation law $P$.}\par\smallskip
