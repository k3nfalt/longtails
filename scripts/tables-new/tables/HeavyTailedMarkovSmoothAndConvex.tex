\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize Convex, differentiable $f$ on a second-countable real Hilbert space has $L$-Lipschitz gradient and a minimizer $x_\star$ with $\|x_0-x_\star\|\le R$. A stationary Markov chain with kernel $\kappa$ and law $\pi$ drives a jointly measurable oracle satisfying $\int g(x,z)\,d\pi(z)=\nabla f(x)$ and $\int\|g(x,z)-\nabla f(x)\|^\alpha\,d\pi(z)\le\sigma^\alpha$, with these integrands integrable. At every integer lag $t\ge0$, $\int\mathrm{TV}(\kappa^t(z,\cdot),\pi)\,d\pi(z)\le(1+t/\tau)^{-\beta}$. Here $L,\tau,\beta,\varepsilon>0$, $R,\sigma\ge0$, and $1<\alpha\le2$. Target: measurable output $Y$ with integrable gap and $\int[f(Y)-f(x_\star)]\,dP\le\varepsilon$, where $P$ is the stationary path law.}\par\smallskip
