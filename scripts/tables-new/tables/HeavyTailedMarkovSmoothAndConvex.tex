\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize
On a second-countable real Hilbert space with its Borel measurable structure, $f$ is convex and differentiable with $L$-Lipschitz gradient and a minimizer $x_\star$ satisfying $\|x_0-x_\star\|\le R$. The jointly measurable oracle satisfies $\int g(x,z)\,d\pi(z)=\nabla f(x)$ and $\int\|g(x,z)-\nabla f(x)\|^\alpha\,d\pi(z)\le\sigma^\alpha$, with the corresponding integrability assumptions. Samples follow a stationary Markov chain with kernel $\kappa$, invariant probability $\pi$, and integrable $d_t(z)=\operatorname{TV}(\kappa^t(z,\cdot),\pi)$ satisfying $\int d_t\,d\pi\le(1+t/\tau)^{-\beta}$ for every lag. Here $L,\tau,\beta,\varepsilon>0$, $R,\sigma\ge0$, and $1<\alpha\le2$. Target: measurable output with integrable gap and $\int[f(\mathrm{output}(\omega))-f(x_\star)]\,dP(\omega)\le\varepsilon$, where $P$ is the stationary path law.}\par\smallskip
