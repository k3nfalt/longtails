\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize Convex sets $X\subset E$, $Y\subset F$ in real inner-product spaces satisfy $\|x\|,\|y\|\le R$, with $(x_0,y_0)\in X\times Y$. The objective is $\Phi(x,y)=g(x)+\langle y,Kx\rangle-h(y)$, where $K$ is continuous linear, $\|K\|\le L$, and $g,h$ are convex and $M$-Lipschitz on their respective domains; $R,L,\varepsilon>0$, $M\ge0$. Exact $K,K^*$ and constrained proximal maps are available. The setup restricts algorithms to constant-step Arrow--Hurwicz. The target is a feasible pair $(\bar x,\bar y)$ with $\Phi(\bar x,y)-\Phi(x,\bar y)\le\varepsilon$ for every $(x,y)\in X\times Y$, deterministically.}\par\smallskip
