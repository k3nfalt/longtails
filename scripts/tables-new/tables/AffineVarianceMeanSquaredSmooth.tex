\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On a separable real Hilbert space, $f$ has an $L$-Lipschitz gradient and $f(x_0)-\inf f\le\Delta$. The jointly measurable, unbiased oracle uses IID samples and returns $(g(x,\xi),g(y,\xi))$ with the same sample, with finite second moments satisfying $\mathbb E\|g(x,\xi)-\nabla f(x)\|^2\le\Gamma^2\|\nabla f(x)\|^2+\Lambda^2$ and $\mathbb E\|g(x,\xi)-g(y,\xi)\|^2\le\bar L^2\|x-y\|^2$. Here $L,\varepsilon>0$ and $\bar L,\Gamma,\Lambda,\Delta\ge0$. Target: $\int\|\nabla f(x_{\rm out}(\omega))\|\,dP(\omega)\le\varepsilon$.}\par\smallskip
