\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On a real Hilbert space, $f:E\to\mathbb R$ is differentiable and convex with the quadratic upper bound of $L$-smoothness; $C$ is convex and $g:E\to\mathbb R$ is convex on $C$. Exact proximal minimizers of $f$ and of $g$ over $C$ are available at one fixed $\gamma$. A minimizer $x_\star\in C$ exists with $\|w_1-x_\star-\gamma\nabla f(x_\star)\|\le R$. Assume $L,R,\varepsilon,\gamma>0$, $0<\lambda\le1$, and $4\gamma L\le\lambda$. Target: $y_N\in C$ and $F(y_N)-F(x_\star)\le\varepsilon$, deterministically.}\par\smallskip
