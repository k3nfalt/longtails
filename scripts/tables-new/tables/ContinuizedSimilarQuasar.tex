\caption{Comparison for \href{https://github.com/k3nfalt/longtails/blob/main/LongTails/Setups/ContinuizedSimilarQuasar.lean}{ContinuizedSimilarQuasar}.}\label{tab:ContinuizedSimilarQuasar}
\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize
Let $F=n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^n f_i$ on a real Hilbert space, with differentiable $f_i$, exact component gradients, and $L$-Lipschitz $\nabla F$.
For a global minimizer $x_\star$, assume $F(x_0)-F(x_\star)\le\Delta$ and
$F(x)-F(x_\star)\le\gamma^{-1}\langle\nabla F(x),x-x_\star\rangle-\frac\mu2\|x-x_\star\|^2$.
For all $x,y$, assume
$\frac1n\sum_i\|[\nabla f_i(x)-\nabla F(x)]-[\nabla f_i(y)-\nabla F(y)]\|^2\le\delta^2\|x-y\|^2$.
Here $n\ge1$, $0<\mu\le L$, $0<\gamma\le1$, $\delta,\Delta\ge0$, and $\varepsilon>0$.
No interpolation is required. Our constructions have deterministic component-gradient budgets and final outputs $X$ satisfying $\int [F(X(\omega))-F(x_\star)]\,dP(\omega)\le\varepsilon$, where $P$ is the execution law.
