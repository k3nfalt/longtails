\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On finite-dimensional real inner-product spaces, $f(x,y)$ has an $L$-Lipschitz joint gradient, a $\rho$-Lipschitz joint Hessian $\nabla^2 f$, and is $\mu$-strongly concave in $y$. Let $\Phi(x)=\max_y f(x,y)$, with $\Phi(x_0)-\inf\Phi\le\Delta$ and $\|\nabla_y f(x_0,y_0)\|\le G$. Here $0<\mu\le L$, $\rho,\sigma,\varepsilon>0$, and $\Delta,G\ge0$. Each call returns a jointly measurable, unbiased joint-gradient estimate with total variance at most $\sigma^2$, using fresh IID samples. The target is $\mathbb E\|\nabla\Phi(\widehat x)\|\le\varepsilon$, over sampling and algorithm randomness; \textbf{(1)} constructs normalized SGDA with implicit gradient transport and a uniformly sampled iterate.}\par\smallskip
