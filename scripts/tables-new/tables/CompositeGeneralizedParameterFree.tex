\caption{Composite optimization with parameter-free updates: \href{https://github.com/k3nfalt/longtails/blob/main/LongTails/Setups/CompositeGeneralizedParameterFree.lean}{CompositeGeneralizedParameterFree}.}\label{tab:CompositeGeneralizedParameterFree}
\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize Minimize $F=f+\psi$ over convex $Q$ in a real Hilbert space. The function $f$ is convex with continuous gradient and satisfies $\|\nabla f(x)-\nabla f(y)\|\le (L_0+L_1M)\|x-y\|$ whenever $\|\nabla f(z)\|\le M$ on $[x,y]$; $\psi$ is finite-valued and convex on $Q$. Exact gradients and minimizers of $\psi(u)+\langle v,u-z\rangle+\|u-z\|^2/(2a)$ over $u\in Q$ are available for every $a>0,z,v$. There is a minimizer $x_\star\in Q$ with $x_0\in Q$, $\|x_0-x_\star\|\le R$, $F(x_0)-F(x_\star)\le\Delta$, and $\|\nabla f(x_\star)\|\le G$. Here $L_0,R,\Delta,h_0,\varepsilon>0$ and $L_1,G\ge0$. Target: deterministic $\widehat x\in Q$ with $F(\widehat x)-F(x_\star)\le\varepsilon$. The constructed method receives only $(x_0,h_0)$; its stopping budget depends on all setup parameters.}\par\smallskip
