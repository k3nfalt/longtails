\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize
On a real Hilbert space with its Borel measurable structure, let $F(x)=n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^n(\langle x,A_ix\rangle/2+\langle b_i,x\rangle)$, where $A_i$ are bounded self-adjoint operators, $\|A_i\|\le L$, and $n^{-1}\sum_i A_i\succeq\mu I$. Here $n\ge1$, $0<\mu\le L$, $R,G\ge0$, and $\varepsilon>0$. A stationary minimizer $x_*$ satisfies $\|x_0-x_*\|\le R$ and $\max_i\|A_ix_*+b_i\|\le G$; components may be indefinite. One oracle call returns an exact component gradient $A_ix+b_i$. The target is $\int[F(\widehat x(\omega))-F(x_*)]\,dP(\omega)\le\varepsilon$. Result \textbf{(1)} constructs constant-step random reshuffling with independent uniform permutations and final endpoint $\widehat x$; the setup does not restrict the algorithm. Comparisons below use $n\ge2$ and $0<\varepsilon<LR^2/2$.}\par\smallskip
