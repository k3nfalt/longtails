\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On a finite-dimensional real Hilbert space, $f(x)=c+\|A(x-u)\|^2/2$ is convex with $L$-Lipschitz gradient and a minimizer $x_\star$ satisfying $\|x_0-x_\star\|\le R$. IID samples from $p$ give jointly measurable, integrable gradients $g$ with $\int g(x,\omega)\,dp(\omega)=\nabla f(x)$ and integrable squared error satisfying $\int\|g(x,\omega)-\nabla f(x)\|^2\,dp(\omega)\le\sigma^2$. Here $L,\varepsilon>0$ and $R,\sigma\ge0$ are known. The setup restricts algorithms to batched SGD with predetermined nonnegative scalar steps and positive integer batches, returning the last iterate. Target: $\int[f(\widehat x)-f(x_\star)]\,dP\le\varepsilon$.}\par\smallskip
