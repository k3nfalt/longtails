\caption{Finite-sum weak-Minty inclusions: \href{https://github.com/k3nfalt/longtails/blob/main/LongTails/Setups/FiniteSumWeakMintyInclusion.lean}{FiniteSumWeakMintyInclusion}.}\label{tab:FiniteSumWeakMintyInclusion}
\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize In a real Hilbert space with its Borel measurable structure, $F=n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^{n}F_i$ is $L$-Lipschitz and $n^{-1}\sum_i\|F_i(x)-F_i(y)\|^2\leq\Lambda^2\|x-y\|^2$. The operator $A$ is monotone with exact, everywhere-defined resolvents for every positive step size. There is $x_\star$ with $0\in F(x_\star)+A(x_\star)$, $\|x_0-x_\star\|\leq R$, and $\langle F(y)+v,y-x_\star\rangle\geq-\rho\|F(y)+v\|^2$ for every $v\in A(y)$. Here $n\geq1$, $0<L\leq\Lambda$, $\rho,R\geq0$, $\rho L<1$, and $\varepsilon>0$. The target is a measurable pair $(Y,V)$ with $V\in A(Y)$ almost surely and integrable squared residual satisfying $\int\|F(Y)+V\|^2\,dP\leq\varepsilon^2$, where $P$ is the execution law. Each component evaluation or resolvent evaluation costs one call.}\par\smallskip
