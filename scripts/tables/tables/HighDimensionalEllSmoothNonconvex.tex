\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize The objective $f:\mathbb R^d\to\mathbb R$ is twice differentiable with $f(x^0)-\inf f\le\Delta$ and $\|\nabla^2 f(x)\|\le\ell(\|\nabla f(x)\|)$. For $\varepsilon>0$, assume $N\ge1$ and $d\ge 2^{56}(N+1)^6(\ell(\varepsilon)/\ell(0))^4$. Randomized algorithms know the setup parameters and can query exact $(f,\nabla f)$ anywhere. The target is $\mathbb E\|\nabla f(\hat x)\|\le\varepsilon$ over the algorithm's seed, with measurable output and integrable gradient norm.}\par\smallskip
