\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On $\mathbb R^d$, the $n$ convex, $L_i$-smooth worker objectives have a common minimizer set $S=\bigcap_i\arg\min f_i\ne\varnothing$. Their average $f$ satisfies $f(x)-\min f\ge\mu\operatorname{dist}(x,S)^2/2$, with $0<n\mu\le\sum_i L_i$ and $\operatorname{dist}(x_0,S)\le R$. Gradients are unbiased with variance at most $\sigma^2$, sampled IID within workers and independently across workers. On the coordinator star, one gradient at worker $i$ takes $h_i>0$ seconds and a $k$-bit message takes $\ell+bk$ seconds. Workers and links operate concurrently, with one pending call per worker and one message per directed link. The target is $\mathbb E_P\operatorname{dist}(x_{\rm out},S)^2\le\varepsilon$, $\varepsilon>0$. Times are compared at $\ell=b=0$.}\par\smallskip
