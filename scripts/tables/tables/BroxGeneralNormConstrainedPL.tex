\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize In a finite-dimensional real normed space, $C$ is closed and convex and $x_0\in C$. The objective is bounded below on $C$ and differentiable at each point of $C$, with $L$-Lipschitz derivative in the dual norm. On $C$, $\mu(f(x)-f_\star)\le s(x)^2/2$. The oracles give exact gradients and exact local LMOs with arbitrary tie-breaking. For $L,\mu,r,\varepsilon>0$, the target, guaranteed almost surely, is $x\in C$ with $f(x)-f_\star\le\varepsilon$.}\par\smallskip
