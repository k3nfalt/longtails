\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize
Consider $\min_{x\in X}\max_{y\in Y,\,c(x,y)\le0}f(x,y)$ on nonempty compact convex Euclidean sets, with supplied starts in $X\times Y$. The gradient of $f$ is $L_f$-Lipschitz, $f(x,\cdot)$ is $\mu$-strongly concave, and each $c_i(x,\cdot)$ is convex. The constraint Jacobian is $L_J$-Lipschitz; $\|J_c\|\le J$, $\|\nabla_x f\|\le G_x$, $\|\nabla_y f\|\le G_y$, $\|c\|\le C$; diameters are bounded by $D_X,D_Y$. At every $(x,y)$, some $d\in N_Y(y)^\circ$ satisfies $\|d\|\le Z$ and $\langle\nabla_y c_i,d\rangle\le-\delta$ for every violated constraint. Here $\mu,\delta,Z,\varepsilon>0$. Target: deterministic $\varepsilon$-KKT, with $\lambda\ge0$, $\|[c]_+\|\le\varepsilon$, $\sum_i|\lambda_i c_i|\le\varepsilon$, and normal-cone stationarity residuals at most $\varepsilon$ for $x$ and $y$. One complete oracle call returns $(f,\nabla f,c,J_c)$.}\par\smallskip
