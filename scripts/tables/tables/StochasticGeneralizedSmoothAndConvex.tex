\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize Convex $f$ on a separable real Hilbert space, with differentiable gradient and $\|\nabla^2 f(x)\|\le L_0+L_1\|\nabla f(x)\|$. A minimizer $x_\star$ satisfies $\|x_0-x_\star\|\le R$ and $f(x_0)-f(x_\star)\le\Delta$. The jointly measurable oracle is IID under a probability law $P$, unbiased, with integrable noise moments, $\mathbb E\|g(x,\xi)-\nabla f(x)\|^2\le\sigma^2$ and $\mathbb E\exp(\|g(x,\xi)-\nabla f(x)\|^2/\sigma^2)\le e$ for $\sigma>0$; $\sigma=0$ gives exact gradients almost surely. Here $L_0,R,\Delta,\varepsilon>0$, $L_1,\sigma\ge0$, and $0<\delta<1$. Target: measurable $\hat x$ with $P\{f(\hat x)-f(x_\star)>\varepsilon\}\le\delta$. All parameters are supplied; costs count individual stochastic-gradient samples, including batches.}\par\smallskip
