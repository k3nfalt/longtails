\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize Convex $f$ on a separable real Hilbert space has differentiable gradient and $\|\nabla^2 f(x)\|\le L_0+L_1\|\nabla f(x)\|$. A minimizer $x^\star$ satisfies $\|x_0-x^\star\|\le R$ and $f(x_0)-f(x^\star)\le\Delta$. The jointly measurable oracle is unbiased, with integrable gradient and squared error, $\mathbb E\|g(x,\xi)-\nabla f(x)\|^2\le\sigma^2$, and $\mathbb E\exp(\|g(x,\xi)-\nabla f(x)\|^2/\sigma^2)\le e$ for $\sigma>0$; $\sigma=0$ means exact gradients almost surely. Samples are IID with marginal $p$ under probability law $P$. Parameters $L_0,R,\Delta,\varepsilon>0$, $L_1,\sigma\ge0$, and $\delta\in(0,1)$ are supplied. Target: measurable $\hat x$ with $P\{f(\hat x)-f(x^\star)>\varepsilon\}\le\delta$. Costs count individual stochastic-gradient samples, including batches and trials.}\par\smallskip
