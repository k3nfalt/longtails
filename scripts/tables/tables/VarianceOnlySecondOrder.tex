\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On a real inner-product space of dimension $d\ge1$, $f\in C^2$ has $L$-Lipschitz gradient, $\rho$-Lipschitz Hessian, and $f(x_0)-\inf f\le\Delta$. Here $L,\rho,\varepsilon>0$, $\sigma,\Delta\ge0$, $0<\delta<1$, and $\rho\varepsilon\le L^2$. Fresh IID samples have law $p$; the jointly measurable oracle satisfies $\int g(x,z)\,dp(z)=\nabla f(x)$ and $\int\|g(x,z)-\nabla f(x)\|^2\,dp(z)\le\sigma^2$. The target is $P\{\|\nabla f(x)\|\le\varepsilon,\ \nabla^2f(x)\succeq-\sqrt{\rho\varepsilon}I\}\ge1-\delta$.}\par\smallskip
