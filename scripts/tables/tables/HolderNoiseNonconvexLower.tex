\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On $\mathbb R^d$, $f$ is differentiable with $L$-Lipschitz gradient and $f(x_0)-\inf f\le\Delta$. Here $L,\varepsilon>0$, $M,\sigma,\Delta\ge0$, $0<\nu\le1$, $L\Delta\ge64\varepsilon^2$, and $d\ge\lceil2^{30}(1+L\Delta/\varepsilon^2)^3(1+\sigma^2/\varepsilon^2)\rceil$. A call returns $(g(x,\omega),g(y,\omega))$ with a fresh IID sample $\omega\in\{0,1\}$ of law $p$. The jointly measurable oracle satisfies $\int g(x,\omega)\,dp=\nabla f(x)$, $\int\|e(x,\omega)\|^2\,dp\le\sigma^2$, and $\int\|e(x,\omega)-e(y,\omega)\|^2\,dp\le M\|x-y\|^{2\nu}$, with the corresponding integrability assumptions. The target is a measurable output with integrable gradient norm and $\int\|\nabla f(\widehat x)\|\,dP\le\varepsilon$. All randomized algorithms are allowed; the hard instance may depend on the algorithm and budget, but not its realized seed.}\par\smallskip
