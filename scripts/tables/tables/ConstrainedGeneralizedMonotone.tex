\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize In a real inner-product space $E$, $X$ is convex with an exact projection oracle. The continuous, monotone operator $F:E\to E$ satisfies $\|F(x)-F(y)\|\le (L_0+L_1\sup_{z\in[x,y]}\|F(z)\|)\|x-y\|$ for all $x,y\in E$. Here $L_0,R,\varepsilon>0$, $L_1,G,G_0\ge0$, $x_0\in X$, and there is a VI solution $x^\star\in X$ with $\|x_0-x^\star\|\le R$, $\|F(x^\star)\|\le G$, and $\|F(x_0)\|\le G_0$. Target: $x\in X$ and some $\xi\in N_X(x)$ with $\|F(x)+\xi\|\le\varepsilon$, using exact $F$ and projection calls.}\par\smallskip
