\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize Minimize $F=f+\psi$ over convex $Q$ in a real Hilbert space. The function $f$ is convex with continuous gradient and satisfies $\|\nabla f(x)-\nabla f(y)\|\le (L_0+L_1\sup_{z\in[x,y]}\|\nabla f(z)\|)\|x-y\|$; $\psi$ is finite-valued and convex on $Q$. Exact gradients and exact minimizers of $\psi(u)+\langle v,u-z\rangle+\|u-z\|^2/(2a)$ over $Q$ are available for every $a>0$. There is $x_\star\in\arg\min_Q F$ with $x_0\in Q$, $\|x_0-x_\star\|\le R$, $F(x_0)-F(x_\star)\le\Delta$, and $\|\nabla f(x_\star)\|\le G$. Here $L_0,R,\Delta,\varepsilon,h_0>0$ and $L_1,G\ge0$. Target: $\widehat x\in Q$, $F(\widehat x)-F(x_\star)\le\varepsilon$. In \textbf{(1)}, updates receive only $x_0,h_0$; the stopping budget uses all setup parameters.}\par\smallskip
