\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize Minimize $F=f+\psi$ over convex $Q$ in a real Hilbert space. The function $f$ is convex with continuous gradient and satisfies $\|\nabla f(x)-\nabla f(y)\|\le (L_0+L_1M)\|x-y\|$ whenever $\|\nabla f\|\le M$ on $[x,y]$. The finite-valued $\psi$ is convex on $Q$; exact gradients and exact proximal minimization over $Q$ are available. For $x_0\in Q$, some minimizer $x_\star\in Q$ satisfies $\|x_0-x_\star\|\le R$, $F(x_0)-F(x_\star)\le\Delta$, and $\|\nabla f(x_\star)\|\le G$. Here $h_0,L_0,R,\Delta,\varepsilon>0$ and $L_1,G\ge0$.
