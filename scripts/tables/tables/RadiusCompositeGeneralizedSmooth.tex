\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize Minimize $F=f+\psi$ over convex $Q$ in a real Hilbert space. The function $f$ is convex and continuously differentiable on the whole space, with $\|\nabla f(x)-\nabla f(y)\|\leq(L_0+L_1\sup_{z\in[x,y]}\|\nabla f(z)\|)\|x-y\|$; $\psi$ is convex on $Q$. Exact gradients and an exact joint prox of $\psi+\iota_Q$ are available. Here $L_0,R,\varepsilon>0$, $L_1,G\geq0$, $x_0\in Q$, and some minimizer $x^\star\in Q$ satisfies $\|x_0-x^\star\|\leq R$ and $\|\nabla f(x^\star)\|\leq G$. Target: feasible $x$ with $F(x)-F(x^\star)\leq\varepsilon$, deterministically.}\par\smallskip
