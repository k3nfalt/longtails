Samples are IID with probability law $p$; the jointly measurable oracle satisfies $\int g(x,\omega)\,dp(\omega)=\nabla f(x)$ and $\int\|g(x,\omega)-\nabla f(x)\|^2\,dp(\omega)\le 2A(f(x)-f_*)+B\|\nabla f(x)\|^2+C$, with integrable oracle values and squared errors.
