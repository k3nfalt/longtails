\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On a real Hilbert space with its Borel measurable structure, $F(x)=n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^n(\langle x,A_i x\rangle/2+\langle b_i,x\rangle)$, with self-adjoint $A_i$, $\|A_i\|\le L$, and $n^{-1}\sum_i A_i\succeq\mu I$. Here $n\ge1$, $0<\mu\le L$, $R,G\ge0$, $\varepsilon>0$; a stationary minimizer $x_\star$ exists with $\|x_0-x_\star\|\le R$ and $\max_i\|A_i x_\star+b_i\|\le G$. Components may be indefinite. The target is $\int [F(\widehat x(\omega))-F(x_\star)]\,dP(\omega)\le\varepsilon$. Costs count exact component-gradient calls. The setup does not restrict the algorithm; \textbf{(1)} constructs random reshuffling.}\par\smallskip
