\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize
On a real Hilbert space, let $F=n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^n f_i$, $n\ge1$, with differentiable components and exact component gradients. Assume $\nabla F$ is $L$-Lipschitz and, for all $x,y$,
$n^{-1}\sum_i\|\nabla(f_i-F)(x)-\nabla(f_i-F)(y)\|^2\le\delta^2\|x-y\|^2$.
A global minimizer $x_\star$ satisfies $F(x_0)-F(x_\star)\le\Delta$ and
$F(x_\star)\ge F(x)+\gamma^{-1}\langle\nabla F(x),x_\star-x\rangle+\frac\mu2\|x-x_\star\|^2$ for all $x$.
Here $0<\mu\le L$, $0<\gamma\le1$, $\delta,\Delta\ge0$, and $\varepsilon>0$.
Target: $\mathbb{E}[F(X)-F(x_\star)]\le\varepsilon$. Costs count all component-gradient calls, including full-gradient refreshes.}\par\smallskip
