\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize In a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, $F$ is $L$-Lipschitz and $G$ is monotone with an everywhere-defined exact resolvent $J_{\gamma G}$ for every $\gamma>0$. For $v\in(F+G)(x)$ and $w\in(F+G)(y)$, $\langle v-w,x-y\rangle\ge-\rho\|v-w\|^2$, with $L>0$ and $0\le2\rho L<1$. A zero $x^\star$ satisfies $\|x_0-x^\star\|\le R$. The jointly measurable oracle $g$ has integrable samples and squared errors, $\int g(x,\omega)\,dp=F(x)$ and $\int\|g(x,\omega)-F(x)\|^2\,dp\le\sigma^2$; samples are IID with law $p$ under $P$. Target: a measurable pair $(\bar x,u)$ with $u\in G(\bar x)$ on every path and $\int\|F(\bar x)+u\|\,dP\le\varepsilon$. Parameters $L,\rho,R,\sigma,\varepsilon$ are supplied; $R,\sigma\ge0$, $\varepsilon>0$. Costs count every $F$ sample and every resolvent evaluation.}\par\smallskip
