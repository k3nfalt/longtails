\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On a separable real Hilbert space with its Borel measurable structure, $f$ is differentiable with $L$-Lipschitz gradient and $f(x_0)-f(x)\le\Delta$ for every $x$. Here $L,\varepsilon>0$, $M,\sigma,\Delta\ge0$, and $0<\nu\le1$. The jointly measurable oracle $g$ uses IID samples with law $p$, satisfies $\int g(x,\omega)\,dp(\omega)=\nabla f(x)$, and, with $e(x,\omega)=g(x,\omega)-\nabla f(x)$, satisfies $\int\|e(x,\omega)\|^2\,dp(\omega)\le\sigma^2$ and $\int\|e(x,\omega)-e(y,\omega)\|^2\,dp(\omega)\le M\|x-y\|^{2\nu}$, with the stated integrands integrable. One call returns gradients at two points using one shared sample. Target: a measurable output with integrable gradient norm and $\int\|\nabla f(x_{\rm out}(\omega))\|\,dP(\omega)\le\varepsilon$, where $P$ is the sampling law. The setup imposes no algorithm restriction.}\par\smallskip
