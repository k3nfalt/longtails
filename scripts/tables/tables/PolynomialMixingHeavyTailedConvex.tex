Its integrated total-variation distance satisfies $\int\|\kappa^t(z,\cdot)-\pi\|_{\rm TV}\,d\pi(z)\le(1+t/\tau)^{-\beta}$. Target: a measurable output with integrable gap and $\int[f(\widehat x)-f(x^\star)]\,dP\le\varepsilon$, where $P$ includes chain and algorithm randomness.
