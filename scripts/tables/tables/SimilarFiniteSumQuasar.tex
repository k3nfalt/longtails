On a real Hilbert space, $F=n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^{n}f_i$, $n\ge1$, with differentiable components and exact component-gradient access. The gradient $\nabla F$ is $L$-Lipschitz and each $\nabla f_i-\nabla F$ is $\delta$-Lipschitz. Parameters satisfy $0<\mu\le L$, $0<\gamma\le1$, $\delta\ge0$, $384\delta^2\le\gamma^3\mu^2$, $\Delta\ge0$, $\varepsilon>0$. A minimizer $x_\star$ satisfies $F(x_0)-F(x_\star)\le\Delta$ and, for every $x$, $F(x_\star)\ge F(x)+\gamma^{-1}\langle\nabla F(x),x_\star-x\rangle+\frac{\mu}{2}\|x-x_\star\|^2$ and $F(x)-F(x_\star)\ge\frac{\gamma\mu}{2(2-\gamma)}\|x-x_\star\|^2$.
For every $x,y$, $F(y)\ge F(x)+\langle\nabla F(x),y-x\rangle-\frac{\gamma\sqrt{\mu L}}{2}\|y-x\|^2$.
Target: $F(\widehat x)-F(x_\star)\le\varepsilon$ almost surely; \textbf{(1)} constructs a deterministic output and satisfies this pathwise.}\par\smallskip
