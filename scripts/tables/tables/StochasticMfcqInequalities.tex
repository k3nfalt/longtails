\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize
Minimize smooth, possibly nonconvex $f$ subject to $c_i\leq0$, $m\geq1$, in a finite-dimensional real inner-product space. Globally, $\nabla f,\nabla c_i$ are $H$-Lipschitz; their norms are at most $H$ on the feasible set. Here $H\geq1$, $0<\mu\leq H$, $\tau,\varepsilon>0$, $\sigma,L,\Delta\geq0$, and every feasible $x$ admits $\|z\|\leq1$ with $\langle\nabla c_i(x),z\rangle\leq-\mu$ whenever $c_i(x)\geq-\tau$. The feasible start may be on the boundary; $f(x_0)-f(x)\leq\Delta$ for every feasible $x$. Jointly measurable, integrable, unbiased samples of $(\nabla f,c,J)$ have joint variance at most $\sigma^2$ and same-sample mean-square differences at most $L^2\|x-y\|^2$ on feasible points; successive samples are IID. Parameters are supplied. Target: with probability at least $1-\rho$, $0<\rho<1$, output $(x,\lambda)$ satisfies $c(x)\leq0$, $\lambda\geq0$, $\|\nabla f(x)+J(x)^\top\lambda\|\leq\varepsilon$, and $\sum_i|\lambda_i c_i(x)|\leq\varepsilon$. The constructed execution also keeps every query feasible on this event.
