\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On $\Delta_d=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:x_i\ge0,\ \sum_i x_i=1\}$, $d\ge2$, $f$ is convex, has an ambient gradient at every feasible point, and satisfies $\|\nabla f(x)-\nabla f(y)\|_\infty\le L\|x-y\|_1$ for feasible $x,y$, with $L>0$. A constrained minimizer $x_\star$ exists. The jointly measurable oracle uses IID samples of law $\nu$, with integrable replies and noise moments, $\int g(x,\omega)\,d\nu=\nabla f(x)$ and $\int\|g(x,\omega)-\nabla f(x)\|_\infty^p\,d\nu\le\sigma^p$, $1<p\le2$, $\sigma\ge0$. Target: a measurable feasible output with $P\{f(\mathrm{output})-f(x_\star)>\varepsilon\}\le\delta$, $\varepsilon>0$, $0<\delta<1$. Public inputs are $d,L,\delta$; the stopping budget also uses $p,\sigma,\varepsilon$.}\par\smallskip
