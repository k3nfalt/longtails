\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize Let $f:\mathbb R^{m\times n}\to\mathbb R$ be differentiable, globally $\mu$-strongly convex and $\rho$-smooth, with exact gradient access and minimizer $X_\star=\bar B\bar A$ of rank $0<r<\min(m,n)$. Assume $0<\mu\le\rho$, $0<s\le R$, $\sigma_r(\bar B),\sigma_r(\bar A^\top)\ge s$, $\|D\pi(\bar B,\bar A)\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le R$, and $\|(B_0,A_0)-(\bar B,\bar A)\|_F\le\mu s^2/(256\rho R)$. The setup restricts algorithms to simultaneous positive scalar-step factor GD, queried at $BA$, returning their final factors. Target: $f(BA)-f(X_\star)\le\varepsilon$, $\varepsilon>0$, deterministically.}\par\smallskip
