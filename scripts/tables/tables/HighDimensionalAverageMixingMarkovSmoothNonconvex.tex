\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize Let $f:\mathbb R^d\to\mathbb R$ have $L$-Lipschitz gradient and $f(x_0)-\inf f\le\Delta$. Each call returns $g(x,Z_t)$ at the next state of one stationary real-valued chain with kernel $P$ and law $\pi$, where $\int\|P^t(z,\cdot)-\pi\|_{\rm TV}\,d\pi(z)\le 2e^{-t/\tau}$ for every $t$. The jointly measurable oracle satisfies $\mathbb E_\pi g(x,Z)=\nabla f(x)$ and $\mathbb E_\pi\|g(x,Z)-\nabla f(x)\|^2\le\sigma^2$ for every $x$. The target is $\mathbb E\|\nabla f(\hat x)\|\le\varepsilon$ for a measurable output of any randomized adaptive algorithm. In \textbf{(1)}, $L,\varepsilon>0$, $\tau\ge1$, $A\ge2^{26}$, $r\ge2^{16}$ and $d\ge2^{40}(1+A)^4(1+\tau r^2\log r)$.}\par\smallskip
