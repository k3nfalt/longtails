\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize A differentiable convex function $f$ on a real Hilbert space satisfies $f(y)\le f(x)+\langle\nabla f(x),y-x\rangle+\frac{L}{2}\|y-x\|^2$, with $L>0$. A minimizer $x_\star$ satisfies $\|x_0-x_\star\|\le R$, $R\ge0$. Every oracle reply obeys $\|g-\nabla f(x)\|\le\alpha\|\nabla f(x)\|$, $0\le\alpha<1$, for every call and seed. Target: $f(x_N)-f(x_\star)\le\varepsilon$, $\varepsilon>0$, almost surely under the execution law; \textbf{(1)} guarantees this for every seed. Parameters $L,\alpha,R,\varepsilon$ are supplied.}\par\smallskip
