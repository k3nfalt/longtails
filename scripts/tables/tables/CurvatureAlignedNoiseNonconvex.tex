\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, $f$ is differentiable, $f\geq f_{\rm low}$, and $f(x_0)-f_{\rm low}\leq\Delta$. For every $x,u$, a linear map $Q(x)$ satisfies $f(x+u)\leq f(x)+\langle\nabla f(x),u\rangle+\frac12\langle Q(x)u,u\rangle$ and $\langle Q(x)\nabla f(x),\nabla f(x)\rangle\leq L_g\|\nabla f(x)\|^2$. The jointly measurable oracle $g$ uses IID samples with law $P_0$, is integrable and unbiased, and has integrable noise moments satisfying $\mathbb E_{P_0}\|\zeta\|^2\leq\sigma^2$ and $\mathbb E_{P_0}\langle Q(x)\zeta,\zeta\rangle\leq L_\xi\mathbb E_{P_0}\|\zeta\|^2$. Here $L_g>0$, $L_\xi,\sigma,\Delta\geq0$, $\varepsilon>0$. Target: measurable output with integrable gradient norm and $\int\|\nabla f(\widehat x)\|\,dP\leq\varepsilon$. \textbf{(1)} constructs constant-step SGD with a uniformly random iterate; the setup imposes no algorithm restriction. Prior costs are converted from expected squared-gradient accuracy to this target.}\par\smallskip
