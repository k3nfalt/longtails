\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, $f$ is differentiable, $f\ge f_{\rm low}$, and $f(x_0)-f_{\rm low}\le\Delta$. For every $x,u$, a linear map $Q(x)$ satisfies $f(x+u)\le f(x)+\langle\nabla f(x),u\rangle+\frac12\langle Q(x)u,u\rangle$ and $\langle Q(x)\nabla f(x),\nabla f(x)\rangle\le L_g\|\nabla f(x)\|^2$. IID samples have law $P_0$; the jointly measurable oracle $g$ is unbiased, with $\zeta=g-\nabla f$, $\int\|\zeta\|^2\,dP_0\le\sigma^2$, and $\int\langle Q(x)\zeta,\zeta\rangle\,dP_0\le L_\xi\int\|\zeta\|^2\,dP_0$; these integrands and $g$ are integrable. Here $L_g>0$, $L_\xi,\sigma,\Delta\ge0$, $\varepsilon>0$. Target: $\int\|\nabla f(\hat x)\|\,dP_{\rm out}\le\varepsilon$. The setup does not restrict the algorithm; \textbf{(1)} constructs constant-step SGD.}\par\smallskip
