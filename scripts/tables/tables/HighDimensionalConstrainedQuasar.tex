\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize
Compact convex $X\subset\mathbb R^d$, $x_0,x^\star\in X$, $\operatorname{diam}(X)\le D$, and $x^\star\in\arg\min_X f$.
On $X$, $f$ is differentiable, $\nabla f$ is $L$-Lipschitz, and $\gamma(f(x)-f(x^\star))\le\langle\nabla f(x),x-x^\star\rangle$.
The jointly measurable gradient oracle uses IID Boolean samples, is unbiased on $X$, and has integrable squared error with variance at most $\sigma^2$; the other channel gives exact Euclidean projections. No algorithm restriction is imposed; gradient replies outside $X$ are unrestricted.
Here $L,D,\varepsilon>0$, $0<\gamma\le1$, $\sigma\ge0$, $0<\delta\le1/4$, $LD^2/\varepsilon\ge10^8$, $\sigma=0$ or $\sigma D\ge64\gamma\varepsilon$, and $d\ge2^{34}(B+1)^5$.
Target: $P\{\hat x\in X,\ f(\hat x)-f(x^\star)\le\varepsilon\}\ge1-\delta$ under the IID execution law $P$.
