\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On the closed Euclidean unit ball $B\subset\mathbb R^d$, $f\in C^1(\mathbb R^d)$ is $\mu$-strongly convex, $L$-smooth and $G$-Lipschitz. Each pair $x,y\in B$ yields one sign from a jointly measurable oracle with fresh IID noise and mean $\rho(f(x)-f(y))$. The unknown odd, nondecreasing $C^1$ link satisfies $|\rho|\le1$ and $c|t|^k\le\rho'(t)\le M$ for $|t|\le r$. The parameters are public, with $d\ge1$, integer $k\ge0$ and $\mu,L,G,r,c,M,\varepsilon>0$. The target is $\mathbb E[f(\hat x)-\min_B f]\le\varepsilon$ for a measurable $\hat x\in B$.}\par\smallskip

\textbf{(2)} Epoch-PDD \citep[Thm.~4]{pmlr-v267-saha25a}
& Derived bound on $B$ for expected gap, using tolerance $\varepsilon/2$ and $\delta=\varepsilon/(4G)$:

\textbf{(3)} NC-SMD \citep[Thms.~4, 13]{ftdKumagai2017v2}
& Derived bound on $B$ with full-range slope bounds ($k=0$):
$\mathcal{O}\!\big((A+H)\log(e+A+H)$ $+AH+GM_0/(m\varepsilon)\big)$.
& \textbf{(1)}: Removes the accuracy logarithm at fixed parameters. Allows $C^1$ objectives and locally controlled flat links. For $\rho=\tanh$, $r=1$ and $\max_B f-\min_B f\asymp G$, dependence on large $G$ is polynomial versus exponential.
& \textbf{(1)}: The $\varepsilon^{-2}$ coefficient can be larger for small $G$ and large $ML/(c\mu)$. \textbf{(3)} also bounds online regret on compact convex domains with a barrier, assuming $C^2$ objectives and a thrice differentiable link with positive, nonincreasing slope on the positive gap range. \\

& \textbf{(1)}: Covers constrained minima with in-ball comparisons. Rates are not directly comparable: \textbf{(4)} assumes unconstrained optimization on $\mathbb R^d$.
& \textbf{(1)} needs a fixed link. \textbf{(4)} requires only a lower margin on the probability of a correct comparison. \\
