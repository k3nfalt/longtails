\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize Let $f:E\times F\to\mathbb{R}$ be differentiable and convex--concave on real Hilbert spaces. Its partial gradients have within-block Lipschitz bounds $L_x,L_y\ge0$ and cross-block bound $L_{xy}\ge0$, with $L_x+L_{xy},L_y+L_{xy}>0$. A saddle $z_\star$ satisfies $\|z_0-z_\star\|\le R$, $R\ge0$. Exact partial-gradient queries are available. The setup restricts algorithms to constant-step Alex-GDA with $1<\gamma\le2$, $\delta\ge1$. Target: $\|\nabla_x f(\widehat z)\|^2+\|\nabla_y f(\widehat z)\|^2\le\varepsilon^2$, $\varepsilon>0$, deterministically at the selected iterate.}\par\smallskip
