\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On real Hilbert spaces $E\times F$, $f$ is differentiable and convex--concave. Its partial gradients have within-block Lipschitz bounds $L_x,L_y\ge0$ and cross-block bound $L_{xy}\ge0$, with $L_x+L_{xy},L_y+L_{xy}>0$. A saddle $z^\star$ satisfies $\|z_0-z^\star\|\le R$, where $R\ge0$. Exact partial-gradient queries are available. The setup requires constant-step Alex-GDA with $1<\gamma\le2$, $\delta\ge1$. The target is $\|\nabla_x f(\widehat z)\|^2+\|\nabla_y f(\widehat z)\|^2\le\varepsilon^2$, $\varepsilon>0$; \textbf{(1)} achieves it deterministically at a selected iterate. Other algorithm families below compare the underlying problem and oracle on the stated restrictions.}\par\smallskip
