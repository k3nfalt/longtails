Access: exact projection and jointly measurable IID stochastic gradients, with $\int g(x,\omega)\,dp=\nabla f(x)$ and $\int\|g(x,\omega)-\nabla f(x)\|^2\,dp\le\sigma^2$. Target: $P\{\widehat x\in X,\ f(\widehat x)-f(x^\star)\le\varepsilon\}\ge1-\delta$, $\varepsilon>0$, $0<\delta<1$.}\par\smallskip
