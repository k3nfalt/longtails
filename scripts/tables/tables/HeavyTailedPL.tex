The jointly measurable oracle is unbiased, with uniformly bounded central moments $\mathbb E\|g(x,\xi)-\nabla f(x)\|^2\leq\sigma^2$ and $\mathbb E\|g(x,\xi)-\nabla f(x)\|^q\leq\nu^q$, where $\sigma,\nu>0$ and $q>2$; samples are IID. The setup restricts algorithms to plain SGD with positive deterministic steps and last-iterate output. Target: $P\{f(x_T)-\inf f>\varepsilon\}\leq\delta$, for $\varepsilon>0$, $0<\delta<1$.
