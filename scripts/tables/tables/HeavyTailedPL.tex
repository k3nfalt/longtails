\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On a separable real Hilbert space with its Borel structure, $f$ is differentiable, bounded below, globally $L$-smooth and satisfies $2\mu(f(x)-f_\star)\le\|\nabla f(x)\|^2$, where $0<\mu\le L$ and $f(x_0)-f_\star\le\Delta$. The jointly measurable, unbiased oracle has IID samples and uniform central moments $\mathbb{E}\|g(x,\xi)-\nabla f(x)\|^2\le\sigma^2$ and $\mathbb{E}\|g(x,\xi)-\nabla f(x)\|^q\le\nu^q$, with $\sigma,\nu>0$, $q>2$. The setup requires plain SGD with positive deterministic steps and last-iterate output. Target: $\mathbb{P}\{f(x_T)-f_\star>\varepsilon\}\le\delta$, $\varepsilon>0$, $0<\delta<1$.}\par\smallskip
