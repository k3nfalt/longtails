\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize
Let $f\colon\mathbb R^d\to\mathbb R$ have an $L$-Lipschitz gradient and $f(x_0)-\inf f\le\Delta$, with $L>0$ and $\varepsilon>0$. The jointly measurable stochastic gradient uses IID real seeds, is unbiased, and has variance at most $\sigma^2$ ($\sigma\ge0$). The target for a measurable output $\hat x$ is $\Pr(\|\nabla f(\hat x)\|\le\varepsilon)\ge1-\delta$. The setup requires $0<\delta\le1/4$, $L\Delta\ge 2^{54}\varepsilon^2$, and $d\ge 2^{40}(1+B)^4$.}
