\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize A separable real Banach space satisfies $\|x+y\|^s\leq\|x\|^s+s j(x)y+K\|y\|^s$ for some continuous-linear-functional-valued $j$, $1<s\leq2$, $K\geq1$. The whole-space map $T$ is nonexpansive, with $Tx_\star=x_\star$ and $\|x_0-x_\star\|\leq D$. A jointly measurable IID oracle $G(x,\xi)$ has integrable values, mean $Tx$, and integrable noise moment $\int\|G(x,\xi)-Tx\|^s\,d\mu\leq\sigma^s$. Here $D,\varepsilon>0$, $\sigma\geq0$. Target: measurable output $\hat x$ with integrable residual and $\int\|\hat x-T\hat x\|\,dP\leq\varepsilon$, where $P$ is the IID sampling law.}\par\smallskip
