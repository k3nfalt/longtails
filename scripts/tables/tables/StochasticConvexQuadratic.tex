\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize
On a finite-dimensional real Hilbert space, $f(x)=c+\|A(x-u)\|^2/2$ is convex with $L$-Lipschitz gradient and a minimizer $x_\star$ satisfying $\|x_0-x_\star\|\le R$. Samples are IID from $p$; the jointly measurable oracle $g$ satisfies $\int g(x,\omega)\,dp=\nabla f(x)$ and $\int\|g(x,\omega)-\nabla f(x)\|^2\,dp\le\sigma^2$, with integrable gradients and squared errors. Known parameters satisfy $L,\varepsilon>0$ and $R,\sigma\ge0$. The setup requires predetermined nonnegative scalar-step batched SGD; \textbf{(1)} constructs Chebyshev steps and targets $\int[f(x_{\rm out})-f(x_\star)]\,dP\le\varepsilon$ for its last iterate. Rows \textbf{(2)}--\textbf{(4)} compare problem-class guarantees outside this algorithm family.}\par\smallskip
