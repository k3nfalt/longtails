\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On Euclidean $\mathbb{R}^d$, $f$ has a continuously differentiable gradient, $f\ge m$, $f(x_0)-m\le\Delta$, and $\|\nabla^2 f(x)\|\le L_0+L_1\|\nabla f(x)\|$. Jointly measurable, integrable, unbiased gradient and Hessian-vector estimators $g(x,\xi),h(x,v,\xi)$ use IID samples of law $P_0$, with joint sample law $P$. For every $x,v$, their integrable central moments satisfy $\mathbb{E}_{P_0}\|g-\nabla f(x)\|^p\le\sigma_g^p+\alpha_g^p\|\nabla f(x)\|^p$ and $\mathbb{E}_{P_0}\|h-\nabla^2 f(x)v\|^q\le(\sigma_h^q+\alpha_h^q\|\nabla f(x)\|^q)\|v\|^q$, where $1<p,q\le2$. All scale parameters and $\Delta$ are nonnegative; $\varepsilon>0$. Target: a measurable returned point $X$ with $\int\|\nabla f(X)\|\,dP\le\varepsilon$. Costs count every gradient or HVP reply, including initialization, batches, guards and validation.}\par\smallskip
