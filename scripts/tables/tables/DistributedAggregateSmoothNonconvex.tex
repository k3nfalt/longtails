\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize The $n\ge1$ differentiable functions $f_i$ on $\mathbb R^d$ have an $L$-smooth average $F=n^{-1}\sum_i f_i$ with $F(x_0)-\inf F\le\Delta$. Component smoothness, gradient magnitudes and worker disagreement are unrestricted. Worker $i$ takes $h_i>0$ per unbiased gradient sample of $f_i$, with variance at most $\sigma_i^2$ and a jointly measurable oracle. Samples are IID within workers and independent across workers. On the dedicated star, each worker and each directed hub--worker link handles one request at a time, concurrently across resources. A $q$-bit transmission takes $\ell+bq$. The hub targets $\mathbb E\|\nabla F(X)\|\le\varepsilon$ under the execution law.}\par\smallskip
