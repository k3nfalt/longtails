\caption{Mixed relative and smooth-increment noise: \href{https://github.com/k3nfalt/longtails/blob/main/LongTails/Setups/MixedRelativeSmoothNonconvex.lean}{Mixed\allowbreak{}Relative\allowbreak{}Smooth\allowbreak{}Nonconvex}.}\label{tab:MixedRelativeSmoothNonconvex}
\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize
On a second-countable real Hilbert space with its Borel measurable structure, $f$ is differentiable, $\nabla f$ is $L$-Lipschitz, and $f(x_0)-\inf f\le\Delta$.
The jointly measurable oracle is unbiased under a probability law $p$; its square-integrable centered error satisfies, for every $x,y$,
$\int\|e(x,\omega)\|^2\,dp\le r\|\nabla f(x)\|^2+\sigma^2$ and
$\int\|e(x,\omega)-e(y,\omega)\|^2\,dp\le r(\|\nabla f(x)\|^2+\|\nabla f(y)\|^2)+M^2\|x-y\|^2$.
Here $L,\varepsilon>0$ and $M,r,\sigma,\Delta\ge0$ are supplied parameters. Independent samples are reused within consecutive call pairs. The target is a measurable output $X$ with integrable gradient norm and $\int\|\nabla f(X)\|\,d\mathbb{P}\le\varepsilon$, where $\mathbb{P}$ includes oracle and algorithm randomness.
