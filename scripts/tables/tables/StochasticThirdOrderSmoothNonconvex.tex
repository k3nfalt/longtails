\caption{Sufficient stochastic-gradient budgets for \href{https://github.com/k3nfalt/longtails/blob/main/LongTails/Setups/StochasticThirdOrderSmoothNonconvex.lean}{third-order smooth nonconvex optimization}.}\label{tab:StochasticThirdOrderSmoothNonconvex}
\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, $f\in C^3$ is nonconvex in general, with $L_1$-Lipschitz gradient, $L_2$-Lipschitz Hessian and $L_3$-Lipschitz third derivative. The initial gap satisfies $f(x_0)-\inf f\le\Delta$. Each query uses a fresh IID sample from a jointly measurable unbiased gradient oracle with variance at most $\sigma^2$. The target is a measurable output $\hat x$ with $\mathbb E\|\nabla f(\hat x)\|\le\varepsilon$. Here $L_i,\varepsilon>0$ and $\Delta,\sigma\ge0$.}\par\smallskip

& \textbf{(1)}: Tuning needs $L_1,L_2,L_3,\sigma,\Delta,\varepsilon$; \textbf{(3)} has parameter-free updates at $\alpha=2$ and needs no $L_2$ bound. \\

& \textbf{(1)}: No established improvement for the heavy-tailed guarantee.
& \textbf{(1)}: Requires finite variance; \textbf{(4)} only requires a bounded $\alpha$th central moment and needs no $L_2$ bound. \\

\textbf{(5)} NIGT \citep{shncscCutkosky2020}, Thm.~3
& $\mathcal{O}\!\left(\begin{aligned}

 &+\sigma^{13/3}/(\Delta^{4/3}L_2^{2/3}\varepsilon^{7/3})
\end{aligned}\right)$
& \textbf{(1)}: On the common $C^3$ class with $\Delta,\sigma>0$, the third-order term improves the $\varepsilon^{-7/2}$ noise term when $L_3^2\varepsilon<L_2^3$, up to constants. For fixed positive parameters, the leading power improves to $\varepsilon^{-10/3}$.
& \textbf{(1)}: Needs Lipschitz third derivative in addition to a Lipschitz Hessian and uses two evaluations per step on the overlap; \textbf{(5)} uses one. \\
