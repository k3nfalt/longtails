\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On a separable real Hilbert space, $F$ is $L$-Lipschitz ($L>0$) and has a zero $x_\star$ with $\|x_0-x_\star\|\le R$. The jointly measurable oracle uses IID samples $\xi$, with $\mathbb E_\xi g(x,\xi)=F(x)$ and $\mathbb E_\xi\|g(x,\xi)-F(x)\|^2\le\sigma^2$. The target is $\mathbb E\|F(\hat x)\|\le\varepsilon$, for $\varepsilon>0$.}\par\smallskip
