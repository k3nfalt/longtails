\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On $\mathbb R^d$, each $f_i$ is convex with public gradient Lipschitz bound $L_i$. The mean $F=n^{-1}\sum_i f_i$ is $\bar L$-smooth and satisfies $F(x)-\min F\ge(\mu/2)\operatorname{dist}(x,X_*)^2$ and $n^{-1}\sum_i\|\nabla f_i(x)\|^2\le\rho\|\nabla F(x)\|^2$, with $\mu>0$ and $\rho\ge1$. A minimizer lies within $R$ of $x_0$. Jointly measurable worker gradients are unbiased with variance at most $\sigma^2$, with IID samples within workers and independence across workers. In the finite-bit coordinator star, each worker handles one gradient at a time in $h_i>0$ time and each directed link handles one packet at a time at latency $\ell$ and cost $b$ per bit. Distinct workers and directed links can run concurrently. The target is $\mathbb E\operatorname{dist}(X,X_*)^2\le\varepsilon$ for the returned point.}\par\smallskip
