\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize $X\subset E$ and $Y\subset F$ are nonempty compact convex subsets of real Hilbert spaces. The function $\varphi$ is convex--concave on $X\times Y$, with partial gradients everywhere and globally $L$-Lipschitz field $G=(\nabla_x\varphi,-\nabla_y\varphi)$ in the max product norm; $L>0$ and $\operatorname{diam}(X\times Y)\le D$, $D\ge0$. The oracle returns exact $G$. Target: a feasible output $(\bar x,\bar y)$ with $\sup_{y\in Y}\varphi(\bar x,y)-\inf_{x\in X}\varphi(x,\bar y)\le\varepsilon$, $\varepsilon>0$ (also implying expected-gap accuracy).}\par\smallskip
