\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize Nonempty compact convex sets $X\subset E$, $Y\subset F$ in real Hilbert spaces have product diameter at most $D\ge0$. The function $\varphi:E\times F\to\mathbb R$ is convex--concave on $X\times Y$, with partial gradients everywhere; the exact oracle $G=(\nabla_x\varphi,-\nabla_y\varphi)$ is globally $L$-Lipschitz, $L>0$. For $\varepsilon>0$, the target is a feasible output with $\int\operatorname{Gap}(z(\omega))\,dP(\omega)\le\varepsilon$. Result \textbf{(1)} constructs deterministic projected extragradient with step $1/(2L)$ and averages its extrapolated points; its gap is at most $\varepsilon$ for every seed.}\par\smallskip
