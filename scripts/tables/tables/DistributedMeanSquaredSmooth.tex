\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize The differentiable objective $f:\mathbb R^d\to\mathbb R$ satisfies $f(x_0)-\inf f\le\Delta$. Worker streams are independent, with IID samples of law $\mu_i$ within worker $i$. Its jointly measurable oracle satisfies $\mathbb E_{\mu_i}g_i(x,\xi)=\nabla f(x)$, $\mathbb E_{\mu_i}\|g_i(x,\xi)-\nabla f(x)\|^2\le\sigma^2$, and $\mathbb E_{\mu_i}\|g_i(x,\xi)-g_i(y,\xi)\|^2\le L^2\|x-y\|^2$. On the coordinator star, worker $i$ takes $2h_i>0$ per same-sample pair. A $q$-bit message takes $\ell+bq$. Each worker handles one computation and each directed link one message at a time; distinct links and computations can overlap. The target is $\mathbb E\|\nabla f(X)\|^2\le\varepsilon^2$ under the execution law, with almost surely finitely many events through the deadline.}\par\smallskip
