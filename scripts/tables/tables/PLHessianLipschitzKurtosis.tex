\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, $f$ has an attained minimum, an $L$-Lipschitz gradient, a differentiable gradient with $\rho$-Lipschitz Hessian, and $2\mu(f(x)-f(x_\star))\le\|\nabla f(x)\|^2$ globally. Here $0<\mu\le L$, $\rho,\sigma,\Delta\ge0$, $K\ge1$, $\varepsilon>0$, and $f(x_0)-f(x_\star)\le\Delta$. The jointly measurable oracle uses IID samples of law $P_0$, with $\int g(x,\omega)\,dP_0=\nabla f(x)$ and integrable central moments of orders two and four bounded by $\sigma^2$ and $K\sigma^4$. The setup requires constant-step SGD with a uniformly sampled final-window output $\widehat x$; the target is $\int(f(\widehat x)-f(x_\star))\,dP\le\varepsilon$, where $P$ is its execution law.}\par\smallskip
