\caption{Stochastic optimization under \href{https://github.com/k3nfalt/longtails/blob/main/LongTails/Setups/PLHessianLipschitzKurtosis.lean}{PLHessianLipschitzKurtosis}.}\label{tab:PLHessianLipschitzKurtosis}
\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, $f$ has an attained minimum $f_\star=f(x_\star)$, an $L$-Lipschitz gradient, and a differentiable gradient with $\rho$-Lipschitz Hessian. Globally, $2\mu(f-f_\star)\leq\|\nabla f\|^2$, with $0<\mu\leq L$ and $f(x_0)-f_\star\leq\Delta$. The jointly measurable oracle uses IID samples of law $p_0$: $\int g(x,\omega)\,dp_0=\nabla f(x)$ and, for $e=g-\nabla f$, $\int\|e\|^2dp_0\leq\sigma^2$, $\int\|e\|^4dp_0\leq K\sigma^4$. Here $\rho,\sigma,\Delta\geq0$, $K\geq1$. The setup requires constant-step SGD with a uniformly sampled final-window iterate $\hat x$; the target is $\int(f(\hat x)-f_\star)\,dP\leq\varepsilon$, $\varepsilon>0$, under its execution probability law $P$.}\par\smallskip
