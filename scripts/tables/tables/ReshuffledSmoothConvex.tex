\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On a real Hilbert space with its Borel measurable structure, let $f=n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^n f_i$, $n\geq1$, with convex differentiable $f_i$ and $\ell_i$-Lipschitz gradients, $0\leq\ell_i\leq L_{\max}$, $n^{-1}\sum_i\ell_i\leq L_{\mathrm{avg}}$, and $0<L_{\mathrm{avg}}\leq L_{\max}$. A minimizer $x_\star$ satisfies $\|x_0-x_\star\|\leq R$ and $n^{-1}\sum_i\|\nabla f_i(x_\star)\|^2\leq\sigma^2$, with $R,\sigma\geq0$. The oracle returns exact component gradients. The setup restricts algorithms to random reshuffling; \textbf{(1)} uses fresh independent uniform permutations and horizon-dependent linear decay. Target: $\int [f(x_{\mathrm{out}}(\omega))-f(x_\star)]\,dP(\omega)\leq\varepsilon$, $\varepsilon>0$, under the algorithm's randomization law $P$, with measurable output and integrable gap.}\par\smallskip
