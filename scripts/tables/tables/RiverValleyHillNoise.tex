Here $P,Q$ project onto $K,K^\perp$; $A$ is self-adjoint, preserves $K$, and $\mu I\preceq A|_K\preceq L_vI$.
The gradient and Hessian of $h$ are $L_h$- and $\rho_h$-Lipschitz, $\|\nabla h\|\le G$;
$\phi$ takes values in $K$, with $J_\phi,DJ_\phi$ respectively $L_J,\rho_J$-Lipschitz and $\|AJ_\phi Q\|\le\Gamma\mu$.
Also $f(x_0)-\inf f\le\Delta$, $0<\mu\le L_v$, $\varepsilon>0$, and remaining bounds are nonnegative.
Require $16\Gamma\le1$, $64c\le\mu$, $64d\sigma_u\le\mu L_v$, and $32L_h\log R_0\le\mu$.
The jointly measurable, integrable oracle is unbiased with IID samples and projected error second moments bounded by $\sigma_u^2,\sigma_v^2$ on $K^\perp,K$.
The setup restricts execution to two-phase step-decay SGD: a uniformly stopped first phase followed by a fixed cooldown, tuned with public constants but without $K,\phi,A$.
Target: $\int\|\nabla f(x_{\rm out}(\omega))\|\,d\mathbb P(\omega)\le\varepsilon$, for its terminal output and joint sampling/stopping law $\mathbb P$.
