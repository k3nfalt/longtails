\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On a second-countable real Hilbert space with its Borel structure, each $f_k$ is differentiable, $\mu$-strongly convex and $L$-smooth, $0<\mu\le L$. Deterministic minimizers satisfy $\|x_0-x_0^*\|\le R$, $\|x_1^*-x_0^*\|\le V$, and $\|x_{k+2}^*-2x_{k+1}^*+x_k^*\|\le\Gamma$. Jointly measurable stochastic gradients are unbiased, with integrable squared error bounded by $\sigma^2$ uniformly in the query; seeds are independent with common law $p$. Here $R,V,\Gamma,\sigma\ge0$, $\varepsilon>0$, and $9792\Gamma^2(2L/\mu+99\sigma^2/(\mu^2\varepsilon))^4\le\varepsilon$. The constructed velocity tracker guarantees $\int\|x_N(\omega)-x_{N-1}^*\|^2\,d p^{\otimes\mathbb{N}}(\omega)\le\varepsilon$ at every $N$ beyond its budget. Updates use $\mu,L,\sigma,\varepsilon$, not $R,V,\Gamma$; the setup restricts the output clock, not the update family.}\par\smallskip
