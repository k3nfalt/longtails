\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On a separable real Hilbert space $E$ with its Borel structure, $f$ is differentiable, $\nabla f$ is $L$-Lipschitz, and $f(x_0)-f(x)\le\Delta$ for every $x$. The jointly measurable oracle $g$ has IID samples with marginal law $p$ and joint law $P$, with $\int g(x,\omega)\,dp=\nabla f(x)$, $\int\|g(x,\omega)-\nabla f(x)\|^2\,dp\le\sigma^2$, and $\int\|g(x,\omega)-g(y,\omega)\|^2\,dp\le M\|x-y\|^{2\nu}$, with these integrands integrable. Here $L,\varepsilon>0$, $M,\sigma,\Delta\ge0$, and $\nu\in[1/2,1]$. Each pair query evaluates two points using the same sample. Target: $\int\|\nabla f(x_{\mathrm{out}})\|\,dP\le\varepsilon$.}\par\smallskip
