\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, $F$ is differentiable, $L_1$-Lipschitz, and has an $L_2$-Lipschitz Jacobian, with $L_1,L_2>0$. There exists $x_\star$ with $F(x_\star)=0$, $\|x_0-x_\star\|\le R$, and $\langle F(x),x-x_\star\rangle\ge-\rho\|F(x)\|^2/2$ for every $x$, where $R,\rho\ge0$ and $\rho L_1<2$. Exact value and full-Jacobian queries are available. Target: $\|F(x_{\rm out})\|\le\varepsilon$ almost surely, $\varepsilon>0$. The constructed algorithm needs no $R$ input.}\par\smallskip
