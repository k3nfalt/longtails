\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, $F$ is differentiable, $L_1$-Lipschitz, and has an $L_2$-Lipschitz Jacobian, with $L_1,L_2>0$. A root $x^\star$ satisfies $\|x_0-x^\star\|\le R$ and $\langle F(x),x-x^\star\rangle\ge-\frac{\rho}{2}\|F(x)\|^2$ for every $x$, where $R,\rho\ge0$ and $\rho L_1<2$. Exact value and full-Jacobian queries are available. The target is $\|F(x_{\mathrm{out}})\|\le\varepsilon$, $\varepsilon>0$, almost surely.}\par\smallskip
