\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On a real Hilbert space with its Borel measurable structure, let $F=n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^n f_i$, $n\ge1$, with convex $f_i$ and $L_i$-Lipschitz gradients, $0\le L_i\le L_{\max}$ and $n^{-1}\sum_iL_i\le L_{\rm avg}\le L_{\max}$, $L_{\rm avg}>0$. A minimizer $x_\star$ satisfies $\|x_0-x_\star\|\le R$ and $n^{-1}\sum_i\|\nabla f_i(x_\star)\|^2\le\sigma^2$, where $R,\sigma\ge0$. The setup requires constant positive-step reshuffling; each epoch uses a fresh independent uniform permutation and exact component gradients. The target is $\int [F(x_{nK}(\omega))-F(x_\star)]\,dP(\omega)\le\varepsilon$, $\varepsilon>0$, for the measurable raw final iterate and its integrable gap. Costs count all component-gradient calls. Prior-work comparisons below are on the common Euclidean case $E=\mathbb{R}^d$; alternative methods need not satisfy the setup's algorithm restriction.}\par\smallskip
