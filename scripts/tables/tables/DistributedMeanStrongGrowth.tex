\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On \(\mathbb R^d\), the \(n\) local losses \(f_i\) are differentiable and convex. Their mean \(F\) is \(L\)-smooth and satisfies \(\mu\)-quadratic growth to \(\mathcal S=\arg\min F\), with \(0<\mu\le L\) and \(\operatorname{dist}(x_0,\mathcal S)\le R\). Mean strong growth is \(\sum_i\|\nabla f_i(x)\|^2\le n\rho\|\nabla F(x)\|^2\) for \(\rho\ge1\). Jointly measurable unbiased stochastic gradients have variance at most \(\sigma^2\), with IID samples within workers and independent streams across workers. Workers compute in parallel, one gradient at a time, taking \(h_i>0\) per gradient. Only hub--worker links carry messages, at most one in flight per directed link, with latency \(\ell\) and time \(b\) per bit. The output satisfies \(\mathbb E[\operatorname{dist}(X,\mathcal S)^2]\le\varepsilon\) for \(\varepsilon>0\).}\par\smallskip
