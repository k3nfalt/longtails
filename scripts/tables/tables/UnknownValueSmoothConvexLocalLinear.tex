\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize In a real Hilbert space, $C$ is closed and convex, $x_0\in C$, and $f$ is differentiable on $C$ with the convex lower model and quadratic upper model of curvature $L\ge0$. A minimizer $x_\star\in C$ satisfies $\|x_0-x_\star\|\le R$ and $f(x_0)-f(x_\star)\le\Delta$, with $R,\Delta\ge0$. Access comprises exact value--gradient queries and exact point-only minimization of $\langle g,v\rangle$ over $C\cap\overline B(x,r)$, with arbitrary ties. Inputs are $x_0,R$ and $\varepsilon>0$; $L,\Delta$ appear only in the budget. The target is feasible $y$ with $f(y)-f(x_\star)\le\varepsilon$; \textbf{(1)} returns a best-observed point for every seed. The gap bound $\Delta$ need not be bounded by $LR^2$.}\par\smallskip
