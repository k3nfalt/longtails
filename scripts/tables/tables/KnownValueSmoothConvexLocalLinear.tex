\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize A closed convex set $C$ in a real Hilbert space, $x_0\in C$, and a differentiable objective satisfying, for $x,y\in C$, $f(x)+\langle\nabla f(x),y-x\rangle\le f(y)\le f(x)+\langle\nabla f(x),y-x\rangle+\tfrac L2\|y-x\|^2$. Known inputs: $L>0$, $R\ge0$, $\varepsilon>0$, and the exact minimum $f_\star=f(x_\star)$, with $x_\star\in\arg\min_C f$ and $\|x_0-x_\star\|\le R$. Exact value--gradient and point-only local linear oracles are supplied.
