\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize $L>0$, $A,B,C\ge0$, $\varepsilon>0$; $f:\mathbb R^d\to\mathbb R$ has $L$-Lipschitz gradient, finite $f_\star=\inf f$, and $f(x_0)-f_\star\le\Delta$. The jointly measurable IID oracle $g(x,\xi)$ is integrable and unbiased, with integrable squared error satisfying $\mathbb E_p\|g(x,\xi)-\nabla f(x)\|^2\le2A(f(x)-f_\star)+B\|\nabla f(x)\|^2+C$. Target: $\mathbb E_P\|\nabla f(\hat x)\|\le\varepsilon$, including algorithm randomness. Validity: $2^{26}\varepsilon^2\le L\Delta$ and $d\ge2^{40}\bigl(1+\frac{L\Delta}{\varepsilon^2}(1+B+\frac{A\Delta+C}{\varepsilon^2})\bigr)^4$.
