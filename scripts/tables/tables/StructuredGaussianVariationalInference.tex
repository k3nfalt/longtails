\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On $\mathbb R^d$, $V$ is differentiable, $\nabla V$ is $L$-Lipschitz and $\mu$-strongly monotone, $0<\mu\le L$. Fix $S_i\subseteq\{1,\ldots,i\}$ containing $i$, with $|S_i|\le w$. The family $\mathcal G_S$ has $C_{ii}>0$ and $C_{ij}=0$ outside $S_i$. A family minimizer $\lambda_\star=(m_\star,C_\star)$ exists, $\lambda_0\in\mathcal G_S$, and $\|m_0-m_\star\|^2+\|C_0-C_\star\|_F^2\le R^2$, $R\ge0$. With exact pointwise $\nabla V$ access, the target is $\mathbb E[F(\widehat\lambda)-F(\lambda_\star)]\le\varepsilon$, $\varepsilon>0$, where $F(m,C)=\mathbb E_{\xi\sim\mathcal N(0,I_d)}V(m+C\xi)-\sum_i\log C_{ii}$. Row \textbf{(1)} constructs single-sample masked proximal SGD with a weighted-average output; the setup imposes no algorithm restriction. Distance comparisons use $\varepsilon_d=2\varepsilon/\mu$: row \textbf{(1)} implies this distance target, whereas a distance guarantee alone does not imply the KL target.}\par\smallskip
