\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On a second-countable real Hilbert space with its Borel measurable structure, $f$ is differentiable, $\nabla f$ is $L$-Lipschitz, $f\geq f_{\rm low}$, and $f(x_0)-f_{\rm low}\leq\Delta$. The jointly measurable unbiased oracle $g$ uses IID samples from $p$, with integrable gradients and squared noise/differences, and satisfies $\mathbb E_p\|g(x)-g(y)\|^2\leq L^2\|x-y\|^2$ and $\mathbb E_p\|g(x)-\nabla f(x)\|^2\leq2A(f(x)-f_{\rm low})+C$. Here $L,\varepsilon>0$ and $A,C,\Delta\geq0$. Same-sample pairs are available. The target is a measurable output with $\int\|\nabla f(x_{\rm out}(\omega))\|\,dP(\omega)\leq\varepsilon$.}\par\smallskip
