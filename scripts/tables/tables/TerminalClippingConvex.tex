\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize
Let $X\subseteq\mathbb R^d$ be closed and convex, $f:\mathbb R^d\to\mathbb R$ measurable and convex on $X$, and $x_0,x_\star\in X$, with $x_\star$ minimizing $f$ on $X$ and $\|x_0-x_\star\|\le R$. Exact projection onto $X$ is available. IID samples of law $\nu$ under probability law $P$ drive a jointly measurable, square-integrable oracle with $m(x)=\int g(x,\omega)\,d\nu(\omega)$ satisfying $f(x)-f(y)\le\langle m(x),x-y\rangle$ and $\int\|g(x,\omega)\|^2\,d\nu(\omega)\le B^2\|x-x_0\|^2+G^2$ for $x,y\in X$. Here $B,G,\varepsilon>0$, $R\ge0$, $0<\delta<1$. The setup restricts algorithms to projected clipped subgradient updates with nonnegative steps and thresholds. Result \textbf{(1)} constructs influence-dependent clipping and returns the actual last iterate $x_T$, with $P\{f(x_T)-f(x_\star)>\varepsilon\}\le\delta$.}\par\smallskip
