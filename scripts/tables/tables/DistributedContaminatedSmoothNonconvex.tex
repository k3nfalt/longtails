\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On $\mathbb R^d$, $F=n^{-1}\sum_i f_i$ averages differentiable local objectives, has $L$-Lipschitz gradient and initial gap at most $\Delta$. Honest workers sample independently across workers and IID locally from $(1-\eta_i)\mu_i+\eta_i\nu_i$, with arbitrary $\nu_i$ and $0\le\eta_i\le1/16$. Under $\mu_i$ the jointly measurable gradient oracle is unbiased for $\nabla f_i$ with variance at most $\sigma_i^2$. All setup parameters are public. Each worker has one batch in flight and pays $h_i>0$ per sample. The complete directed network allows concurrent links and serializes messages per link, with cost $\ell+bm$ for $m$ bits. The target is one measurable $X$ with $\Pr(\|\nabla F(X)\|>\varepsilon)\le\delta$, where $0<\delta<1$, $\varepsilon>0$ and $\varepsilon\ge2^{18}n^{-1}\sum_i\sigma_i\sqrt{\eta_i}$.}\par\smallskip
