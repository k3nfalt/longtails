\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize
$f:\mathbb{R}^d\to\mathbb{R}$ is differentiable with $L$-Lipschitz gradient and $f(x_0)-\inf f\le\Delta$; $L,\varepsilon>0$ and $\Lambda,\sigma,\Delta\ge0$. The jointly measurable, integrable oracle is unbiased and returns $(g(x,\xi),g(y,\xi))$ using the same sample, with IID samples of law $p$ under $P$. For $\zeta(x,\xi)=g(x,\xi)-\nabla f(x)$, $\int\|\zeta(x,\xi)\|\,dp\le\sigma$ and $\int\|\zeta(x,\xi)-\zeta(y,\xi)\|\,dp\le\Lambda\|x-y\|$; every coordinate of $\zeta(x,\xi)$ has a symmetric marginal distribution. Target: measurable output with integrable gradient norm and $\int\|\nabla f(x_{\rm out})\|\,dP\le\varepsilon$.}\par\smallskip
