\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize Convex differentiable $f$ on a separable real Hilbert space with its Borel measurable structure; globally $L$-Lipschitz gradient and a minimizer $x^\star$ with $\|x_0-x^\star\|\le R$. IID samples have law $\nu$; the jointly measurable oracle is integrable and satisfies $\int g(x,\omega)\,d\nu=\nabla f(x)$ and $\int\|g(x,\omega)-\nabla f(x)\|^p\,d\nu\le\sigma_0^p+\sigma^p\|\nabla f(x)\|^p$, with integrable moments. Here $L,R,\varepsilon>0$, $\sigma_0,\sigma\ge0$, $1<p\le2$, and $0<\delta<1$ are supplied parameters. Target: measurable output with $P\{f(\mathrm{out})-f(x^\star)>\varepsilon\}\le\delta$ under the IID execution law $P$. Costs count all individual stochastic-gradient replies. Literature comparisons are on the common finite-dimensional restriction.}\par\smallskip
