\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize In a finite-dimensional real normed space $E$, $F:E\to E^*$ is $L$-Lipschitz from the primal to the dual norm, $L>0$. A root $x_\star$ satisfies $\langle F(x),x-x_\star\rangle\ge-\rho\|F(x)\|_*^2$ for all $x$, with $0\le\rho L\le1/20$. The differentiable mirror $h$ satisfies $D_h(u,x)\ge(\mu/2)\|u-x\|^2$, $\mu>0$, and $D_h(x_\star,x_0)\le D$, $D\ge0$. Exact $F$ evaluations are counted; the target is $\|F(x_{\rm out})\|_*\le\varepsilon$, $\varepsilon>0$, almost surely.}\par\smallskip
