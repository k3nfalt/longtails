A minimizer $x_\star$ satisfies $\|x_0-x_\star\|\le R$ and $\int\|\nabla F_\xi(x_\star)\|^2\,dp(\xi)\le\sigma^2<\infty$; $L,\varepsilon>0$ and $R,\sigma\ge0$. Samples are IID under $P$. The setup restricts algorithms to linear-decay SGD; the target is $\int[f(x_T(\omega))-f(x_\star)]\,dP(\omega)\le\varepsilon$, with measurable output and integrable gap.}\par\smallskip
