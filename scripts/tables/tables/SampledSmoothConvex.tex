\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On a separable real Hilbert space with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra, let $f(x)=\int F_\xi(x)\,dp(\xi)$, with $p$ a probability law and IID samples. Each $F_\xi$ is convex and differentiable with $L$-Lipschitz gradient; sample gradients are jointly measurable and losses are integrable at every point. A minimizer $x_\star$ satisfies $\|x_0-x_\star\|\le R$ and $\int\|\nabla F_\xi(x_\star)\|^2\,dp(\xi)\le\sigma^2<\infty$. Here $L,\varepsilon>0$ and $R,\sigma\ge0$. Target: $\mathbb{E}[f(x_T)-f(x_\star)]\le\varepsilon$ for the last iterate.}\par\smallskip
