\caption{Sufficient and necessary comparison budgets for \href{https://github.com/k3nfalt/longtails/blob/main/LongTails/Setups/SphericalRandomUtility.lean}{spherical random utility}.}\label{tab:SphericalRandomUtility}
\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize Let $f$ be convex on the closed Euclidean ball $Q$ of radius $R>0$ centered at zero in dimension $d\ge2$. Each query $(x,y)\in Q^2$ uses one fresh IID draw $Z$ from an unknown spherically symmetric law and returns the jointly measurable bit $\mathbf{1}\{u_Z(x)\ge u_Z(y)\}$, where $u_Z(x)=f(x)+\langle Z,x\rangle$. With probability at least $1/2$, $u_Z$ is $B$-Lipschitz throughout $Q$. For $B>0$ and $0<\varepsilon\le BR/16$, the target is a feasible $\hat x$ with integrable gap and $\mathbb E[f(\hat x)-\min_Q f]\le\varepsilon$.}\par\smallskip
