\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize Convex $f\in C^3(\mathbb R^d)$ has globally $L_3$-Lipschitz third derivative; a minimizer $x_\star$ satisfies $\|x_0-x_\star\|\le R$ and $f(x_0)-f(x_\star)\le\Delta$. Known $L_3,R,\Delta,\varepsilon>0$ and $\sigma_g,\sigma_h\ge0$ tune (1). Jointly measurable oracles use fresh IID samples with law $p$: $\int g(x,\omega)\,dp=\nabla f(x)$, $\int\|g(x,\omega)-\nabla f(x)\|^2dp\le\sigma_g^2$, and $\int\|H(x,\omega)-\nabla^2f(x)\|^2dp\le\sigma_h^2$, with the corresponding integrability conditions. Hessians need not be unbiased, symmetric, or positive semidefinite. Target: measurable $\hat x\in\bar B(x_0,R)$ and $P\{f(\hat x)-f(x_\star)\le\varepsilon\}\ge2/3$ under the IID sampling law $P$.}\par\smallskip
