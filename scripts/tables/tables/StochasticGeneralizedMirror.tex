A differentiable mirror map on open convex $U$ satisfies $D_h(u,z)\ge\|u-z\|^2/2$ for $u\in Q$, $z\in Q\cap U$; measurable exact composite Bregman-prox updates stay in $Q\cap U$, and $x_0\in Q\cap U$. A minimizer $x^*\in Q$ satisfies $2D_h(x^*,x_0)\le R^2$, $F(x_0)-F(x^*)\le\Delta$, $\|df(x^*)\|_*\le G$. Jointly measurable stochastic gradients use IID samples with law $\mu$ under probability $P$, with integrable gradients and central moments, $\int g(x,\omega)\,d\mu=df(x)$ and $\int\|g(x,\omega)-df(x)\|_*^\alpha\,d\mu\le\sigma^\alpha$ on $Q$. Here $L_0,R,\Delta,\varepsilon>0$, $L_1,G,\sigma\ge0$, $1<\alpha\le2$, $0<\delta<1$. All displayed parameters are supplied to \textbf{(1)}.
