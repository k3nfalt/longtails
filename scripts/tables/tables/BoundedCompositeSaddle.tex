\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize Convex sets $X\subset E$, $Y\subset F$ in real inner-product spaces satisfy $\|x\|,\|y\|\le R$, with $(x_0,y_0)\in X\times Y$. The continuous linear map $K$ satisfies $\|K\|\le L$; $g,h$ are convex and $M$-Lipschitz on their respective sets. Here $R,L,\varepsilon>0$ and $M\ge0$. Exact calls to $K$, its adjoint, and the constrained proximal maps of $g,h$ are available. The setup requires positive constant-step Arrow--Hurwicz (primal update, then dual update). Our uniform averages are feasible and deterministically satisfy $\Phi(\bar x,y)-\Phi(x,\bar y)\le\varepsilon$ for every $(x,y)\in X\times Y$, where $\Phi(x,y)=g(x)+\langle y,Kx\rangle-h(y)$.}\par\smallskip
