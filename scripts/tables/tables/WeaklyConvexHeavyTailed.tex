\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize In a finite-dimensional real inner product space, $X$ is closed and convex, $x_0\in X$, and $f(y)\ge f(x)+\langle v(x),y-x\rangle-\frac{\rho}{2}\|y-x\|^2$ for $x,y\in X$, with $\|v(x)\|\le G$. A lower bound $\ell\le f|_X$ satisfies $f(x_0)-\ell\le\Delta$. The jointly measurable oracle uses IID samples, with $\mathbb{E} g(x,\xi)=v(x)$ and $\mathbb{E}\|g(x,\xi)-v(x)\|^\alpha\le\sigma^\alpha$ (the corresponding integrability holds); exact projection onto $X$ is available. Here $\rho,G,\varepsilon>0$, $\sigma,\Delta\ge0$, and $1<\alpha\le2$. Target: $\mathbb{E}\|2\rho(x_J-\widehat x_J)\|^2\le\varepsilon^2$ for a uniformly sampled iterate.}\par\smallskip
