\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize In a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, $X$ is closed and convex, $x_0\in X$, and the selected subgradient satisfies $f(y)\ge f(x)+\langle v(x),y-x\rangle-\rho\|y-x\|^2/2$ and $\|v(x)\|\le G$ on $X$. A lower bound $f_{\rm low}$ satisfies $f(x_0)-f_{\rm low}\le\Delta$. The jointly measurable oracle has $\int g(x,z)\,d\nu(z)=v(x)$ and $\int\|g(x,z)-v(x)\|^\alpha\,d\nu(z)\le\sigma^\alpha$, with integrable terms and IID samples of law $\nu$ under $P$; $\rho,G,\varepsilon>0$, $\sigma,\Delta\ge0$, $1<\alpha\le2$. Exact projection onto $X$ is supplied. The setup requires constant-step, constant-threshold projected clipped subgradient iterations. The target is $\int\|\nabla\varphi(x_J)\|^2\,d(P\otimes U)=\int4\rho^2\|x_J-\hat x_J\|^2\,d(P\otimes U)\le\varepsilon^2$, with an independent uniform iterate index $J\sim U$.}\par\smallskip
