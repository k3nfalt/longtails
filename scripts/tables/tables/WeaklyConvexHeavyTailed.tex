\caption{Weakly convex optimization with heavy-tailed noise (\href{https://github.com/k3nfalt/longtails/blob/main/LongTails/Setups/WeaklyConvexHeavyTailed.lean}{WeaklyConvexHeavyTailed}).}\label{tab:WeaklyConvexHeavyTailed}
\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize A finite-dimensional real inner-product space, closed convex $X$, $x_0\in X$, and a selection $v$ satisfying $f(y)\ge f(x)+\langle v(x),y-x\rangle-\rho\|y-x\|^2/2$ and $\|v(x)\|\le G$ on $X$. Here $\rho,G>0$, $f\ge f_{\rm low}$ on $X$, and $f(x_0)-f_{\rm low}\le\Delta$. The jointly measurable oracle uses IID samples with $\mathbb E g(x,\xi)=v(x)$ and $\mathbb E\|g(x,\xi)-v(x)\|^\alpha\le\sigma^\alpha$, $1<\alpha\le2$, $\sigma\ge0$, with these integrals finite; exact projection onto $X$ is available. The setup requires constant-step, constant-threshold projected clipped subgradient iterations. The target is $\mathbb E\|\nabla\phi(x_J)\|^2\le\varepsilon^2$ for a uniformly sampled iterate, $\varepsilon>0$.}\par\smallskip
