\caption{Stochastic fixed points in smooth norms: \href{https://github.com/k3nfalt/longtails/blob/main/LongTails/Setups/StochasticSmoothBanachFixedPoint.lean}{StochasticSmoothBanachFixedPoint}.}\label{tab:StochasticSmoothBanachFixedPoint}
\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize
A finite-dimensional real Banach space $E$ with its Borel measurable structure; $C\geq1$, $R,\sigma\geq0$, $\varepsilon>0$. There is a continuous $J:E\to E^*$ with $\|J(x)\|\leq\|x\|$ and $\|x+y\|^2\leq\|x\|^2+2J(x)[y]+C\|y\|^2$. The nonexpansive map $T:E\to E$ has $Tx_\star=x_\star$ and $\|x_0-x_\star\|\leq R$. A jointly measurable oracle $g$ satisfies $\int g(x,\xi)\,dp(\xi)=Tx$ and $\int\|g(x,\xi)-Tx\|^2\,dp(\xi)\leq\sigma^2$, with these integrands integrable. Samples are IID with marginal probability measure $p$ under probability measure $P$. Target: measurable output $Z$ with integrable squared residual and $\int\|TZ-Z\|^2\,dP\leq\varepsilon^2$. The construction uses $R,C,\sigma,\varepsilon$; the setup imposes no algorithm restriction.}\par\smallskip

& $\mathcal{O}_{\kappa,\log(1/\delta)}\!\left(\left[1+\frac{(R+\sigma)^2}{\varepsilon^2}+\frac{(R+\sigma)^6}{\varepsilon^5}\right]\right.$\newline
$\left.{}\cdot\operatorname{polylog}(2+(R+\sigma)/\varepsilon)\right)$.
Variance-only oracle; quadratically smoothable norm; anytime HP residual.
& \textbf{(1)}: on smooth native norms, fixed positive remaining parameters and constant confidence, $\varepsilon^{-5}$ becomes $\varepsilon^{-2}$ up to logarithms for the shared probability target.
& \textbf{(1)} gives no anytime HP guarantee; requires the native norm to be smooth, excluding merely equivalent smoothable norms such as $\ell_\infty$. \\

Samplewise nonexpansive oracle with same-seed queries; quadratically smoothable norm; anytime HP residual.

& \textbf{(1)} loses anytime HP accuracy and applicability to merely equivalent smoothable norms, including $\ell_\infty$. \\
