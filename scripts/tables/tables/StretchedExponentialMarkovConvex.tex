\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize Convex $L$-smooth $f:\mathbb{R}^d\to\mathbb{R}$, $d\ge1$, with a minimizer $x^\star$ satisfying $\|x_0-x^\star\|\le R$. A stationary real-state Markov chain with kernel $\kappa$ and invariant probability $\pi$ supplies jointly measurable gradients with $\int g(x,z)\,d\pi(z)=\nabla f(x)$ and $\int\|g(x,z)-\nabla f(x)\|^\alpha\,d\pi(z)\le\sigma^\alpha$; these quantities and the mixing distances are integrable. Averaged mixing obeys $\int\|\kappa^t(z,\cdot)-\pi\|_{\rm TV}\,d\pi(z)\le e^{-(t/\tau)^\gamma}$. Here $L,R,\sigma,\varepsilon>0$, $1<\alpha\le2$, $\tau\ge1$, $0<\gamma\le1$, $4\varepsilon\le LR^2$, $32\varepsilon\le\sigma R$, and $LR^2/\varepsilon\le(\sigma R/\varepsilon)^{\alpha/(\alpha-1)}$. Target: measurable output with integrable gap $\int[f(\widehat x)-f(x^\star)]\,dP\le\varepsilon$, where $P$ is the execution law. The setup permits arbitrary randomized adaptive algorithms. The necessary and sufficient orders match for fixed $\alpha,\gamma$ in this noise-dominated regime; constants are not uniform as $\alpha\downarrow1$ or $\gamma\downarrow0$.}\par\smallskip
