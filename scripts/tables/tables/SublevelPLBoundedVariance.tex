\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, $f$ is differentiable, globally $L$-smooth and bounded below. With $f_\star=\inf_x f(x)$, assume $f(x_0)-f_\star\le\Delta$ and $f(x)-f_\star\le\tau\|\nabla f(x)\|^\alpha$ whenever $f(x)\le f(x_0)$, where $L,\tau>0$, $1\le\alpha<2$, $\sigma,\Delta\ge0$ and $\varepsilon>0$. The jointly measurable oracle uses IID samples from a probability measure $p$, with $\int g(x,z)\,dp(z)=\nabla f(x)$ and $\int\|g(x,z)-\nabla f(x)\|^2\,dp(z)\le\sigma^2$; these integrals are integrable. The target is the unconditional last-iterate guarantee $\int [f(x_K(\omega))-f_\star]\,dP(\omega)\le\varepsilon$.}\par\smallskip
