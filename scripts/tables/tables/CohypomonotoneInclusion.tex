\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On a real inner product space $E$, $F:E\to E$ is $L$-Lipschitz, $L>0$, and $H:E\rightrightarrows E$ is monotone with exact, everywhere-defined resolvents $J_\gamma=(I+\gamma H)^{-1}$ for every $\gamma>0$. For $g\in(F+H)(x)$ and $h\in(F+H)(y)$, $\langle g-h,x-y\rangle\ge-\rho\|g-h\|^2$, where $0\le\rho L<1$. A zero $x_\star$ satisfies $\|x_0-x_\star\|\le R$, $R\ge0$. Target: output $u$ with some $\xi\in H(u)$ satisfying $\|F(u)+\xi\|\le\varepsilon$, $\varepsilon>0$. Each evaluation of $F$ or $J_\gamma$ counts as one call.}\par\smallskip
