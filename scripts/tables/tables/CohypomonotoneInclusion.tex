\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize In a real inner-product space, $F$ is $L$-Lipschitz and $H$ is monotone with exact, everywhere-defined resolvents $J_{\gamma H}$ for every $\gamma>0$. For $g\in(F+H)(x)$ and $h\in(F+H)(y)$, $\langle g-h,x-y\rangle\ge-\rho\|g-h\|^2$. Assume $L>0$, $\rho\ge0$, $\rho L<1$, and $0\in(F+H)(x_\star)$ with $\|x_0-x_\star\|\le R$. Target: an output $u$ with some $\xi\in H(u)$ satisfying $\|F(u)+\xi\|\le\varepsilon$, where $R\ge0$ and $\varepsilon>0$. Each evaluation of $F$ or $J_{\gamma H}$ counts as one call.}\par\smallskip
