\caption{Sampled composite smooth convex optimization: \href{https://github.com/k3nfalt/longtails/blob/main/LongTails/Setups/SampledCompositeSmoothConvex.lean}{SampledCompositeSmoothConvex}.}\label{tab:SampledCompositeSmoothConvex}
\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize Minimize $\Phi=f+\psi$ on closed convex $Q$ in a separable real Hilbert space, where $f=\int F_\xi\,dp$. Every $F_\xi$ is convex and globally $L$-smooth; $\psi$ is real-valued, lower semicontinuous, and convex on $Q$, with an exact constrained prox. Samples are IID with law $p$ under $P$; sample gradients are jointly measurable and losses integrable. A minimizer $x^*\in Q$ satisfies $\|x_0-x^*\|\le R$, $\Phi(x_0)-\Phi(x^*)\le\Delta$, and $\int\|\nabla F_\xi(x^*)-\nabla f(x^*)\|^2\,dp\le\sigma^2$, with integrable gradients and squared centered norms there. Here $x_0\in Q$, $L,R,\varepsilon>0$, $\Delta,\sigma\ge0$. The setup restricts algorithms to linearly decaying proximal SGD and its last iterate: $\int[\Phi(x_T)-\Phi(x^*)]\,dP\le\varepsilon$.}\par\smallskip

& \textbf{(1)}: Requires finite $\psi$ on closed $Q$, excluding barriers. \\

& \textbf{(1)}: Variance only at $x^*$ instead of uniform variance; includes regularized random-design least squares with $\sigma_u=\infty$. Matches leading terms at equal noise on the overlap.
& \textbf{(1)}: Needs samplewise convexity and smoothness; adds $\Delta/\varepsilon$. No Bregman geometry; assumption classes are incomparable. \\

& \textbf{(1)}: Scope extends to nonzero $\psi$; plain proximal SGD attains the leading terms. No problem-class rate improvement on the constraint-only overlap.
& \textbf{(1)}: Extra $\Delta/\varepsilon$ dominates when $\Delta\gg LR^2+\sigma^2R^2/\varepsilon$, even with $\psi=0$ and a constrained minimizer. \\
