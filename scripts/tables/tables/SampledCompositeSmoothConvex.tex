\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize Minimize $\Phi=f+\psi$ on a closed convex set $Q$ in a second-countable real Hilbert space, with $f(x)=\int F_\xi(x)\,dp(\xi)$. Each integrable sample loss is convex and globally $L$-smooth; sample gradients are jointly measurable. The real-valued $\psi$ is lower semicontinuous and convex on $Q$; its constrained prox is exact. IID samples have law $p$. A minimizer $x_\star\in Q$ satisfies $\|x_0-x_\star\|\le R$, $\Phi(x_0)-\Phi(x_\star)\le\Delta$, and $\int\|\nabla F_\xi(x_\star)-\nabla f(x_\star)\|^2\,dp(\xi)\le\sigma^2$. Here $x_0\in Q$, $L,R,\varepsilon>0$, $\Delta,\sigma\ge0$. The setup restricts the algorithm to proximal SGD with linear decay and last-iterate output $x_T$; the target is $\int[\Phi(x_T(\omega))-\Phi(x_\star)]\,dP(\omega)\le\varepsilon$ for the IID trajectory law $P$.}\par\smallskip
