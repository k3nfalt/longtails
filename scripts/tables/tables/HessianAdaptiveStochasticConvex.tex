\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize Convex differentiable $f:\mathbb{R}^d\to\mathbb{R}$ has differentiable gradient and $\rho$-Lipschitz Hessian on a compact convex body $C$ of diameter at most $D$; $x_0,x^\star\in C$, $x^\star\in\arg\min_C f$, $D,\rho,\varepsilon>0$. Jointly measurable oracles use IID samples of law $\mu$ under $P$, with $\int g(x,\omega)\,d\mu=\nabla f(x)$, $\int\|g(x,\omega)-\nabla f(x)\|^2\,d\mu\le\sigma_g^2$, and $\int\|H(x,\omega)-\nabla^2 f(x)\|_{\mathrm{op}}^2\,d\mu\le\sigma_h^2$ on $C$. Hessian samples are symmetric PSD pointwise and may be biased; the stated moments are integrable. Target: measurable feasible output $w$ with integrable gap and $\int[f(w)-f(x^\star)]\,dP\le\varepsilon$.}\par\smallskip
