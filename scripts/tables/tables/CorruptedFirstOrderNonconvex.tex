\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize Let $f:\mathbb R^d\to\mathbb R$ have $L$-Lipschitz gradient and $f(x_0)-\inf f\le\Delta$, with $L,\varepsilon>0$ and $\Delta\ge0$. A clean reply at $x$ gives a value within $\varepsilon^2/(128L)$ of $f(x)$ and a fixed vector $g(x)$ satisfying $\|g(x)-\nabla f(x)\|\le\varepsilon/8$. On every reachable history, at most $k$ replies may be replaced adaptively by arbitrary pairs. The target is $\Pr(\|\nabla f(x_{\rm out})\|>\varepsilon)\le\delta$ for $0\le\delta\le1/4$.}\par\smallskip
