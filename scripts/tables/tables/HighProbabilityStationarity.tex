\caption{High-probability stationarity under bounded variance (\href{https://github.com/k3nfalt/longtails/blob/main/LongTails/Setups/HighProbabilityStationarity.lean}{setup}).}\label{tab:HighProbabilityStationarity}
\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize Let $f$ be differentiable and bounded below on a complete, second-countable real inner-product space with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra. Assume $\nabla f$ is $L$-Lipschitz and $f(x_0)-\inf f\le\Delta$. The jointly measurable stochastic-gradient oracle uses IID samples, with mean $\nabla f(x)$ and mean squared error at most $\sigma^2$ at each $x$. The target is one measurable point $\hat x$ satisfying $\Pr(\|\nabla f(\hat x)\|\le\varepsilon)\ge1-\delta$, where $L,\varepsilon>0$, $\Delta,\sigma\ge0$, and $0<\delta\le1/4$.}\par\smallskip
