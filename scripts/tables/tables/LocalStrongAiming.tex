\caption{Local acceleration under \href{https://github.com/k3nfalt/longtails/blob/main/LongTails/Setups/LocalStrongAiming.lean}{LocalStrongAiming}.}\label{tab:LocalStrongAiming}
\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize A globally differentiable function $f$ on a finite-dimensional real Hilbert space attains $f^*$. On $U_r$, its gradient is $L$-Lipschitz and, for every $x,y\in U_r$,
$f(y)\ge f(x)+\langle\nabla f(x),y-x\rangle-\sqrt{\mu L}\|y-x\|^2$.
For every nearest minimizer $z$ to $x\in U_r$,
$\langle\nabla f(x),x-z\rangle\ge f(x)-f^*+\frac{\mu}{2}\|x-z\|^2$.
Here $0<\mu\le L$, $r>0$, $x_0\in U_r$, and $f(x_0)-f^*\le\Delta\le\mu r^2/256$, with $\Delta\ge0$.
Exact gradients; target $f(x_K)-f^*\le\varepsilon$, $\varepsilon>0$.
The constructed fixed-parameter Nesterov method uses $L,\mu$; its horizon also uses $\Delta,\varepsilon$.}\par\smallskip
