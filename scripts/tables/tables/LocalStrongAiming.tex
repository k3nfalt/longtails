\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize A differentiable function $f$ on a finite-dimensional real Hilbert space attains $f^*=f(x^\star)$; exact gradients are available. On $U_r=\{x:d(x,M)<r\}$, $\nabla f$ is $L$-Lipschitz and $f(y)\ge f(x)+\langle\nabla f(x),y-x\rangle-\sqrt{\mu L}\|y-x\|^2$ for all $x,y\in U_r$. Strong aiming means $\langle\nabla f(x),x-z\rangle\ge f(x)-f^*+\frac{\mu}{2}\|x-z\|^2$ for every $x\in U_r$ and every nearest $z\in M$. Assume $0<\mu\le L$, $r>0$, $x_0\in U_r$, and $0\le f(x_0)-f^*\le\Delta\le\mu r^2/256$. Target: $f(x_K)-f(x^\star)\le\varepsilon$, $\varepsilon>0$. Result \textbf{(1)} constructs fixed-parameter Nesterov with deterministic last-iterate accuracy; updates use $L,\mu$, and the horizon uses $\Delta,\varepsilon$.}\par\smallskip
