\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize In a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, $F$ is $L$-Lipschitz ($L>0$), $G$ is maximal monotone, and $F+G$ is $\rho$-cohypomonotone with $0\le\rho L<1$. Some zero $x^\star$ of $F+G$ satisfies $\|x_0-x^\star\|\le R$. Each call gives an exact resolvent of $G$ or a jointly measurable, unbiased sample of $F$ with variance at most $\sigma^2$ using IID randomness. The target is a measurable pair $(x,u)$ with $u\in G(x)$ for every seed and $\mathbb E\|F(x)+u\|\le\varepsilon$, $\varepsilon>0$.}\par\smallskip
