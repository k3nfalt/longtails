Samples are continuous with locally integrable weak gradients $G$ satisfying $\mathbb E\|G(x,\omega)\|^2\le M_2^2$ pointwise in the interior; $F,G$ are jointly measurable, $F(x,\cdot)$ integrable. Target: feasible measurable $\hat x$ with $\Pr\{f(\hat x)-f(x_\star)>\varepsilon\}\le\rho$.
