The jointly measurable sample functions are almost surely continuous with locally integrable weak gradients $G$, and $\mathbb{E}\|G(x,\omega)\|^2\le M_2^2$ pointwise in the interior; $|b(x)|\le\Delta$. The smooth case additionally has an $L$-Lipschitz gradient there. Target: $\mathbb{P}\{f(\widehat x)-f(x_\star)>\varepsilon\}\le\beta$, with $\widehat x\in Q$.
