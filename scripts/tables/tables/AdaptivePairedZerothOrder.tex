\caption{Paired zeroth-order convex optimization: \href{https://github.com/k3nfalt/longtails/blob/main/LongTails/Setups/AdaptivePairedZerothOrder.lean}{AdaptivePairedZerothOrder}.}\label{tab:AdaptivePairedZerothOrder}
\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On $\mathbb{R}^d$, $d\ge1$, let $Q$ be compact and convex, with diameter at most $D>0$, $x_0\in Q$, and an exact Euclidean projection. The population objective $f=\mathbb{E}F(\cdot,\omega)$ is convex and $M$-Lipschitz on $Q+\overline B(0,\gamma_{\max})$, where $\gamma_{\max}>0$; the smooth branch additionally assumes an $L$-Lipschitz gradient in its interior. Samples are almost surely continuous with locally integrable weak gradients $G$, satisfying $\mathbb{E}\|G(x,\omega)\|^2\le M_2^2$ pointwise in the interior. The oracle is jointly measurable and integrable, with IID samples across pairs and the same sample within each pair; it returns $F(x,\omega)+b(x)$, $|b(x)|\le\Delta$. For $\varepsilon>0$, the constructed output $\widehat x\in Q$ satisfies $\Pr\{f(\widehat x)-f(x_\star)>\varepsilon\}\le\delta$, where $x_\star\in\arg\min_Q f$. Take the smallest admissible budgets and require $\Delta\le\varepsilon\gamma/(16384\sqrt d\,D)$.}\par\smallskip
