\caption{Generalized-smooth stochastic nonconvex--strongly-concave minimax optimization with an LMO on $x$: \href{https://github.com/k3nfalt/longtails/blob/main/LongTails/Setups/MinimaxLmo.lean}{MinimaxLmo}.}\label{tab:MinimaxLmo}
\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize $f:\mathbb{R}^{d_1}\times\mathbb{R}^{d_2}\to\mathbb{R}$ has partial gradients $\nabla_x f,\nabla_y f$ and is $\mu$-strongly concave in $y$. $N$ is a norm on $\mathbb{R}^{d_1}$ with dual norm $\|\cdot\|_\star$, $N(v)\le1\Rightarrow\|v\|_2\le R$, and $\|u\|_\star\le\zeta\|u\|_2$; the LMO returns minimizers of linear functions over the $N$-ball of radius $\rho>0$. Whenever $L_1D\le1$ with $D=N(x-x')+\|y-y'\|_2$: $\|\nabla_x f(x,y)-\nabla_x f(x',y')\|_\star\le(L_0+L_1\|\nabla_x f(x,y)\|_\star)D$ and $\|\nabla_y f(x,y)-\nabla_y f(x',y')\|_2\le(L_0+L_1\|\nabla_y f(x,y)\|_2)D$. With $\Phi(x)=\max_y f(x,y)$: $\Phi(x_1)-\inf\Phi\le\Delta$, and every maximizer $y^\star(x_1)$ satisfies $\|y_1-y^\star(x_1)\|_2\le W$. Each call at $(x,y)$ returns jointly measurable, unbiased estimates of $(\nabla_x f,\nabla_y f)$ from a fresh IID sample, with $\ell_2$ variances at most $\sigma_x^2,\sigma_y^2$. Here $0<\mu\le L_0$, $L_1,\sigma_x,\sigma_y,\Delta,W,\zeta\ge0$, $32L_1W\le1$, $\varepsilon>0$. The setup restricts the method to momentum-LMO steps on $x$ and clipped stochastic ascent on $y$ with arbitrary nonnegative schedules, outputting $x_J$ for a random index $J$. Target: $\mathbb{E}\|\nabla\Phi(x_J)\|_\star\le\varepsilon$.}\par\smallskip
