\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize Minimize $F=f+\psi$ over convex $Q$ in a real Hilbert space. The function $f:E\to\mathbb{R}$ is convex with continuous gradient and satisfies $\|\nabla f(x)-\nabla f(y)\|\le (L_0+L_1\sup_{z\in[x,y]}\|\nabla f(z)\|)\|x-y\|$ for all $x,y\in E$; $\psi:E\to\mathbb{R}$ is convex on $Q$. Exact gradient and proximal queries are available, with the proximal minimum attained for every positive step. For the same minimizer $x_\star\in Q$, $x_0\in Q$, $\|x_0-x_\star\|\le R$, $F(x_0)-F(x_\star)\le\Delta$, and $\|\nabla f(x_\star)\|\le G$, where $L_0,R,\Delta,\varepsilon>0$ and $L_1,G\ge0$. Target: $x\in Q$ and $F(x)-F(x_\star)\le\varepsilon$, deterministically.}\par\smallskip
