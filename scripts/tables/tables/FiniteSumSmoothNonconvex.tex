\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On a real Hilbert space $E$ equipped with a measurable structure, let $F=n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^n f_i$, with differentiable $f_i$ and $n\ge1$. Assume $n^{-1}\sum_i\|\nabla f_i(x)-\nabla f_i(y)\|^2\le L^2\|x-y\|^2$ for all $x,y$, and $F\ge\ell$ with $F(x_0)-\ell\le\Delta$, where $L>0$, $\Delta\ge0$. The oracle returns exact component gradients. For $\varepsilon>0$, the target is a measurable output $X$ with integrable squared gradient and $\int\|\nabla F(X(\omega))\|^2\,dP(\omega)\le\varepsilon^2$, where $P$ is the algorithm's randomization law. Convexity and attainment of the infimum are not assumed.}\par\smallskip
