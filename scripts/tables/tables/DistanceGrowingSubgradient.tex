\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize Let \(X\subseteq\mathbb R^d\) be closed and convex, \(f\) measurable and convex on \(X\), and \(x_0,x^\star\in X\) with \(x^\star\) minimizing \(f\) on \(X\) and \(\|x_0-x^\star\|\le R\). Each call returns an exact projection onto \(X\) or a jointly measurable sample \(g(x,\xi)\) with fresh IID noise. On \(X\), \(\mathbb E g(x,\xi)\in\partial_X f(x)\) and \(\mathbb E\|g(x,\xi)\|^2\le G^2+B^2\|x-x_0\|^2\). Given \(x_0\), \(B,G\ge0\), \(R,\varepsilon>0\), and \(0<\delta<1\), return a feasible \(\hat x\) with \(\Pr\{f(\hat x)-f(x^\star)>\varepsilon\}\le\delta\).}\par\smallskip
