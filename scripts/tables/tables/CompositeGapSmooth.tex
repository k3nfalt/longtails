\caption{Composite gap-curvature acceleration: \href{https://github.com/k3nfalt/longtails/blob/main/LongTails/Setups/CompositeGapSmooth.lean}{CompositeGapSmooth}.}\label{tab:CompositeGapSmooth}
\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize In a real Hilbert space $E$, minimize $F=f+\psi$ over convex $Q$, with globally convex differentiable $f$ and real-valued $\psi$ convex on $Q$. Exact gradients and exact minimizers of $\psi(u)+\langle v,u-z\rangle+\|u-z\|^2/(2a)$ over $Q$ are available for every $a>0,z,v$. For $x_0,x_\star\in Q$, assume $x_\star\in\arg\min_Q F$, $\|x_0-x_\star\|\le R$, and $F(x_0)-F(x_\star)\le\Delta$, with $H_0,H_1\ge0$ and $R,\Delta,\varepsilon>0$. For every $x\in Q$ and ambient $y\in E$ with $\sqrt{H_1}\|y-x\|\le1$, assume $f(y)\le f(x)+\langle\nabla f(x),y-x\rangle+[H_0+H_1(F(x)-F(x_\star))]\|y-x\|^2$. Target: a deterministic $\widehat x\in Q$ with $F(\widehat x)-F(x_\star)\le\varepsilon$.}\par\smallskip
