\caption{Gradient sliding for \href{https://github.com/k3nfalt/longtails/blob/main/LongTails/Setups/SlidingGeneralizedSmooth.lean}{SlidingGeneralizedSmooth}.}\label{tab:SlidingGeneralizedSmooth}
\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize Minimize $F=f+h$ over convex $Q$ in a real Hilbert space. The convex differentiable $f$ has continuous gradient and satisfies $\|\nabla f(x)-\nabla f(y)\|\le (L_0+L_1\sup_{z\in[x,y]}\|\nabla f(z)\|)\|x-y\|$ on the whole space. Oracles supply exact gradients, exact projections onto $Q$, and possibly adversarial subgradients of $h$, valid on $Q$ and bounded by $M$. For $x_0,x^*\in Q$, $x^*$ minimizes $F$, $\|x_0-x^*\|\le R$, $F(x_0)-F(x^*)\le\Delta$, and $\|\nabla f(x^*)\|\le G$. Here $L_0,R,\Delta,\varepsilon>0$ and $L_1,M,G\ge0$. Target: a feasible output $x$ with $F(x)-F(x^*)\le\varepsilon$ almost surely.}\par\smallskip

& Total and gradients:\par

Counts $\nabla f$, $h'$ and $\Pi_Q$, plus $F$ values to select the best iterate.
& \textbf{(1)}: Gradients $\varepsilon^{-1/2}$ instead of $\varepsilon^{-2}$ for fixed $G+M>0$; no $F$ values.
