\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On a finite-dimensional real Hilbert space, $F$ is continuous and monotone, $F(x^\star)=0$, $\|x_0-x^\star\|\le R$, and $\|F(x)-F(y)\|\le(L_0+L_1\sup_{z\in[x,y]}\|F(z)\|)\|x-y\|$. The jointly measurable oracle satisfies $\int g(x,\omega)\,dp=F(x)$ and $\int\|g(x,\omega)-F(x)\|^2\,dp\le\sigma^2$, with integrable values and squared errors, and IID samples of law $p$ under $P$. Here $L_0,L_1,R,\sigma\ge0$, $\varepsilon>0$, $0<\delta<1$. Target: measurable output $X$ with $P\{\|F(X)\|>\varepsilon\}\le\delta$.}\par\smallskip
