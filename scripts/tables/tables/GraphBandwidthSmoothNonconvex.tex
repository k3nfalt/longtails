\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize Differentiable $L$-smooth $f:\mathbb R^d\to\mathbb R$, bounded below by $a$, with $f(x_0)-a\le\Delta$; $L,\varepsilon>0$, $\Delta,\sigma\ge0$. A jointly measurable, unbiased stochastic gradient has variance at most $\sigma^2$; samples are IID within workers and independent across workers. The connected graph has symmetric full-duplex FIFO links of bandwidth $b_e>0$ bits/s, zero latency, and per-bit time $1/b_e$. Worker $i$ takes $h_i>0$ seconds per sample, with one outstanding computation batch; different workers and directed links operate concurrently. Local operations are free. The setup restricts algorithms to quantized minibatch SGD with a uniform iterate selector. Target: $\int\|\nabla f(x_J(\omega))\|^2\,dP(\omega)\le\varepsilon$, where $P$ is the joint sample and algorithm law, for the actual execution at the deadline.}\par\smallskip
