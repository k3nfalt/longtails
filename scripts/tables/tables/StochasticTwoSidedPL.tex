\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize
Real inner-product spaces $E,F$, with $E$ finite-dimensional and $F$ complete and second-countable, carry their Borel structures. Partial gradients are $L$-Lipschitz in $\|x-x'\|+\|y-y'\|$; $f(\cdot,y)$ and $-f(x,\cdot)$ satisfy PL inequalities against every competitor, with $0<\mu_x,\mu_y\le L$. Every maximization in $y$ is attained. Jointly measurable partial-gradient oracles are unbiased, integrable, and have integrable squared errors of expectation at most $\sigma^2$; calls use independent samples with common probability law $p$. The setup restricts algorithms to stochastic Smoothed-AGDA. Given $\sigma,\Delta\ge0$ and $\varepsilon>0$, the target is $\int[\Phi(\hat x)-\Phi^*+\Phi(\hat x)-f(\hat x,\hat y)]\,dP\le\varepsilon$, with measurable output and integrable gap; $P$ includes the sample stream and output randomization.}\par\smallskip
