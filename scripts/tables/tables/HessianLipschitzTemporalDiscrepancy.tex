\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On a finite-dimensional real Hilbert space, $f\in C^2$ is $\mu$-strongly convex, $L$-smooth, and $\|\nabla^2 f(x)-\nabla^2 f(y)\|\le M\|x-y\|$, with $0<\mu\le L$. A minimizer $x_*$ exists and $f(x_0)-f(x_*)\le\Delta$. Call $k$ returns $\nabla f(x)+e_k$, with hidden, query- and seed-independent errors satisfying $\sup_n\|\sum_{k<n}e_k\|\le D$. Here $M,D,\Delta\ge0$, $\varepsilon>0$; all parameters are supplied. The target is $f(x_{\rm out})-f(x_*)\le\varepsilon$; \textbf{(1)} achieves it for every seed. Costs count all gradient replies, including batches and padding. The reviews identify no concrete application establishing this exact cancellation model.}\par\smallskip
