\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize
Let $F:\mathbb{R}^d\to\mathbb{R}^d$ be differentiable and $L_1$-Lipschitz with $L_2$-Lipschitz Jacobian $J$. Some zero $x_\star$ satisfies $\|x_0-x_\star\|\le R$ and $\langle F(x),x-x_\star\rangle\ge-(\rho/2)\|F(x)\|^2$ for every $x$. Assume $L_1,L_2,R,\varepsilon>0$, $0\le\rho L_1\le1$, $M\ge2^{15}$, and $d\ge M^3$. Randomized adaptive algorithms may query any point, with each call returning exact $F(x)$ or exact $J(x)$. The target is an integrable residual with $\mathbb{E}\|F(\widehat x)\|\le\varepsilon$ over the algorithm's random seed.}\par\smallskip
