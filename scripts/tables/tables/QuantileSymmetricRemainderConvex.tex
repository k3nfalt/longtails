\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize Convex differentiable $f:\mathbb R^d\to\mathbb R$ has $L$-Lipschitz gradient and a minimizer $x_\star$ with $\|x_0-x_\star\|\le R$. The jointly measurable oracle uses IID samples with law $p$ and stream law $P$. At every query, each coordinate-noise law is $\mu=S+A$, where $S,A$ are nonnegative measures, $S$ is reflection-symmetric, $\int |z|\,dA<\infty$, $\int z\,dA=0$, $\int z^2\,dA\le M$, and $\mu\{|z|>r\}\le1/64$. Here $d\ge1$, $L,R,r,\varepsilon>0$, $M\ge0$; no moments of $S$ are required. Target: measurable output with integrable gap and $\int[f(\mathrm{output}(\xi))-f(x_\star)]\,dP(\xi)\le\varepsilon$.}\par\smallskip
