\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize Minimize $F(w)=n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^n\log m_i(w)$ over a nonempty closed convex set $W$ in a separable real Hilbert space, where $m_i(w)=\int e^{s_i(w;\xi)}\,dp_i(\xi)$. Jointly measurable scores and subgradients satisfy $s_i(w;\xi)\in[c_0,c_1]$, $s_i(y;\xi)\ge s_i(w;\xi)+\langle g_i(w;\xi),y-w\rangle$, and, uniformly on $W$, $\int\|g_i\|^2dp_i\le G^2$, $\int e^{2s_i}dp_i\le\kappa m_i^2$, $\int e^{2s_i}\|g_i\|^2dp_i\le\sigma^2m_i^2$. Here $n,B\ge1$, $G>0$, $\kappa\ge1$, $\sigma,R\ge0$, $\varepsilon>0$, and $w_0,w_\star\in W$, with $w_\star$ minimizing $F$ and $\|w_0-w_\star\|\le R$. The oracle provides exact projection and IID uniform-anchor draws, each with two conditionally independent score--subgradient samples from $p_i$. Target: $\int[F(\bar w(\omega))-F(w_\star)]\,dP(\omega)\le\varepsilon$ under the IID execution law $P$; \textbf{(1)} constructs the SCENT average.}\par\smallskip
