\caption{Relative-gradient acceleration under \href{https://github.com/k3nfalt/longtails/blob/main/LongTails/Setups/RelativeGradientStrongQuasar.lean}{RelativeGradientStrongQuasar}.}\label{tab:RelativeGradientStrongQuasar}
\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On a finite-dimensional real Hilbert space with its Borel measurable structure, $f$ has an $L$-Lipschitz gradient and a minimizer $x_\star$ satisfying $f(x_0)-f(x_\star)\le\Delta$ and $f(x_\star)\ge f(x)+\gamma^{-1}\langle\nabla f(x),x_\star-x\rangle+\frac{\mu}{2}\|x-x_\star\|^2$ for all $x$. Here $L,\mu,\varepsilon>0$, $0<\gamma\le1$, $\Delta\ge0$, and $0\le\delta<1$. Each oracle call returns the same measurable $g(x)$, with $\|g(x)-\nabla f(x)\|\le\delta\|\nabla f(x)\|$. Target: $\int(f(x_K(\omega))-f(x_\star))\,dP(\omega)\le\varepsilon$, with integrable gap and $P$ the algorithm's randomization law. The setup does not restrict the algorithm; \textbf{(1)} constructs a randomized continuized method.}\par\smallskip
