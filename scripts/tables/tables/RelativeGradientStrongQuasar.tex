\caption{Relative-gradient optimization under strong quasar-convexity. \href{https://github.com/k3nfalt/longtails/blob/main/LongTails/Setups/RelativeGradientStrongQuasar.lean}{RelativeGradientStrongQuasar setup}.}\label{tab:RelativeGradientStrongQuasar}
\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On a finite-dimensional real Hilbert space with its Borel measurable structure, $f$ is differentiable with $L$-Lipschitz gradient and a minimizer $x_\star$ satisfying $f(x_0)-f(x_\star)\leq\Delta$ and, for every $x$, $f(x_\star)\geq f(x)+\gamma^{-1}\langle\nabla f(x),x_\star-x\rangle+\frac{\mu}{2}\|x-x_\star\|^2$. Here $L,\mu>0$, $0<\gamma\leq1$, $\Delta\geq0$, $\varepsilon>0$. The oracle returns a fixed measurable $g$ with $\|g(x)-\nabla f(x)\|\leq\delta\|\nabla f(x)\|$, $0\leq\delta<1$. Target: $\mathbb E[f(x_K)-f(x_\star)]\leq\varepsilon$ after a fixed number of calls; randomness is internal to the constructed method.}\par\smallskip

& \textbf{(1)}: Scope extends to nonconvex strongly quasar-convex objectives; matches polynomial rate dependence on the strongly convex overlap.
