\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On a finite-dimensional real Hilbert space, $f$ has an $L$-Lipschitz gradient and a minimizer $x_\star$ with $f(x_0)-f(x_\star)\le\Delta$ and, for every $x$, $f(x_\star)\ge f(x)+\gamma^{-1}\langle\nabla f(x),x_\star-x\rangle+\frac{\mu}{2}\|x-x_\star\|^2$. Here $L,\mu,\varepsilon>0$, $0<\gamma\le1$, $\Delta\ge0$, and $0\le\delta<1$. The oracle is a fixed measurable map $g$ satisfying $\|g(x)-\nabla f(x)\|\le\delta\|\nabla f(x)\|$. Target: $\mathbb{E}[f(x_K)-f(x_\star)]\le\varepsilon$, for the constructed randomized method's last iterate after a fixed number of calls.}\par\smallskip
