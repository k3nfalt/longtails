\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize $f:\mathbb R^d\to\mathbb R$, $d\geq1$, has an $L$-Lipschitz gradient and a $\rho$-Lipschitz Hessian, with $f(x_0)-m\leq\Delta$ for a global lower bound $m$. Here $L,\rho,\varepsilon>0$, $\Delta,\sigma_g,\sigma_h\geq0$, and $1<p\leq2$. Jointly measurable, unbiased gradient and linear Hessian-vector estimators use fresh IID samples and satisfy $\mathbb E\|g(x)-\nabla f(x)\|^p\leq\sigma_g^p$ and $\mathbb E\|(H(x)-\nabla^2f(x))v\|^p\leq\sigma_h^p\|v\|^p$ for every $x,v$. The target is $\Pr\{\|\nabla f(\widehat x)\|\leq\varepsilon,\ \nabla^2f(\widehat x)\succeq-\sqrt{\rho\varepsilon}I\}\geq2/3$, over the IID oracle samples and algorithm randomness.
