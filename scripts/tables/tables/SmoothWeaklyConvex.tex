Access is to exact gradients. The setup requires positive predetermined GD steps and the best-gradient queried iterate. The deterministic output satisfies $\|\nabla f(x_{\mathrm{out}})\|\leq\varepsilon$, hence the setup's expected squared-gradient target.}\par\smallskip
