\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize A finite-dimensional real inner-product space; a monotone, globally $L$-Lipschitz operator $F$, $L>0$, with $F(x^\star)=0$ and $\|x_0-x^\star\|\le R$. IID samples have law $p$ and joint law $P$. The jointly measurable oracle $g$ is integrable, $\int g(x,\omega)\,dp=F(x)$, and its integrable central moment satisfies $\int\|g(x,\omega)-F(x)\|^\alpha\,dp\le\sigma^\alpha$, $1<\alpha\le2$, $R,\sigma\ge0$. Target: $\int\|F(Z)\|^\alpha\,dP\le\varepsilon^\alpha$, $\varepsilon>0$, for the constructed method's last completed epoch $Z$. Only $x_0,L$ are public inputs; the stopping budget depends on $\alpha,\sigma,R,\varepsilon$.}\par\smallskip
