\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize Let $X$ be a nonempty closed convex subset of a complete, second-countable real inner-product space with its Borel measurable structure. The population law $P_0$ is arbitrary. Jointly measurable losses and subgradients satisfy $0\le\ell(x;\xi)\le B$, convex subgradient inequalities on $X$, and $\|g(x;\xi)\|\le G$. A minimizer $x^\star\in X$ satisfies $\|x_0-x^\star\|\le R$, with $x_0\in X$, $\rho,G,B,\varepsilon>0$ and $R\ge0$. Each IID oracle call returns projection onto $X$, loss, and subgradient. Define $L(x)=\sup\{\int w\ell(x;\xi)\,dP_0: w\ge0,\ w\in L^2(P_0),\ \int w\,dP_0=1,\ \frac12\int(w-1)^2\,dP_0\le\rho\}$. The target is a measurable feasible output $\hat x$ with $\int[L(\hat x(\omega))-L(x^\star)]\,dP(\omega)\le\varepsilon$ under the IID sampling law $P$.}\par\smallskip
