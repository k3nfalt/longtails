\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize Differentiable $f:\mathbb R^d\to\mathbb R$ satisfies $f(x_0)-f(x)\le\Delta$ for every $x$ and
$\|\nabla f(y)-\nabla f(x)\|_1\le(L_0+L_1\|\nabla f(x)\|_1)\|y-x\|_\infty$ whenever $L_1\|y-x\|_\infty\le1$.
Here $L_0,L_1,\Delta,\sigma_{0,i},\sigma_1\ge0$, $L_0+L_1>0$, $1<p\le2$, and $\varepsilon>0$.
IID samples have law $\mu$; the jointly measurable oracle is integrable, with
$\int g(x,\omega)\,d\mu=\nabla f(x)$ and integrable coordinate errors satisfying
$\int|g_i(x,\omega)-\partial_i f(x)|^p\,d\mu\le\sigma_{0,i}^p+\sigma_1^p|\partial_i f(x)|^p$.
The setup restricts algorithms to bias-corrected coordinatewise Adam with arithmetic minibatches and a uniformly selected pre-update iterate $x_I$.
The target is $\int\|\nabla f(x_I)\|_1\,dP\le\varepsilon$, with measurable output and integrable gradient norm; $P$ includes the IID samples and independent uniform selection.}\par\smallskip
