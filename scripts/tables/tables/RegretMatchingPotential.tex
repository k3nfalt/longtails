\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize Two players have $m,n\ge1$ actions and payoff matrices $A,C$. Unilateral payoff differences equal those of an exact potential $P\in[0,B]^{m\times n}$, with $B,\varepsilon>0$; payoff levels are unrestricted. One oracle call returns $(Ay,C^\top x)$. The setup requires zero-initialized simultaneous RM$^+$, with public inputs $m,n,\varepsilon$; $B$ determines the horizon, not the updates. Target: a mixed profile $(x,y)\in\Delta_m\times\Delta_n$ with $\max_i(Ay)_i-x^\top Ay\le\varepsilon$ and $\max_j(C^\top x)_j-x^\top Cy\le\varepsilon$.
