\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize On compact convex $X\subset E$, $Y\subset F$ in finite-dimensional real inner-product spaces, players minimize $h+g$ and $g-h$, respectively. The function $h$ is $\mu$-strongly convex in $x$ and $\nu$-strongly concave in $y$; $g$ is jointly convex. Within-domain gradients exist, $\nabla(h\pm g)$ are $L$-Lipschitz, and $\nabla g$ is $\delta$-Lipschitz. Assume $0<\mu,\nu\le L$, $0\le\delta\le L$, a feasible initial point, diameter at most $D>0$, and an equilibrium. Exact own-gradient and projection queries are available. Target: a feasible $z_{\rm out}$ with $\|z_{\rm out}-z_\star\|^2\le\varepsilon$ for an equilibrium $z_\star$, where $\varepsilon>0$.}\par\smallskip
