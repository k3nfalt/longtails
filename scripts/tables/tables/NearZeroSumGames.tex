On the domain, gradients of $h\pm g$ are $L$-Lipschitz and $\nabla g$ is $\delta$-Lipschitz, with $0<\mu,\nu\le L$, $0\le\delta\le L$, and $D,\varepsilon>0$. An equilibrium exists. Available oracles return players' own-gradient field and exact projection. Target: feasible $z_{\rm out}$ with $\|z_{\rm out}-z_\star\|^2\le\varepsilon$ almost surely for an equilibrium $z_\star$.}\par\smallskip
