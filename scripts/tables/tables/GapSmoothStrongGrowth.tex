\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize Convex differentiable $f$ on a separable real Hilbert space with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra; a minimizer $x^\star$ satisfies $\|x_0-x^\star\|\le R$ and $f(x_0)-f(x^\star)\le\Delta$. For all $x,y$ with $(2\sqrt3+\sqrt6)\sqrt{H_1}\|y-x\|\le1$, $f(y)\le f(x)+\langle\nabla f(x),y-x\rangle+(H_0+H_1[f(x)-f(x^\star)])\|y-x\|^2$. The jointly measurable oracle is integrable, unbiased, and has integrable squared error satisfying $\int\|g(x,\omega)-\nabla f(x)\|^2\,dp(\omega)\le(\rho-1)\|\nabla f(x)\|^2$; samples are IID with marginal probability measure $p$ and joint law $P$. Parameters satisfy $H_0,H_1\ge0$, $R,\Delta>0$, $\rho\ge1$, $0<\varepsilon\le\Delta$, $0<\delta<1$. Target: a measurable output $\widehat x$ with $P(f(\widehat x)-f(x^\star)>\varepsilon)\le\delta$.}\par\smallskip
